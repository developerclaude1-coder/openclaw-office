# astrocytes: Astrocytes and the tripartite synapse: metabolic/homeostatic support, glutamate clearance, gliotransmission, synaptogenic secretome (thrombospondins, hevin/SPARC, glypicans), astrocytic synapse phagocytosis (MEGF10/MERTK), calcium microdomains, and territorial tiling

## Summary
An astrocyte is not a support process. It is a spatially-scoped homeostatic controller that owns a territory and arbitrates every synapse inside it, using five signal classes on five different timescales: transmitter uptake (hundreds of microseconds), Ca2+ microdomain events (ms–s), metabolite export (s–min), secreted synaptogenic ligands (hours–days), phagocytic receptor engagement (hours–lifelong).

CLEARANCE. Glutamate in the cleft is terminated by Na+-coupled EAAT transporters that are overwhelmingly glial. Lehre & Danbolt's stereological/immunoblot quantification gives ~12,000 GLT-1 (EAAT2) and ~3,200 GLAST (EAAT1) molecules per µm³ of CA1 stratum radiatum — ~8,500 and ~2,300 per µm² of astrocyte membrane — and the total glial transporter pool is 3–5× the glutamate content of one vesicle from every glutamatergic synapse. The buffer is provisioned against a simultaneous discharge of the entire population. Crucially, Diamond & Jahr showed the fast operation is *binding*, not transport: transporters shape cleft glutamate within the first few hundred microseconds, while the transport cycle (tens of ms) is far too slow to explain it. The system runs a fast adsorb-and-hold stage decoupled from a slow dispose stage. Failure is not graceful degradation: GLT-1-null mice carry elevated residual glutamate, lethal spontaneous seizures, and amplified injury — an unbounded queue, not a slow service.

GEOMETRY AS GAIN. How much astrocyte membrane wraps a synapse sets clearance rate, spillover distance, and therefore presynaptic mGluR activation. Oliet et al. showed that physiologically driven retraction of glial coverage in supraoptic nucleus raises extracellular glutamate and changes release probability. Coverage is a tunable isolation parameter between adjacent channels, not a fixed wall.

METABOLISM. Uptake doubles as a demand signal. Glutamate co-imports Na+; the [Na+]i rise activates Na+/K+-ATPase, whose ADP drives glycolysis and lactate output (Pellerin & Magistretti). The event that must be cleared pays for its own fuel. Suzuki et al. showed the dependency is consolidation-specific: blocking astrocytic glycogenolysis, MCT1/MCT4, or neuronal MCT2 abolishes long-term but not short-term memory and blocks induction of pCREB, Arc and p-cofilin. The durable write, not the transient one, has an energy precondition.

GLIOTRANSMISSION AS A THRESHOLD GATE. Ca2+-dependent astrocytic D-serine occupies NMDAR co-agonist sites. Clamping Ca2+ in a single astrocyte, or depleting its D-serine, blocks LTP at nearby synapses; exogenous D-serine or glycine rescues it (Henneberger et al.). One cell sets the plasticity threshold for many thousands of synapses: plasticity is permitted, not produced, by the astrocyte.

Ca2+ MICRODOMAIN CODE. Di Castro et al. resolved two event classes in adult hippocampus: ~4 µm, millisecond-scale "miniature" responses to spontaneous single-vesicle release, and ~12 µm regional elevations encoding synchronous multi-synapse firing — both GTP/IP3-dependent, and required for basal synaptic reliability. Per-synapse detection with a coincidence-gated escalation to regional scope.

CONSTRUCTION. The secretome is staged and self-opposing. TSP1/2, expressed only by immature astrocytes, build ultrastructurally normal but postsynaptically *silent* synapses — a permissive switch that times the developmental window. Glypican-4/6 then unsilence them by recruiting surface GluA1 without changing total AMPA receptor protein. Hevin induces synapses while SPARC specifically antagonizes hevin, so the hevin:SPARC *ratio*, not either level, sets synapse number (hevin-null: fewer; SPARC-null: more).

DESTRUCTION. MEGF10 and MERTK are the phagocytic receptors by which astrocytes engulf synapses; engulfment is strongly activity-dependent and continues lifelong. Double-KO mice fail retinogeniculate refinement and retain excess functional synapses. In adult CA1, astrocytic MEGF10 loss accumulates excess but functionally *impaired* synapses, with defective LTP and impaired memory — pruning buys capacity, not tidiness.

SCOPE. Protoplasmic astrocytes tile into essentially exclusive territories; GFAP labels only ~15% of the true cell volume, so the marker underestimates the domain sixfold. Within its domain the astrocyte actively explores: perisynaptic processes extend and retract on an actin-dependent, minutes timescale, are on average *more* motile than the spines they contact, and stabilize preferentially at larger, stronger spines.

## Key mechanisms (computational logic)
- **Two-timescale glutamate buffer (fast bind, slow cycle)**: Signal = free glutamate in the cleft. Tag = none; the transporter is a stoichiometric sink. Fast path: binding to GLT-1/GLAST removes free ligand within a few hundred microseconds. Slow path: the transport cycle (tens of ms) actually disposes of it. Capacity is provisioned at 3-5x the glutamate in one vesicle from EVERY glutamatergic synapse (~12,000 GLT-1 + ~3,200 GLAST molecules per um^3 of CA1 neuropil), i.e. sized for worst-case simultaneous discharge, not average load. Failure mode is not slow service but runaway: loss of the transporter yields persistent elevated ambient glutamate, seizures and excitotoxic death. Design lesson: separate a cheap reversible hold from an expensive irreversible disposal, and size the hold against peak, not mean.
- **Coverage geometry as tunable channel isolation**: Signal = fraction of synaptic surface ensheathed by astrocytic membrane. Changing that fraction (physiologically, e.g. during lactation) changes clearance rate, spillover radius, ambient glutamate, and thereby presynaptic mGluR-mediated feedback on release probability. Crosstalk between neighbouring synapses is therefore a continuously adjustable parameter set by a third party, not a fixed property of the two participants. Isolation is a dial, and retracting it deliberately couples previously independent channels.
- **Uptake-coupled metabolic supply with a consolidation-specific fuel gate**: The clearance event is also the billing event: each glutamate co-imports Na+, the Na+ rise activates Na+/K+-ATPase, ATP consumption raises ADP, ADP drives glycolysis and lactate export (astrocyte-neuron lactate shuttle). Self-metering: demand signal and payment signal are the same token, so no separate accounting channel is needed. The gate is timescale-selective: blocking glycogenolysis or MCT1/MCT4/MCT2 lactate transport abolishes LONG-term memory and the pCREB/Arc/p-cofilin induction that underwrites it, while leaving short-term memory intact. Durable writes have an energy precondition that transient ones do not.
- **D-serine as a permissive co-agonist gate on the plasticity threshold**: Signal = astrocytic [Ca2+]i. Output = exocytotic D-serine occupying the NMDAR co-agonist site. Logic = AND-gate: coincident pre/post activity is necessary but insufficient; the NMDAR only passes if the co-agonist site is occupied, and occupancy is controlled by a cell that is not either synaptic partner. Clamping Ca2+ in ONE astrocyte blocks LTP at thousands of nearby synapses; exogenous D-serine or glycine restores it. The astrocyte permits plasticity within its territory rather than instructing which synapse changes.
- **Two-scale Ca2+ microdomain event code**: Scale 1: ~4 um, millisecond-scale confined events triggered by spontaneous single-vesicle release - effectively per-synapse detection. Scale 2: ~12 um regional elevations encoding synchronous events from sparse action potentials - a coincidence-triggered escalation to a supra-synapse scope. Both are GTP/IP3-dependent. The astrocyte is not one integrator but a tree of semi-independent compartments with a local-to-regional promotion rule; buffering the Ca2+ signal degrades basal synaptic reliability, so the local events are load-bearing, not epiphenomenal.
- **Staged synaptogenic secretome with matched agonist/antagonist pairs**: Assembly is two-phase and gated by a developmental clock. Phase 1: thrombospondin-1/2, expressed only by IMMATURE astrocytes, builds ultrastructurally complete but postsynaptically SILENT synapses - a permissive time window, not an instruction. Phase 2: glypican-4/6 unsilence by recruiting GluA1 to the surface and clustering it, with no change in total AMPA receptor protein - promotion of an existing latent structure to an active one, by relocation rather than synthesis. Set-point control is by ratio: hevin induces synapses, SPARC specifically antagonizes hevin, so synapse number tracks hevin:SPARC, and knocking out either side moves the count in the expected direction.
- **MEGF10/MERTK activity-tagged phagocytic pruning, lifelong**: Signal = relative neuronal activity at a candidate synapse; the elimination cue is derived from the same usage stream that scores retention, so no separate scoring pipeline exists. Effector = MEGF10 and MERTK phagocytic receptors driving engulfment. Weakly-active synapses are eaten. This runs during developmental refinement (double-KO fails retinogeniculate refinement and retains excess functional synapses) AND continuously in the adult brain. The capacity argument is direct: adult astrocytic MEGF10 loss accumulates excess but functionally IMPAIRED synapses, degrades LTP and impairs memory - failing to delete degrades the surviving representation, it does not merely waste space.
- **Exclusive territorial tiling plus motile perisynaptic exploration**: Partitioning: adjacent protoplasmic astrocytes occupy essentially non-overlapping domains, so every synapse has exactly one owning controller and the union of domains covers the neuropil with minimal duplication. GFAP - the canonical marker - reveals only ~15% of true cell volume, so the observable proxy drastically understates the real scope. Within the owned territory, exploration is continuous and cheap: perisynaptic processes extend and retract on an actin-dependent minutes timescale, with motility on average exceeding that of the dendritic spines they contact, and contacts are preferentially STABILIZED at larger (stronger) spines. Search is undirected and constant; commitment is use-dependent.

## ETTZ relevance notes from researcher
DIRECT ANSWERS TO THE OWNER'S POSED QUESTIONS.

(1) Filopodia analog / what actively EXPLORES. The astrocyte answer is better than the neuronal one for ETTZ, because it is cheap, continuous, and third-party. Perisynaptic astrocytic processes (Haber 2006) extend and retract on an actin-driven minutes timescale with motility EXCEEDING that of the spines they contact, and stabilize preferentially at larger (stronger) spines; Bernardinelli-type activity dependence makes the search itself activity-modulated. The logic is: undirected constant probing + use-dependent commitment. ETTZ has no organ whose job is to probe adjacencies it does not already have a FLOW REF for. A "perisynaptic prober" would be an organ that samples cross-organ subject pairs that co-occur in envelopes but have no flow ref linking them, emits a candidate CHOICE-like envelope, and retains only pairs that subsequently accrue usage BALANCE. Note the crucial constraint: exploration is scoped to the owned territory, never global.

(2) Use-dependent pruning vs "compress and archive, never erase". Astrocytes genuinely destroy (MEGF10/MERTK engulfment, Chung 2013), so they are not a model for quarantine — BUT they supply the strongest available argument that ETTZ must confront: Lee 2021 shows that blocking adult astrocytic pruning does not merely leave clutter, it leaves EXCESS BUT FUNCTIONALLY IMPAIRED synapses, with broken LTP and impaired memory. Un-pruned material actively degrades the surviving representation. The reconcilable reading for ETTZ: the destructive step in biology is *disconnection from the active substrate*, and the archival law is satisfiable by moving the item out of the retrieval path (quarantine batch, closed validity window, archived procedure) rather than out of existence. The CUSTODIAN interlock ("does a verified snapshot hold this?") is the correct ETTZ substitute for the point of no return; what ETTZ lacks is the *upstream selector* — an activity-tagged scorer that nominates candidates. In biology the elimination cue is derived from the same usage stream that scores retention, so no second pipeline exists. ETTZ already has that stream (BANK usage balances from envelopes) and no consumer of it.

(3) Soma = organ core, and territory. Astrocytic tiling (Bushong 2002) is exactly ETTZ's fractal law: exclusive non-overlapping domains whose union covers the field, parent = union of children, one and only one owner per element. This validates the ORGANON scope model and adds a requirement to watch-contracts: when it PROPOSES a join, it should also propose a subject-space boundary, because two organs claiming overlapping subjects is the pathology tiling exists to prevent. Second warning from the same paper: GFAP reveals only ~15% of true astrocyte volume — the canonical marker massively understates the real domain. GRAPHIFY/PHAROS render what is instrumented; assume the rendered connectome understates real coupling by a similar factor, and treat contract-declared scope as a lower bound, not the truth.

(4) Hebbian strengthening beyond proto-Hebbian balances. The glypican mechanism (Allen 2012) is the cleanest software analogy in this whole topic: TSP builds a structurally complete but POSTSYNAPTICALLY SILENT synapse, and glypican later unsilences it by relocating existing GluA1 to the surface — promotion by relocation, no new synthesis. That is caching / precomputation / myelination-as-precomputation with a two-phase build: create the link cheaply and inactive, promote it to hot only when a separate signal arrives. ETTZ's BANK balances are the natural promotion signal; the missing piece is a "silent vs. active" state on links and a rule that moves them.

(5) Synaptic tagging. D-serine (Henneberger 2010) is a better match than either flow refs or claims-trials for the PERMISSIVE half of tagging: it is a spatially scoped, third-party-supplied gate that must be open before a coincident event can be written durably. Model it as a territory-level enabling predicate (an organ-scoped claim whose validity window is open) that must hold before a fold is allowed to promote anything in that scope. It permits, it does not instruct.

(6) Sleep replay = memory-nightly, and the 60s cold fold. Suzuki 2011 is directly on point: long-term consolidation, and not short-term retention, has a dedicated energy precondition (astrocytic glycogen → lactate via MCT4/MCT1/MCT2), and blocking it blocks the molecular durable-write markers themselves. Biology's answer to consolidation cost is a LOCAL PRE-STAGED FUEL DEPOT positioned next to the consumer, not a faster central computation. The ETTZ analogue is pre-staged incremental fold state held per-organ (working-memory cursors already exist for this) so the nightly fold merges deltas rather than recomputing 71k entries cold. Also note Pellerin: the demand signal and the payment signal are the same token — an envelope that must be folded should carry its own fold-cost accounting rather than requiring a separate metering channel.

(7) Neurogenesis = new organs joining. Tiling makes joining a boundary negotiation, not just a registry drop. A new astrocyte must claim exclusive territory; a new ETTZ host should have to claim subject scope by exclusion, with watch-contracts arbitrating.

(8) Active forgetting to GENERALISE — the suspected sharpest gap. This topic supplies two halves of the missing mechanism. The selector: MEGF10 engulfment is activity-tagged and lifelong, so the promotion/retirement signal is a derivative of ordinary use, requiring no new instrumentation — ETTZ can compute exactly this from envelope-derived BANK balances plus flow-ref recurrence. The promoter: the hevin/SPARC ratio (Kucukdereli 2011) shows biology regulates a population set-point with a matched agonist/antagonist PAIR from one source, so the controlled variable is a ratio, not a level. An ETTZ generaliser should therefore be a nightly process that (a) detects repeated episodic patterns via recurring flow-ref shapes, (b) proposes a candidate claim/procedure, and (c) is held at a set-point by an explicit opposing term — a promoter score and a suppressor score emitted by the same fold, with DESIDERATA and the IGNORANCE GRAPH as the natural sinks for patterns that recur but do not yet meet the promotion threshold.

(9) One structural gap this topic exposes that ETTZ's design questions did not list. Nothing in ETTZ occupies the astrocyte's actual role: a non-participant homeostatic controller that sits beside every exchange, meters it, fuels it, gates its durability, and prunes it. BEATS are tonic SELF-reports by organs; there is no organ-external clearance/backpressure controller for the envelope stream. Lehre & Danbolt plus Tanaka give the design rule: size the intake buffer against a simultaneous worst-case discharge of the whole population (3-5x one full round from every producer), and understand that clearance failure produces runaway, not slow service. And Diamond & Jahr's fast-bind/slow-cycle split independently validates ETTZ's existing QUARANTINE architecture: a cheap reversible hold decoupled from an expensive authorized disposal is the biologically correct shape.
