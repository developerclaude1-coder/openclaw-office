# forgetting-apoptosis: Forgetting as an active process, and programmed cell death: Rac1/dopamine-driven intrinsic decay, interference, neurogenesis- and microglia-induced forgetting, retrieval-induced suppression, transience-as-regularization, and apoptosis vs necrosis as orderly vs catastrophic disposal

## Summary
Biology does not let memories fade for lack of maintenance; it spends ATP to remove them. The central architectural finding is that decay is a driven, always-on background process that consolidation must actively oppose — Davis and Zhong argue intrinsic forgetting may be the brain's default state, with stabilization as the exception that wins locally.

The best-characterized erase pathway runs dopamine to Rac1 to cofilin to actin. In Drosophila, dopaminergic neurons MP1/MV1 show ongoing, synchronized tonic activity onto mushroom-body engram cells before and after learning — a chronic signal, not an event. The same transmitter is demultiplexed at the receptor: dDA1 carries acquisition, DAMB carries forgetting, so one wire runs write and erase through two decoders with different kinetics. Downstream, the small GTPase Rac1 toggles between GTP-bound (active) and GDP-bound states; active Rac1 drives cofilin-mediated actin depolymerization and spine remodeling. Dominant-negative Rac extends early memory from a few hours to more than a day; constitutively active Rac accelerates loss. Crucially this is orthogonal to the write path — Rac forgetting is independent of rutabaga adenylyl cyclase and does not affect acquisition. Endogenous Rac activates on two timescales: slow ramp for passive decay (hours), sharp transient for interference and reversal learning. The mechanism is conserved: hippocampal Rac1 inhibition extends mouse object memory past 72 h and blocks interference-induced forgetting.

The synaptic effector is AMPA-receptor removal. Synaptotagmin-3 sits at the postsynaptic endocytic zone, senses Ca2+, binds GluA2 and internalizes it; Syt3 knockouts learn normally, but LTP does not decay and the animals do not forget. Blocking GluA2/AMPAR removal with the GluA2-3Y interference peptide during the retention interval preserves object-location and reward memories — and, decisively for a systems reading, also blocks contextual fear generalization and prevents spontaneous recovery of extinguished fear. Preventing forgetting prevents generalization: the abstraction is manufactured by the deletion.

Two further channels operate on structure rather than on molecules. Adult dentate neurogenesis degrades established memories by remodeling the circuit that indexes them — raising neurogenesis after encoding induces forgetting in adult mice, lowering it during infancy mitigates infantile amnesia, and precocial species that generate granule cells prenatally show no infantile amnesia unless neurogenesis is artificially boosted. Microglia perform use-dependent pruning: complement tags engram synapses, microglial phagocytosis engulfs them, and expressing the complement inhibitor CD55 specifically in engram cells blocks remote forgetting — an activity-dependent, complement-gated destroy path operating over weeks. At the cognitive level, retrieval itself is an eraser: practicing some category members suppresses unpracticed competitors for 20+ minutes, and the suppression is selectively aimed at high-frequency (strong) competitors — inhibition targeted precisely at what would otherwise contend at retrieval.

Yet the erasure is often indexical, not physical. Ryan and Frankland argue all natural forgetting is a switch of engram cells from an accessible to an inaccessible state, frequently reversible by direct reactivation, with rate modulated by mismatch between expectation and environment. Richards and Frankland formalize the payoff: transience is regularization — it prevents overfitting to specific episodes and promotes generalization, so memory's objective is optimal decision-making, not faithful transmission.

Programmed cell death supplies the disposal discipline. Kerr's apoptosis proceeds in two stages: condensation and packaging into membrane-bound bodies with intact ultrastructure, then phagocytosis and lysosomal degradation inside the ingesting cell — no spillage. Caspase cascades (initiators to executioner caspase-3) simultaneously kill, recruit macrophages, and post an "eat-me" tag: caspase-driven scramblase activation and flippase inactivation externalize phosphatidylserine, read by TIM4/TAM receptors. Failed engulfment collapses to secondary necrosis, releasing DAMPs and provoking autoimmunity — the catastrophic mode is precisely the orderly mode without a completed handoff. Development uses overproduction-then-elimination: roughly half of neurons die competing for limiting target-derived trophic factor, sizing populations by pruning rather than by plan. Notably, the same machinery runs sub-lethally: NMDAR stimulation transiently activates dendritic caspase-3 to internalize AMPARs for LTD without killing the neuron, with XIAP/Bcl-xL setting the threshold that keeps demolition compartment-scoped.

## Key mechanisms (computational logic)
- **Intrinsic forgetting as a default-on erase daemon (dopamine → DAMB → Rac1 → cofilin → actin)**: Signal: tonic, ongoing dopaminergic firing onto engram cells — present before, during and after encoding, i.e. a chronic background drive rather than an event. Demultiplexing: the same transmitter reaches two receptors on the same cell, dDA1 (write/acquisition) and DAMB (erase/forgetting), so one channel carries opposing operations decoded separately. Effector: Rac1 GTP/GDP toggle → cofilin-driven actin depolymerization → spine shrinkage. Threshold: level of active Rac1 sets decay rate; dominant-negative Rac extends memory from hours to >24 h, constitutively active Rac accelerates loss. Timescale: two distinct regimes — slow ramp over hours for passive decay, sharp transient for interference/reversal. Architectural point: the erase path is ORTHOGONAL to the write path (independent of rutabaga adenylyl cyclase; acquisition unaffected), so retention = write strength minus a continuously running deletion pressure, not the absence of refresh. Conserved invertebrate→vertebrate (hippocampal Rac1 in mice).
- **Interference-driven acute erasure**: Trigger: arrival of new, conflicting information addressed to the same store (retroactive interference, reversal learning). Response: the same Rac1 effector fires as a fast transient rather than a slow ramp — a step function on a conflict event, not a clock. Consequence: newer information does not merely outcompete older at read time; it actively schedules destruction of the older trace. Blocking Rac abolishes interference-induced forgetting while leaving acquisition intact, proving interference forgetting is an executed operation, not a read-time collision.
- **Synaptic-strength decrement as receptor removal with a calcium threshold (Syt3 / GluA2 endocytosis)**: Tag: GluA2-containing AMPA receptors in the postsynaptic membrane are the physical carrier of 'strength'. Sensor: Synaptotagmin-3 at the postsynaptic endocytic zone reads Ca2+ and, above threshold, binds GluA2 and internalizes it. Result: LTP decay, LTD, and behavioural forgetting are all one primitive — receptor removal. Knockout isolates the primitive cleanly: normal learning, absent forgetting, non-decaying LTP. Engineering read: decay is implemented as an explicit scheduled removal call gated by a threshold on a second-messenger signal, not as a leak or TTL expiry.
- **Forgetting manufactures generalization (deletion as regularization)**: Empirical hinge: blocking GluA2/AMPAR removal during the retention interval preserves specific memories AND blocks contextual fear generalization and blocks spontaneous recovery of extinguished fear. So the coarsening of a specific episode into a general rule is CAUSED by removing episode-specific detail, not by a separate abstraction process running alongside. Formal framing: transience = regularization; it prevents overfitting to particular past events and reduces the influence of stale data on decisions, so the objective function of memory is decision quality under environmental drift, not fidelity of transmission. Implication: a store that never loses detail cannot generalize by this route.
- **Use-dependent phagocytic pruning with a tag-and-eat protocol (complement + microglia)**: Tag: complement deposited on synapses in an activity-dependent way — weakly used synapses get marked. Executor: microglia engulf tagged synaptic components. Veto: expressing CD55 (a complement inhibitor) specifically in engram cells blocks forgetting, proving the tag, not the microglia, is the decision point — the destroyer is dumb and the labelling is where policy lives. Timescale: weeks (remote memory). Failure mode observed on removal: microglia depletion prevents both forgetting and 'engram dissociation', so pruning also maintains ensemble coherence, not only capacity.
- **Neurogenesis-induced forgetting (adding storage degrades existing indices)**: Mechanism: newly generated dentate granule cells integrate into and rewire established hippocampal circuits, degrading the retrievability of memories those circuits indexed. Dose-response and directional control: raising neurogenesis after encoding induces forgetting in adults; lowering it during infancy prevents infantile amnesia; species that finish granule-cell production prenatally (guinea pig, degu) show no infantile amnesia until neurogenesis is artificially raised. Engineering read: growth is not free — inserting new nodes into a live index is itself a destructive rewrite of prior addressing, and forgetting rate is a function of insertion rate.
- **Retrieval-induced suppression targeted at strong competitors**: Trigger: the act of retrieving one item under a shared cue. Effect: lasting inhibition (20+ min, beyond output interference) of the non-retrieved items sharing that cue. Selectivity rule: impairment falls on HIGH-frequency/strong competitors and largely spares weak ones — the suppression is aimed exactly at whatever would have contended at the next read, i.e. inhibition proportional to interference potential rather than uniform decay. Reading is therefore a write operation on neighbours.
- **Forgetting as accessibility-state change, not deletion**: Claim: natural forgetting switches engram cells from an accessible state (reachable by natural cues) to an inaccessible state, via circuit remodelling; the trace frequently survives and is recoverable by direct (e.g. optogenetic) reactivation. Modulation: forgetting rate is tuned by mismatch between expectation and environment — the system forgets faster when the world contradicts its model. Consequence: the durable object is the trace; what is edited is the index/retrievability. Pathology arises when disease hijacks this native mechanism to push accessibility down.
- **Apoptosis: two-phase orderly disposal with mandatory phagocyte handoff**: Phase 1: nuclear/cytoplasmic condensation and fragmentation into membrane-bound bodies whose contents stay intact and contained. Phase 2: engulfment by a neighbour or macrophage, then lysosomal degradation INSIDE the ingesting cell. Signalling: caspase cascade (initiator caspases → executioner caspase-3) does three jobs at once — kills, emits 'find-me' recruitment signals, and posts an 'eat-me' tag by caspase-driven scramblase activation plus flippase inactivation, externalizing phosphatidylserine read by TIM4/TAM (MerTK) receptors. Property: immunologically silent by construction. Failure mode: if engulfment does not complete in time, the body proceeds to SECONDARY NECROSIS, rupturing and releasing DAMPs that drive inflammation and autoimmunity. Necrosis is not a different plan; it is this plan with an unacknowledged handoff. Scale: ~10^11 cells/day cleared in a human.
- **Sub-lethal, compartment-scoped use of the death machinery (caspase-3 in LTD)**: Same executioner, smaller blast radius: NMDAR stimulation transiently activates caspase-3 via mitochondria/caspase-9 in DENDRITES to internalize AMPARs and produce LTD, without killing the cell. Selectivity: caspase-3 knockout abolishes LTD but leaves LTP normal. Threshold/containment: inhibitors XIAP and Bcl-xL gate escalation, and a cleavage-resistant Akt1 mutant blocks the effect — i.e. the destructive routine is bounded by explicit inhibitor levels and a short activation window, so 'demolition' can be invoked as a local weakening primitive rather than a whole-object delete.
- **Overproduction-then-elimination as sizing by competition**: Policy: generate a surplus (roughly 50% excess neurons), then let survival be decided by competition for limiting target-derived neurotrophic factor; losers die. The population is matched to the target field's actual requirement by pruning against a real resource signal, not by predicting the right count in advance. Engineering read: capacity is calibrated post-hoc by empirical demand, with death as the calibration instrument.
