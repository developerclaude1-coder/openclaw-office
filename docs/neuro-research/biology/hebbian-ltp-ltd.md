# hebbian-ltp-ltd: Hebbian plasticity, LTP and LTD: NMDA coincidence detection, AMPA trafficking, early vs late LTP, STDP, metaplasticity and homeostatic scaling

## Summary
Hebbian plasticity is a layered weight-update system in which one physical signal — postsynaptic Ca2+ influx — is decoded by amplitude, duration, and timing into opposite outcomes, then stabilized by slower supervisory loops. All citations below were verified against PubMed (PMIDs and DOIs given).

SIGNAL AND GATE. The synaptic weight is, to first order, the count of AMPA-type glutamate receptors in the postsynaptic density. The write-enable line is the NMDA receptor: at resting potential its pore is blocked by extracellular Mg2+, and the block is voltage-dependent, so the channel conducts Ca2+ only when glutamate is bound (presynaptic spike) AND the postsynaptic membrane is depolarized (postsynaptic activity) — a molecular AND gate implementing 'fire together, wire together' (Nowak et al. 1984). Bliss and Lomo (1973) first showed the consequence: brief tetanic stimulation of the perforant path potentiates transmission for hours.

SIGN SELECTION. The same Ca2+ wire carries both signs. A large, brief Ca2+ transient activates CaMKII; a small, prolonged transient (e.g. 900 pulses at 1–3 Hz for ~15 min) favors phosphatases and produces NMDAR-dependent long-term depression, while 50 Hz at the same synapses gives potentiation — a frequency/level threshold separating LTD from LTP (Dudek & Bear 1992). CaMKII acts as a latch: Ca2+/calmodulin triggers Thr286 autophosphorylation, keeping the kinase active after Ca2+ falls; it translocates to the spine, binds the NMDA receptor, and phosphorylates AMPA receptor subunits — all locally, which is what makes LTP synapse-specific (Lisman et al. 2012). Expression is receptor trafficking: LTP inserts/traps AMPARs into the PSD by exocytosis and lateral diffusion; LTD removes them by clathrin-mediated endocytosis (Malinow & Malenka 2002).

TIMING. Spike-timing-dependent plasticity refines the coincidence rule into a causality detector: presynaptic-before-postsynaptic spiking within ~20 ms yields LTP, postsynaptic-before-presynaptic within ~20 ms yields LTD, with a sharp asymmetric window (Bi & Poo 1998). The postsynaptic 'acknowledgment' is the action potential back-propagating into the dendrite, which supplies the depolarization that unblocks NMDARs at just-active synapses (Markram et al. 1997).

PERSISTENCE TIERS. Early-LTP (<~3 h) needs only kinase activity and trafficking. Late-LTP requires gene transcription and protein synthesis in the soma. Input specificity of a cell-wide resource is solved by synaptic tagging and capture: induction sets a local, protein-synthesis-independent tag that decays in under ~3 h; plasticity-related proteins made after strong stimulation anywhere on the neuron are captured only by tagged synapses. A weak input that alone would yield transient early-LTP becomes persistent if a strong input elsewhere triggered the protein-synthesis pulse — a two-factor commit: local tag AND global consolidation product (Frey & Morris 1997).

STABILITY. Pure Hebbian rules are positive-feedback-unstable (strong synapses fire the cell more, so they strengthen further). Three counter-mechanisms: (1) BCM sliding threshold — the Ca2+/activity level separating LTD from LTP is not fixed but moves as a supralinear function of time-averaged postsynaptic activity, so a chronically active neuron finds LTP harder and LTD easier (Bienenstock, Cooper & Munro 1982). (2) Metaplasticity generalizes this: prior activity changes the rules of subsequent plasticity without itself changing weights — e.g. via altered NMDAR function (Abraham & Bear 1996). (3) Homeostatic synaptic scaling: over 24–48 h a neuron senses deviation of its own firing rate from a set point via calcium-dependent sensors and multiplies ALL its excitatory weights by a common factor — multiplicative, so relative Hebbian-learned ratios are preserved while total gain is renormalized (Turrigiano et al. 1998; Turrigiano 2008). The architecture is thus: fast, synapse-local, correlation-driven writes; a slower tag-plus-consolidation gate deciding what becomes durable; and slowest, cell-global normalization keeping the whole weight vector inside its operating range.

## Key mechanisms (computational logic)
- **NMDA receptor coincidence detection (Mg2+ AND gate)**: Signal: glutamate binding (presynaptic fire) + membrane depolarization (postsynaptic fire). Gate: voltage-dependent Mg2+ block of the channel pore — conducts Ca2+ only when both conditions hold simultaneously. Output: Ca2+ influx localized to the active spine. This is a hardware AND gate that converts correlation of two event streams into a write-enable signal; timescale ~milliseconds-to-tens-of-milliseconds of coincidence.
- **Calcium amplitude/duration decoding of LTP vs LTD sign**: One scalar signal (spine Ca2+) decoded by level and duration: large brief transient → CaMKII kinase pathway → weight increase; small sustained transient (e.g. 900 pulses at 1-3 Hz, ~15 min) → phosphatase pathway → weight decrease; intermediate → no change. A single channel carries bidirectional updates; the threshold between signs is a tunable parameter (see BCM).
- **CaMKII autophosphorylation latch (early-LTP write head)**: Ca2+/calmodulin activates CaMKII; autophosphorylation at Thr286 makes activity persist after the trigger signal ends — a set-dominant latch converting a transient event into a lasting local state. It translocates to and docks at the NMDA receptor in the stimulated spine only, giving synapse-granular (not cell-granular) writes. Holds early-LTP for minutes-to-hours without new protein.
- **AMPA receptor trafficking (weight = receptor count)**: The stored weight is the number of AMPA receptors in the postsynaptic density. LTP = insertion/diffusional trapping of receptors (increment); LTD = clathrin-mediated endocytosis (decrement). Strengthening and weakening are the same currency moved in opposite directions — weakening is graded removal from the active site, not destruction of the synapse; the resource is recycled.
- **Spike-timing-dependent plasticity (asymmetric causal window)**: Pre-before-post within ~+20 ms → potentiate; post-before-pre within ~-20 ms → depress; outside the window → no update. The back-propagating action potential is the acknowledgment signal delivered to recently active synapses. The asymmetry makes the rule a causality detector: inputs that predict the neuron's output are strengthened, inputs that arrive after the decision are weakened.
- **Synaptic tagging and capture (two-factor durable commit; early vs late LTP)**: Tier 1 (early-LTP): local kinase/trafficking state, decays in ~1-3 h. Tier 2 (late-LTP): requires a cell-wide, threshold-gated protein-synthesis event. Specificity is solved by a local TAG set at induction (protein-synthesis-independent, lifetime <~3 h) that captures globally broadcast plasticity proteins. Commit rule: durable only if (local tag still fresh) AND (global consolidation pulse fired within the tag window). Weak events near-in-time to a strong event get consolidated for free — temporal association at the persistence layer.
- **Metaplasticity / BCM sliding threshold (rule modulation)**: The LTP/LTD crossover threshold θm is itself a state variable: it slides up as a supralinear function of time-averaged postsynaptic activity (integration window of minutes-to-hours-to-days). High recent activity → raising the bar for further strengthening and easing weakening; low activity → the reverse. Prior activity changes the plasticity RULES without changing current weights — a control loop on the learning rate/threshold, guaranteeing selectivity instead of saturation.
- **Homeostatic synaptic scaling (multiplicative renormalization)**: Sensor: the neuron's own average firing rate vs a set point, read through calcium-dependent sensors. Actuator: multiply ALL excitatory synaptic weights by one common factor (up if silenced, down if hyperactive). Timescale: 24-48 h — orders of magnitude slower than Hebbian writes. Multiplicative, so relative weight ratios (the learned information) are preserved while total gain is renormalized; prevents runaway strengthening and saturation of the dynamic range.

PMID:7054394; DOI:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.02-01-00032.1982. [verified] — supports: Defined metaplasticity: prior synaptic or cellular activity persistently changes the ability to induce subsequent LTP/LTD without necessarily changing baseline transmission — a higher-order form of plasticity, partly via altered NMDA receptor function.

- Turrigiano GG (2008). The self-tuning neuron: synaptic scaling of excitatory synapses. *Cell*. PMID:18984155; DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2008.10.008. [verified] — supports: Review of synaptic scaling mechanism: neurons detect deviations in their own firing rate through calcium-dependent sensors and adjust glutamate receptor accumulation globally, forming nested homeostatic loops over different temporal and spatial scales.

## ETTZ relevance notes from researcher
Direct mappings to the owner's design questions. (1) Hebbian strengthening beyond usage balances: biology's weight is a physical resource count (AMPARs) updated by correlation, not a log-derived statistic. The actionable analog is making BANK balances actuate something — precomputed caches, prompt ranking, replica placement (myelination-as-precomputation) — so that 'strength' changes future latency/selection, as receptor count changes future gain. CHOICE envelopes ('considered X, picked Y') already resemble STDP's causal credit assignment: strengthen what preceded and predicted a successful outcome envelope, weaken what was considered-but-bypassed. Note biology's ack — the back-propagating spike — maps cleanly onto the trigger fabric's confirming envelope: an update rule could strengthen exactly those items active in the window before a confirmation arrives. (2) Synaptic tagging: the tag is NOT the flow ref (which is an identity thread) — it is a short-TTL eligibility mark. Claims' trials (+confirm/-fail) are the closer analog of the weight itself; the missing piece is a decaying 'recently touched, eligible for consolidation' flag that memory-nightly consults. Frey-Morris's two-factor commit (local tag AND global protein-synthesis pulse) is precisely a design for the owner's sharpest gap — automatic promotion of repeated episodic patterns into claims/procedures: let events set cheap tags; let a threshold-crossing episode trigger a 'consolidation pulse' during memory-nightly; promote only tagged items whose tags are still fresh. This also consolidates weak events that co-occurred with strong ones — temporal association for free. (3) Sleep replay: memory-nightly is the protein-synthesis/consolidation tier; the 60s+ cold fold is the cost of late-LTP. Biology amortizes it by consolidating only above-threshold, tagged material — an argument for incremental folds gated by tags rather than full refolds of 71k envelopes. (4) Runaway strengthening: any Hebbian ranking added to the BANK will need Turrigiano-style multiplicative renormalization (periodically rescale all balances to a fixed total, preserving ratios) plus a BCM sliding threshold (a busy organ's bar for 'notable' rises with its recent event rate — directly applicable to beat-jitter alerting and to promotion thresholds). Metaplasticity says: store and adapt the RULES' thresholds as state, separate from the weights — ETTZ's why-ledger could carry threshold-change judgments auditable like any other. (5) Use-dependent pruning vs 'never erase': LTD is the encouraging precedent — biology's primary weakening operator does not destroy the synapse; it gradedly withdraws receptors (recyclable resource) while the structure persists. That is window-closing/quarantine, not microglial destruction: ETTZ can adopt bidirectional weight updates (demote, de-cache, close validity windows) without violating the no-delete law. Procedures' 'fail when tried, never age' is already LTD-like: performance-driven, not clock-driven, matching the finding that depression requires activity (900 low-frequency trials), not silence.
