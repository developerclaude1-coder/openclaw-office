# microglia-pruning: Microglial synaptic pruning: complement opsonin tagging (C1q/C3/CR3), use-dependent selection, don't-eat-me vetoes (CD47/SIRPα), CX3CR1 fractalkine signaling, surveillance motility, trogocytosis vs full engulfment, and contact-induced spine-head filopodia (microglia as connection-promoters)

## Summary
Microglia are the CNS's resident phagocyte and its only cell type running a continuous whole-territory scan. In vivo two-photon imaging showed that "resting" microglia are not resting: they tile the parenchyma in non-overlapping territories and continuously extend/retract processes, resampling their entire domain on a timescale of hours (Nimmerjahn 2005). At synapse resolution this is a polling loop — each synapse receives a brief direct contact of ~5 minutes roughly once per hour, and contact frequency falls when neuronal activity is pharmacologically reduced. The diagnostic variable is dwell time, not presence: after ischemia contacts lengthen to ~1 hour and the contacted presynaptic bouton frequently disappears afterward (Wake 2009).

Selection is implemented as opsonin tagging, deliberately decoupled from execution. The target writes its own mark first: presynaptic terminals locally externalize phosphatidylserine, and blocking accessible PS with Annexin V or deleting microglial TREM2 partially prevents elimination (Scott-Hewitt 2020). Neuronal C1q — induced by immature-astrocyte signals — binds and initiates the classical cascade, which cleaves C3 into iC3b/C3b that covalently attaches to the target membrane. That covalent bond is the durable, item-local tag. Microglia read it through complement receptor 3 (CR3 = CD11b/CD18, Mac-1), the only cell type expressing it in the P5 dLGN (Stevens 2007; Schafer 2012).

Use-dependence is the threshold on that tag. Injecting TTX into one eye and vehicle into the other at P4 and assaying at P5, microglia engulfed significantly more inputs from the silenced eye; forskolin reversed the preference, sparing the more active eye (Schafer 2012). Engulfment is window-scoped: high at P5, sharply reduced by P9 even though microglia retain phagocytic capacity. Engulfed material is overwhelmingly presynaptic — 40 nm vesicle-containing profiles in 35% of sampled microglia, postsynaptic elements very rarely — and engulfed terminals characteristically lack mitochondria. CR3 or C3 deletion cuts engulfment only ~50%, yet leaves adults with 1.3x RGC synapse density, 1.8x total VGlut2 puncta and permanent eye-specific segregation deficits: the pathway is redundant, but the window does not reopen.

Crucially, activity acts largely through a veto, not the tag. Neuronal CD47 signals to microglial SIRPα, an ITIM receptor that recruits SHP phosphatases and aborts the phagocytic cup. Both peak at peak pruning; in CD47-null mice engulfment rises, synapse number falls, and — the key result — microglia lose their preference for less-active inputs entirely (Lehrman 2018). Strength does not mark a synapse for keeping; it renews a don't-eat-me brake.

Tag families are context-specific. Whisker lesioning drives cortical elimination requiring neuron-derived CX3CL1, its sheddase ADAM10, and microglial CX3CR1 — but not CR3 (Gunner 2019); CX3CR1 loss alone leaves a transient excess of immature spines and delayed maturation (Paolicelli 2011). Experience modulates surveillance itself: light deprivation makes microglia less motile and biases them toward persistently shrinking large spines, reversed by re-exposure (Tremblay 2010).

The destruction framing is incomplete. Light-sheet imaging plus CLEM showed trogocytosis — sub-micron "nibbles" of presynaptic boutons and axons over minutes — with no observed engulfment of postsynaptic material and, notably, no deficit in CR3-KO slices (2.3 ± 0.7 vs 1.5 ± 0.6 events/cell/3 h, p = 0.37), so partial nibbling is a pathway separate from complement-tagged engulfment. In the same preparation microglia–spine contacts were brief (4.2 ± 0.85 min), non-random and repeated, and 39% of contacted spines extruded a spine-head filopodium toward the microglial process versus 7% of non-contacted spines; filopodia often relocated the spine head to their tip, occasionally onto a new bouton (Weinhard 2018). In vivo, microglial contact induces dendritic filopodia at P8–10, preceded by a Ca2+ transient and actin accumulation, and microglial ablation reduces subsequent spine density and L4→L2/3 connectivity (Miyamoto 2016). The same contact both nibbles and grows.

Finally, the machinery is dangerous outside its window: C1q rises and relocalizes to synapses before plaque deposition in AD models, and blocking C1q, C3 or CR3 rescues early synapse loss (Hong 2016; reviewed in Wilton 2019).

## Key mechanisms (computational logic)
- **Complement opsonin tagging: C1q -> C3 -> iC3b -> CR3**: A two-stage architecture that separates MARK from EXECUTE. Signal: neuronal C1q binds a synapse; the cascade cleaves C3 to iC3b/C3b, which forms a COVALENT bond to the target membrane. Tag: iC3b is item-local, durable, cell-extrinsic to the disposer, and carries no instruction — only 'flagged'. Executor: microglial integrin CR3 (CD11b/CD18, Mac-1) is the sole reader in the P5 dLGN, so read-permission is scoped to exactly one cell type. Timescale: tag deposition and readout are decoupled by hours-to-days. Redundancy: deleting C3 or CR3 removes only ~50% of engulfment, so the reader set is plural, not singular.
- **Phosphatidylserine externalization as the upstream, self-written eat-me signal**: The item declares its own weakness before any external judge acts. Signal: the presynaptic terminal locally flips PS to the outer leaflet at sub-terminal spatial scale. Threshold: PS accessibility — masking it with Annexin V, or deleting the microglial reader TREM2, partially blocks elimination. Ordering: C1q-null mice show ELEVATED presynaptic PS and REDUCED PS engulfment, proving PS is upstream of complement, not a consequence of it. Logic: a self-emitted decay marker feeds a downstream amplifier (complement), so the decision is not made by the deleter alone.
- **CD47/SIRPa inhibitory checkpoint — the don't-eat-me veto**: Deletion is default-permitted and blocked by an active, expiring veto. Signal: neuronal CD47 engages microglial SIRPa, an ITIM receptor that recruits SHP-1/2 phosphatases and aborts assembly of the phagocytic cup at the point of commitment. Threshold: veto strength is regulated by neuronal activity, and CD47/SIRPa expression peaks exactly at peak pruning. Critical result: in CD47-null mice microglia do not merely over-eat — they lose activity preference ENTIRELY. So use-dependence is carried by the BRAKE, not by the tag. Being used renews an exemption; being silent simply lets an exemption lapse.
- **Activity-dependent competitive selection with a scoped competence window**: Signal: relative firing rate between competing inputs onto shared postsynaptic territory. Manipulation: TTX (0.5 uM) into one eye at P4 vs vehicle in the other, read out at P5 (~24 h) — the silenced eye's inputs are preferentially engulfed; 10 mM forskolin inverts the preference. So the comparator is RELATIVE, not absolute. Window: engulfment is high at P5 and sharply reduced by P9, four days later, even though microglia retain full phagocytic capacity — pruning COMPETENCE is time-boxed independently of pruning CAPABILITY. Irreversibility: CR3/C3 nulls carry 1.3x synapse density and segregation deficits into adulthood; the window never reopens to correct the error.
- **Surveillance motility as a tonic polling loop with dwell-time escalation**: Microglia tile the parenchyma in non-overlapping territories and continuously extend/retract processes, resampling the whole domain in hours — a complete-coverage scan, not sampling on demand. Per-synapse polling rate: ~5-minute contacts about once per hour, and the RATE is activity-gated (reducing neuronal activity reduces contact frequency). Escalation variable: contact DURATION, not contact existence. Normal ~5 min; after ischemia ~1 h, and those prolonged contacts are frequently followed by disappearance of the bouton. Dwell time is the state variable that distinguishes routine polling from a commitment to act.
- **Trogocytosis: partial, sub-micron nibbling as a separate pathway from full engulfment**: A non-destructive edit operation. Scope: sub-micron (<1 um) bites of presynaptic boutons and axons, executed over minutes; the synapse survives the operation. Asymmetry: trogocytosis was restricted to presynaptic and axonal material with NO evidence of postsynaptic elimination; 10% of contacted spines vanished during imaging but none were in microglial contact when they did. Pathway independence: CR3 knockout did not reduce trogocytosis (2.3 +/- 0.7 KO vs 1.5 +/- 0.6 WT events/cell per 3 h, p = 0.37), so 'shrink in place' runs on different machinery from complement-tagged 'remove entirely'.
- **Contact-induced spine-head filopodia: the same agent that prunes also explores**: Microglial contact is a growth stimulus, not only a destruction stimulus. Statistics: 39% of microglia-contacted spines extruded a spine-head filopodium vs 7% of nearby non-contacted spines; filopodia extended TOWARD the microglial process (directed, not random). Contacts were brief (4.2 +/- 0.85 min) and the same spine was re-contacted, so contact selection is non-random. Consequence: the spine head frequently relocated to the filopodium tip, sometimes forming a new bouton contact — i.e. partner SWITCHING, not partner deletion; relocated filopodia outlived non-relocated ones (27 vs 12 min). In vivo confirmation: contact triggers a Ca2+ transient then actin accumulation then filopodium, restricted to P8-10; microglial ablation lowers spine density and L4->L2/3 connectivity.
- **Parallel, context-gated tag families (CX3CL1/ADAM10/CX3CR1 vs complement)**: One executor, several independent tag protocols selected by context. Signal: membrane-tethered neuronal CX3CL1 is cleaved by the sheddase ADAM10 into a soluble ligand read by microglial CX3CR1 (the only such receptor microglia express). Dissociation: whisker-lesion-driven cortical elimination requires CX3CL1, ADAM10 and CX3CR1 but NOT CR3 — while retinogeniculate refinement requires CR3/C3. Baseline role: CX3CR1 loss alone leaves a TRANSIENT excess of immature spines and delayed maturation, i.e. it retards the schedule rather than abolishing pruning. Design consequence: the pruning decision is a family of context-specific protocols sharing an effector, so blocking one route does not stop the system.

## ETTZ relevance notes from researcher
Bearing on the owner's explicit design questions:

1) "What actively EXPLORES for new connections (filopodia analog)?" — This is the sharpest transferable result. Weinhard 2018 and Miyamoto 2016 show the SAME agent that nibbles also grows: 39% of microglia-contacted spines extrude a filopodium directed at the microglial process (vs 7% uncontacted), often relocating the spine head onto a NEW partner bouton. ETTZ currently has no exploratory organ. watch-contracts proposes joins from DECLARED published contracts; nothing proposes edges from OBSERVED co-activity in envelopes. The analog is a surveyor that samples the envelope stream and emits speculative ref proposals (as CHOICE-style envelopes with a 'why'), where being touched by the surveyor is what triggers a candidate edge — exploration as a byproduct of the same scan that flags decay, not a separate subsystem.

2) "Is use-dependent pruning adoptable given 'compress and archive, never erase'?" — Biology says adopt the ARCHITECTURE, not the executor. Two findings license this. (a) Trogocytosis: sub-micron partial edits that shrink an item while preserving its identity and its synapse, running on machinery independent of CR3 (2.3 vs 1.5 events/cell/3h, p=0.37). That IS compress-and-archive as a first-class operation distinct from removal. (b) Lehrman 2018: use-dependence is carried by the CD47 don't-eat-me VETO, not by the eat-me tag — CD47-null microglia lose activity preference entirely. So the correct ETTZ mapping is not "prune the least-used" but "usage renews an expiry-veto": BANK usage balances become a CD47 analog that suppresses quarantine, making silence the lapse of an exemption rather than positive evidence for disposal. That inverts the polarity of ENTROPY's dwell clocks in exactly the direction the owner's law requires.

3) Synaptic tagging = flow refs or claims' trials? — Neither, quite. iC3b is a COVALENT, item-local, content-addressed mark deposited by the item's own context (neurons make C1q; the terminal itself flips phosphatidylserine first), read later by a different process on a different timescale, and carrying no instruction. ETTZ's closest fit is a tag written onto the referenced item that composes the WHY-LEDGER with flow refs: an append-only "flagged, because Z, at T, by S" annotation whose reader set is scoped to exactly one organ — as CR3 is the sole reader in the P5 dLGN.

4) Microglia destroy; ETTZ quarantines. — Two safety lessons argue ETTZ is right and should go further. First, competence is WINDOW-SCOPED: engulfment is high at P5 and collapses by P9 while phagocytic capability persists — competence expires independently of capability. Hong 2016 shows what an ambient, unwindowed pruner does: the same C1q/C3/CR3 machinery reactivating in adulthood produces pathological synapse loss. Any ETTZ pruning proposal must be time-boxed and expiring, never ambient. Second, biology has NO analog of ETTZ's guard test that no delete path exists — CR3/C3 nulls retain ~50% of engulfment, and the deficits are permanent (1.3x synapse density into adulthood, window never reopens). ETTZ's single-path-with-guard is strictly safer than biology's redundant-paths-no-guard. The CUSTODIAN interlock ("does a verified snapshot hold this?") is structurally the CD47/SIRPα ITIM checkpoint: an inhibitory signal evaluated at the moment of commitment that aborts the phagocytic cup. Worth noting biology places the veto at the ACT site, not the decision site.

5) Sleep replay = memory-nightly; BEATS. — Wake 2009 gives a concrete polling contract: ~5-minute contacts once per hour, frequency gated by activity, and escalation carried by contact DURATION (5 min normal, ~1 h before a bouton is lost). ETTZ beats analyse jitter/missed/drift; DWELL is the missing variable — how long an organ lingers on an item, not whether it touched it.

6) Suspected sharpest gap (episodic -> claims/procedures promotion). — Microglia offer only a partial answer: they promote via contact-induced filopodia and spine relocation onto better partners, i.e. re-wiring rather than abstraction. The generalisation-through-forgetting mechanism is not in this literature; that gap likely lives in hippocampal-cortical consolidation, not in microglia. What microglia DO supply is the promotion-side primitive: repeated non-random re-contact of the same item (spines were re-contacted, contacts non-random) as the signal that an item deserves a new edge.
