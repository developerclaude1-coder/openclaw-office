# synaptogenesis: Synaptogenesis: how a contact becomes a synapse — adhesion-mediated induction, assembly kinetics, stoichiometric thresholds, activity-dependent validation, and overproduction-then-selection

## Summary
All citations below were verified against PubMed (PMIDs/DOIs in the citation records).

**1. Contact generation is a cheap, high-turnover search.** Before any partner is known, dendrites extrude filopodia up to ~10 µm long with a mean lifetime of ~9.5 min, sampling a 5–10 µm neighbourhood. Most retract; a minority that physically contact an axon are stabilized and become spines with functional boutons (Ziv & Smith 1996). In vivo this is generative, not incidental: in zebrafish tectum nearly all synapses form on newly extended filopodia, and it is *retention of the nascent synapse* that stabilizes the filopodium, which then matures into a dendritic branch — arbor shape is the integral of which probes were validated (Niell et al. 2004). Search timescale ~10² s; the default outcome is retraction.

**2. Assembly is capture of prefabricated modules, not local synthesis.** Presynaptic active-zone material (Bassoon, VAMP, tubulovesicular membrane) travels the axon as discrete pre-packaged "transport packets" that are immobilized specifically at new sites of contact; evoked vesicle recycling appears there within <1 h (Ahmari et al. 2000). Quantitatively: a functional bouton within ~30 min of first contact, PSD-95 and AMPA/NMDA receptor clusters ~45 min later, whole junction functional in 1–2 h, presynaptic preceding postsynaptic (Friedman et al. 2000). So probe = minutes, commitment = hours.

**3. The induction signal is a bidirectional adhesion handshake carrying a type code.** The definitive assay is artificial-synapse coculture: a non-neuronal cell expressing postsynaptic neuroligin-1/2 induces morphologically and functionally complete presynaptic terminals in contacting axons, and soluble β-neurexin decoy blocks it (Scheiffele et al. 2000). Reciprocally, presynaptic neurexin alone induces postsynaptic differentiation, and isoform identity specifies the *class*: neuroligin-2 → GABAergic postsynapse, neuroligins-1/3/4 → glutamatergic (Graf et al. 2004). Many parallel CAM systems run simultaneously and redundantly (Südhof 2018).

**4. Induction has a stoichiometric (avidity) threshold, not a binary lock.** In the cerebellar triad, GluD2 is a membrane tetramer, Cbln1 a hexamer, neurexin monomeric, assembling 1:2:4. Dimeric GluD2 N-terminal domain plus Cbln1 fails to accumulate presynaptic Bassoon/VGLUT1; artificially *tetramerized* GluD2-NTD succeeds (Lee et al. 2012). The presynaptic terminal reads the number of clustered engaged neurexins — a quorum on partner count.

**5. Adhesion proposes; activity validates.** Loss-of-function inverts the gain-of-function intuition: neuroligin triple-knockout mice have *normal synapse density and ultrastructure* yet fail maturation, with reduced glutamatergic and GABA/glycinergic transmission and fatal neonatal respiratory failure (Varoqueaux et al. 2006). The gate is activity-specific: neuroligin-1's synapse-boosting effect is abolished by chronic NMDA-receptor or CaMKII blockade, while neuroligin-2's is abolished by general activity blockade — i.e. neuroligins do not establish but *specify and validate* synapses (Chubykin et al. 2007). The excitatory validation predicate is literally a coincidence detector (NMDAR Mg²⁺-block relief → Ca²⁺ → CaMKII autophosphorylation), timescale seconds-to-hours.

**6. Build surplus first, then select.** Synaptogenesis proceeds concurrently and at identical rates across macaque visual, somatosensory, motor and prefrontal cortex — a globally clocked, non-hierarchical build phase producing excess connections (Rakic et al. 1986). Selection is then competitive and *relative*: genetically silencing one of two axons converging on a muscle fibre makes it the loser; the input best able to excite the shared target wins (Buffelli et al. 2003). Retention is graded and probationary — thin spines turn over in days while thick ones persist for months, and the persistent fraction rises with age (35% → 73%) (Holtmaat et al. 2005).

**7. Removal is tag-plus-delegated-executor.** Losing inputs are opsonized by complement (C1q/C3) and physically engulfed by microglia via CR3; engulfment scales with *relative* activity — less-active inputs are eaten preferentially (Schafer et al. 2012). Judgment (activity comparison) and execution (phagocytosis) are separated across cell types, and execution is irreversible.

## Key mechanisms (computational logic)
- **Filopodial exploratory search (contact generation before partner is known)**: A cheap, high-frequency, spatially bounded random prober: extend ~10 µm, hold ~9.5 min, default action = retract. Probes are emitted speculatively into a neighbourhood with no prior target, so the connection space is sampled rather than computed. Cost per probe is near-zero; only the rare validated probe incurs real construction cost. Signal = physical contact; threshold = does anything answer within the ~10-minute window; timescale = 10^2 s.
- **Prefabricated module capture (transport packets) rather than in-place synthesis**: Components of the presynaptic active zone are pre-assembled elsewhere and continuously trafficked past all possible sites; a contact event immobilizes a passing packet. Construction = capture + local remodelling, so time-to-first-function is <1 h instead of a full biosynthetic cycle. Ordering is fixed: presynaptic bouton at ~30 min, postsynaptic scaffold (PSD-95) + receptors at ~+45 min, complete junction 1–2 h. The 'installer' is decoupled from the 'site selector'.
- **Bidirectional trans-synaptic adhesion handshake with an isoform type code**: Each side both emits and receives a differentiate-here signal: postsynaptic neuroligin induces presynaptic assembly, presynaptic neurexin induces postsynaptic assembly. Neither side can unilaterally declare a synapse. Isoform pairing carries the type field (NL2 → inhibitory program, NL1/3/4 → excitatory program), so the same contact primitive instantiates different connection classes by which adhesion variant is presented. A soluble decoy of one partner suppresses the whole handshake — the signal is competitive binding, not a broadcast.
- **Stoichiometric avidity threshold on the induction signal**: Induction is quorum-based on the number of clustered engaged partners, not on a single bond. GluD2 tetramer : Cbln1 hexamer : neurexin = 1:2:4; a dimeric receptor engaging fewer neurexins produces no presynaptic accumulation while the tetramer does. The receiving side integrates partner count over a contact patch and fires the assembly program only above the count threshold — noise-rejection built into the connection primitive, so incidental single contacts cannot trigger construction.
- **Activity-gated validation as a separate step from formation**: Adhesion creates and specifies the contact; use confirms it. Knocking out all neuroligins leaves synapse number and ultrastructure normal but blocks maturation and function — formation and validation are dissociable subsystems. The validation predicate is class-specific: excitatory contacts are gated by NMDA-receptor coincidence detection feeding CaMKII (blocking either abolishes the boost), inhibitory contacts by bulk activity. Unvalidated contacts persist structurally but never accrue capacity — biology's answer to 'a link exists' vs 'a link is trusted'.
- **Overproduction on a global clock, then selection**: Build wide before knowing what is needed: synapse production runs concurrently and at identical rates across functionally unrelated cortical areas, driven by one global signal rather than per-region demand. Precision comes from subsequent subtraction, not from precise addition. The system deliberately pays for surplus so that selection has a population to choose from — search breadth is front-loaded and the cost is amortized into the pruning phase.
- **Relative-efficacy competition on a shared target resource**: The retention decision is comparative, not absolute: among inputs converging on one target, the one that best drives the target wins and the weakened one is withdrawn. Silencing one competitor genetically makes it lose even when total activity is unchanged, so the ranking variable is relative drive, not aggregate use. This is a winner-take-most arbitration scoped to a single postsynaptic cell — a per-target leaderboard rather than a global usage counter.
- **Probationary survival window with two retention regimes**: New contacts enter a high-turnover pool (thin spines, lifetimes of days) and only a fraction cross into a long-lived pool (thick spines, months); the persistent fraction rises with system age (35% → 73%). Effectively a two-state retention policy: young links are clocked and cheap to drop, consolidated links are clocked far more slowly and expensive to drop. Age-of-link is itself a first-class variable in the eviction decision.
- **Tag-and-engulf removal with a delegated, non-neuronal executor**: Losing synapses are marked with an explicit molecular tag (C1q/C3 opsonin) and a separate agent — microglia bearing complement receptor 3 — reads the tag and physically removes the input. Tag deposition is graded by relative activity (less-active inputs are engulfed preferentially). Judgment and destruction are split across different cell types, the tag is an inspectable intermediate artifact between the two, and execution is irreversible.

## ETTZ relevance notes from researcher
Direct bearings on the owner's posed design questions:

(1) FILOPODIA ANALOG — the explicit open question. Biology's answer has four properties ETTZ currently lacks: probes are emitted *before a target is known*, they are *cheap and disposable* (default = retract), they are *spatially bounded* (5–10 µm, i.e. only near-neighbours in the existing topology), and they are *time-boxed* (~9.5 min, then die unless answered). ETTZ's watch-contracts is a deterministic diff of published contracts against mesh membership — it can only propose joins between things that already declare themselves. That is not exploration, it is reconciliation. A true filopodial layer would emit speculative CONTACT envelopes ("organ A tried subject S against organ B, unprompted") with a short TTL and no expectation of success, sourced from the IGNORANCE GRAPH and DESIDERATA (which already encode "what we don't have"), and let the vast majority expire silently. The Niell result adds the payoff argument: the arbor's shape is the accumulated record of which probes were validated — ETTZ's mesh topology would become an earned structure rather than a declared one.

(2) VERB DOORS AS THE ADHESION INTERFACE, AND THE CONFIRMING ENVELOPE AS RECIPROCAL INDUCTION. The neurexin/neuroligin result is that neither side can unilaterally declare a synapse: each side both emits and receives a differentiate-here signal. ETTZ's trigger fabric already has the shape (n8n decides WHEN, verb doors do WHAT, a confirming envelope closes the loop) — the missing half is that in biology the *confirmation itself induces construction on the confirming side*. A join proposal answered by an ack should cause the acking organ to build local state for that partner, not merely record the ack. Soma = organ core, adhesion machinery = the published contract at the verb door: the identity that determines *which* partners can bind lives at the interface, not in the core.

(3) STOICHIOMETRIC QUORUM ON NEW CONNECTIONS. The GluD2 1:2:4 tetramer result says induction fires on *count of engaged partners*, not on a single bond — noise rejection built into the connection primitive. ETTZ's usage BALANCES are scalar accumulators over envelopes; there is no valency threshold gating whether a proposed join is promoted. A rule of the form "a proposed link is instantiated only when N distinct flow refs have crossed it within a window" would import this directly and would make incidental single contacts unable to trigger construction.

(4) ADHESION PROPOSES, ACTIVITY VALIDATES — the closest structural analogue ETTZ already owns, applied to the wrong object. Neuroligin TKO gives normal synapse *density* with failed maturation: existence and trustworthiness are separate subsystems, and the validation predicate is an explicit coincidence test (NMDAR→CaMKII). ETTZ's claims/ trials (+confirm / -fail / ~anomalous, bi-temporal windows, close-never-delete) *is* an activity-gated validation predicate — but it is applied to beliefs, not to connections. Running newly proposed organ links, flow paths and BANK entries through the same trials machinery would give ETTZ synaptic validation for free, and the bi-temporal window is a better fit than deletion: an unvalidated link's window simply never opens. This is also the most promising handle on the suspected sharpest gap (no automatic promotion of repeated episodic patterns into claims/procedures): synaptogenesis says promotion should be driven by *repeated coincident traffic over a nascent link within a window*, which memory-nightly is already positioned to compute over the events/ fold — "contacts that recurred N times this window get promoted" is the fold-time implementation of Hebbian validation, and it is exactly the missing episodic→semantic/procedural ratchet.

(5) OVERPRODUCTION-THEN-SELECTION VS "COMPRESS AND ARCHIVE, NEVER ERASE". Rakic's concurrent-overproduction result argues for building surplus on a global clock and paying for precision in the selection phase — compatible with ETTZ, since surplus proposals are cheap envelopes. The pruning half needs decomposition: biology separates (a) the *comparison* (relative efficacy, Buffelli — a per-target leaderboard, not a global usage counter), (b) the *tag* (C1q/C3 opsonin — an inspectable intermediate artifact), and (c) the *executor* (microglia, irreversible). ETTZ can adopt (a) and (b) wholesale without violating the owner's law: a "not-selected" envelope carrying its why is precisely a complement tag, and QUARANTINE with .why.md manifests plus the CUSTODIAN snapshot interlock is a tag-and-hold executor rather than a destroying one. The important design note is that biology's ranking is scoped *per target* (which inputs converge on this cell), whereas ETTZ's usage balances are global per-item — the per-target scoping is the part worth importing.

(6) PROBATION WINDOW AS THE SECOND CLOCK REGIME. Holtmaat's two-population survival curve (thin/transient days vs thick/persistent months, persistent fraction rising 35%→73% with age) argues that ETTZ's rule set is currently one regime short. Procedures correctly have NO CLOCK and fail only when tried — that is the biology of a *consolidated* synapse. But nascent contacts do have a clock and die by disuse. ETTZ's ENTROPY 30-day dwell clock is already this probation timer for files; the missing dual is a probation clock on *newly formed links, new bank entries, and freshly joined organs* (ORGANON participant→organ→system maturation is the natural carrier), with graduation to the unclocked regime once validated. Neurogenesis analog: a new host joining by one registry drop is packet-capture-style instantiation, and should enter probation, not full membership, on arrival.
