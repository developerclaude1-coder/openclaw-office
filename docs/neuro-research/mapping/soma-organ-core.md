# soma-organ-core
MECHANISM: Soma/dendrite/axon division of labour: distributed nonlinear afferent pre-processing (dendritic branch subunits), one centralized factory/identity hub (soma), one movable low-threshold decision comparator (axon initial segment), cargo-typed bidirectional logistics with retrograde receipts (axonal transport), and compartment-scoped death distinct from identity death (pruning/Wallerian vs apoptosis).
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: Soma → organ core/harness (trusted supervisor S-4: identity, code/config manufacturing, registry entry; CUSTODIAN guards against 'soma death'). Axon → verb doors + outbox, with the trigger fabric's confirming envelopes as the retrograde acknowledgment lane and organ contracts/ORGANON conformance as the AIS polarity gate. AIS threshold → the n8n WHEN-node serializing conditions into discrete verb-door actions. Wallerian dead-man's switch → beats law ('silence is the one thing a heartbeat may never produce') + stale-fabric alarms + custodian A-002 interlock as the trophic check. Dendrites → inbox/Collector-pull/heartbeat listening — but this leg is transport-and-store only; the mesh's beat jitter analysis is the sole (weak) integrative element.
VERDICT: partial

## Argument for
Three matches hold at the level of computational commitment, not vocabulary. (1) Retrograde acknowledgment: axonal transport is a bidirectional cargo-typed queue where the inbound lane is quality control plus telemetry back to the nucleus (Maday et al. 2014, PMID:25374356); ETTZ's 'a confirming envelope closes each loop' is exactly this — efferent action is not complete until the receipt returns. Most software systems fire-and-forget; ETTZ and neurons both refuse to. (2) Silence-as-failure: axon survival is a lease renewed by continuous NMNAT2 delivery; cessation of delivery — not an error message — arms SARM1 and kills the segment (Coleman & Hoke 2020, PMID:32152523). ETTZ's beats law and stale-fabric alarms encode the identical inversion: absence of tonic signal IS the alarm condition. The custodian A-002 interlock ('does a verified snapshot hold this?' — no blocks the delete) plays the trophic check before scoped disposal. (3) Identity gate: the AIS is a single choke point that clusters the decision machinery, enforces what may leave as output, and whose loss makes the axon revert to dendritic character (Rasband 2010, PMID:20631711; Kole et al. 2008, PMID:18204443); an ETTZ organ's verb doors, defined by published contract and diffed by watch-contracts, are likewise the sole surface through which effects leave, and the contract IS the organ's identity in the mesh. (4) The death taxonomy transfers: biology separates compartment-scoped removal with parent survival (Luo & O'Leary 2005, PMID:16022592) from identity death; ETTZ separates capability removal (contract leave, entropy file disposal) from organ death (registry/identity loss), and centralizes everything unrecoverable at the 'soma' so only its loss is fatal — which is precisely why CUSTODIAN backs up the core.

## Argument against
The dendrite leg is anatomy-flavored naming. Real dendrites are the input layer AND a computer: thin branches are sigmoidal subunits firing regenerative NMDA spikes only on ~10-20 synapses co-activated within ~20-40 um and a few ms (Major, Larkum & Schiller 2013, PMID:23841837), making the cell a two-layer network (Poirazi et al. 2003, PMID:12670427), with branch-addressable gain plasticity (Losonczy et al. 2008, PMID:18368112) and a coincidence AND gate between top-down and bottom-up streams (Larkum 2013, PMID:23273272). ETTZ's afferent path does none of this: the Collector is pull-only transport, the inbox is a queue, and SPEC-SPINE-READ mandates that a parent is the LINEAR union of its children with no per-organ code — the exact opposite of nonlinear subunit integration. Nothing on the afferent path detects clustered co-occurrence, which is why the estate's sharpest admitted gap (no automatic episodic-to-semantic promotion) sits exactly where biology puts its clustered-input detector. Second, the AIS analogy is loose: biology gives each neuron its OWN threshold, physically colocated and homeostatically self-tuning — the whole segment relocates up to ~17 um over hours-days of altered activity to retune threshold (Grubb & Burrone 2010, PMID:20543823). n8n is a shared estate-wide fabric with static hand-configured conditions: the organ does not own its decision element and the threshold never adapts to beat jitter/drift statistics. Third, transport direction is inverted — biology pushes anterograde via kinesin motors; the Collector PULLS from outboxes — though the queue-plus-receipt logic survives the inversion.

## Strongest objection
The whole mapping is unfalsifiable decoration: ANY service architecture has inputs, a supervisor, a decision point, and outputs, so of course an ETTZ organ 'maps' — the analogy does no work a generic dataflow diagram would not, and 'partial' just launders a trivial structural resemblance into pseudo-rigor.

## Verdict survives because
The objection would kill a mapping that matched only at inputs/decision/outputs granularity, but three of the matches are non-generic design commitments most systems lack and ETTZ demonstrably has: mandatory receipt-closure of every efferent loop (retrograde endosome logic), absence-of-tonic-signal as the primary failure detector with a supply-side check gating disposal (NMNAT2/SARM1 logic, down to the interlock ordering), and contract-as-identity enforced at a single efferent choke point whose loss dissolves the organ's role (AIS polarity logic). These are falsifiable correspondences — an estate with fire-and-forget triggers, error-message-based alerting, or multi-surface efferents would fail them. Simultaneously, the verdict cannot rise to 'have it' because the dendrite half fails a concrete test the sweep makes precise: ETTZ specifies LINEAR union on the afferent path where biology's computational payload is nonlinear thresholded coincidence detection, and the decision threshold has no homeostatic plasticity. A mapping that is specifically right in three places and specifically wrong in two named places is 'partial', and both halves are evidenced, not vibes.

## Missing pieces
- Dendritic nonlinear integration: no thresholded coincidence/cluster detector anywhere on the afferent path — the Collector stores, BINNA's merged read is explicitly linear union; nothing fires on 'N related envelopes inside a spatiotemporal window' (the NMDA-spike logic, Major 2013 PMID:23841837), which is also the estate's admitted episodic-to-semantic promotion gap
- Homeostatic threshold plasticity: n8n trigger conditions are static; no analog of the AIS relocating over hours-days to retune threshold from chronic activity statistics (Grubb & Burrone 2010 PMID:20543823) — e.g. stale-fabric alarm thresholds tuned from beat jitter/drift history on a slow clock, decoupled from per-event logic
- Branch-addressable gain: bank balances and procedure verified-counts COUNT use but change no routing/coupling gain — no per-route strengthening analog of branch strength potentiation (Losonczy 2008 PMID:18368112) or task-to-branch allocation with enforced separation (Cichon & Gan 2015 PMID:25822789)
- BAC-firing coincidence class: no distinct high-priority 'burst' signal when the parent's union (top-down) view and a child organ's bottom-up event agree within a window (Larkum 2013 PMID:23273272) — agreement between scales is not a signal class in ETTZ
- Read-time arbitration: no divisive-normalization analog (Carandini & Heeger 2012 PMID:22108672, per critic) bounding which of several matching procedures/claims/bank entries wins a cue — sparsity and competition are unimplemented

## Adoptable proposal: FOLD-SPIKE: a dendritic coincidence detector in the nightly fold
- biological principle: NMDA-spike clustered-input detection: a terminal branch fires a regenerative event outlasting its inputs only when enough correlated inputs co-occur inside a spatial and temporal window, converting raw afferent traffic into a candidate pattern that then gates plasticity (Major, Larkum & Schiller 2013 PMID:23841837; Poirazi 2003 PMID:12670427). Window scale corrected per the critic: use behavioral-timescale, seconds-to-minutes, symmetric-around-trigger windows (Bittner 2017 PMID:28883072), not millisecond STDP windows. Output-rate bounding borrows divisive normalization (Carandini & Heeger 2012 PMID:22108672): candidates per fold are normalized by per-source activity so chatty organs cannot dominate.
- ettz change: One additional read-only pass inside memory-nightly's existing fold: group the night's new envelopes (incremental from the working-store cursor, not a cold 71k re-fold) by shared subject/refs/flow-ref; when N or more envelopes from >=2 distinct sources co-occur within a sliding behavioral-timescale window and recur across >=K days, emit ONE append-only QUESTION envelope into the ignorance graph ('these events cluster — is there a claim or procedure here?'), carrying the member envelope hashes as refs and a why. Tonic streams (beats, heartbeat-derived envelopes) are excluded from clustering, and candidates are capped per fold per source. It writes questions only — never claims, never procedures; trial-based confirmation and the owner remain the promotion gate. Nothing builds until the owner rules.
- cost: Build: a grouping+windowing pass over the nightly delta plus one new question-envelope template — small, no new schema, no new service, no change to any organ. Runtime: O(new envelopes per night) grouping; on current volume (~71k over 48 days, ~1.5k/night) negligible next to the existing 60s+ cold fold, and it rides the incremental path, not the cold one.
- risks: (1) Question-spam: a mis-set N/K floods the ignorance graph and buries real frontier items — mitigated by the per-source normalization cap, but the cap itself can starve a genuinely bursty new pattern; the owner should see candidate counts per fold on the memory face before trusting it. (2) Confounded clustering: envelopes co-occur because a cron schedule or the trigger fabric fired them together, not because they share a cause — without excluding fabric-originated and tonic sources the detector 'discovers' the estate's own scheduler every night and every 15s beat becomes a pattern. (3) Where the biology would be WRONG for ETTZ: an NMDA spike gates plasticity directly — biology tolerates false associations and lets later plasticity erode them. ETTZ claims are CHECKABLE PREDICATES with trials; wiring detector output straight into claims/ would pollute the semantic layer with correlation-as-causation and force window-closes to do cleanup. Hence the hard design line: detector output lands in the ignorance graph only, upstream of the claims gate, never in it.
- owning session: memory-nightly (the fold is where merged afferent traffic already flows; the ignorance graph and spine schema it writes to are memory-spine's, but the pass runs in the nightly consolidation), with kallimachos/entropy A-003 explicitly NOT involved — this is growth, not disposal
- priority argument: Ranks at or near the top of all proposals from this mapping exercise because it is the unique intersection of (a) this mapping's named missing piece — the afferent path computes nothing — and (b) the owner's own suspected sharpest gap: nothing PROMOTES repeated episodic patterns toward claims/procedures. It imports biology's actual promoter (a thresholded coincidence detector, not a curator), costs one pass in an existing scheduled job, touches no organ, adds no delete path, and its worst failure mode (noisy questions) degrades the frontier display, not the memory stores. The threshold-plasticity proposal (AIS-style alarm tuning) is worthwhile but ranks below it: it refines an existing working mechanism, whereas FOLD-SPIKE creates a capability class the estate has zero of.

## Doctrine notes
Two explicit touches on owner law. (1) Wallerian degeneration and apoptosis are where this mapping meets 'compress and archive, NEVER erase': biology's endpoint is local self-DESTRUCTION (SARM1 executes within days, no somatic involvement). ETTZ must adopt only the DETECTION half (silence of renewal as the failure signal — already law via beats) and the SCOPING half (retire the compartment, never the identity — matches capability-removal vs organ-death), while the EXECUTION half is deliberately different by doctrine: quarantine with .why.md manifests and owner-gated disposal replaces SARM1, and the custodian A-002 interlock is the trophic check biology runs implicitly. This divergence is doctrine, not failure, and the mapping should record it as such. (2) FOLD-SPIKE is doctrine-clean by construction — append-only question envelopes, no delete path, no automatic promotion into claims/ (the trials+owner gate is preserved), and it is proposed FOR OWNER REVIEW only; nothing builds until he rules. One caution: any future extension that lets detector output auto-open or auto-close claim windows would cross from research into the claims layer's guarded invariants and needs a separate owner ruling.
