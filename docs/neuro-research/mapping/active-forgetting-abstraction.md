# active-forgetting-abstraction
MECHANISM: Active forgetting as learning's regularizer: driven trace decay (dopamine→Rac1→cofilin; Syt3/GluA2 AMPAR removal), interference- and neurogenesis-induced forgetting, and transience-as-regularization — episodic detail is actively removed so that the extracted regularity (the gist) is what persists (Richards & Frankland 2017, PMID:28641107; Davis & Zhong 2017, PMID:28772119).
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: Decay half: ENTROPY dwell clocks (kallimachos A-003) + grace verification + QUARANTINE with .why.md manifests, and the planned CUSTODIAN A-002 snapshot interlock. Accessibility-switching half: claims' CLOSE-THE-WINDOW-NEVER-DELETE and procedures' fail-on-trial-not-on-clock. Weak abstraction half: memory-nightly's fold of 71k envelopes into claims/procedures state renders. Promotion half (episodes → new claims/procedures): NONE — 4 claims and 12 procedures are all hand-seeded/hand-verified; no mechanism mines recurring episodic patterns into candidate semantic or procedural knowledge, and entropy's disposal is keyed to dwell-time, not to whether the disposed detail was first abstracted.
VERDICT: partial

## Argument for
More of biology's forgetting logic is present than the raw hypothesis credits, once read at the level of computational logic rather than name. (1) Ryan & Frankland 2022 (PMID:35027710) argue natural forgetting is a switch of engram cells from accessible to inaccessible state — the trace survives, often recoverable by direct reactivation; the durable object is the trace, what is edited is the index. That is structurally identical to ETTZ's close-the-window-never-delete: a superseded claim with a closed window is an inaccessible-but-intact trace, recoverable by --asof time travel. ETTZ's owner law is therefore not anti-biological; it matches the indexical (dominant) reading of biological forgetting. (2) Interference-driven forgetting — new conflicting information actively schedules demotion of the old trace rather than merely outcompeting it at read time (Shuai 2010, PMID:20178749; Liu 2016, PMID:27593377) — is exactly what a contradicting trial does to a claim: the conflict event closes the validity window. A step function on a conflict event, not a clock. (3) Procedures' 'no clock, fail-on-trial' rule matches the modulation Ryan & Frankland describe: forgetting rate tuned by mismatch between expectation and environment, i.e. retention decided by outcome-on-use, not by elapsed time. (4) Entropy + quarantine + the CUSTODIAN interlock reproduce apoptosis's disposal discipline with precision: tagged candidate (.why.md = the phosphatidylserine eat-me tag), grace window, and — decisively — the rule from Kerr 1972 (PMID:4561027) / Nagata 2018 (PMID:29400998) that orderly disposal REQUIRES a completed, acknowledged handoff to the receiving party, secondary necrosis being the same plan with an unacknowledged handoff; A-002's 'does a verified snapshot hold this? no → block' is that acknowledgment. So the decay/disposal machinery genuinely carries biological logic.

## Argument against
The mapping breaks exactly where the owner suspected, and the break is load-bearing. Migues 2016 (PMID:27013677) is the hinge result: blocking GluA2/AMPAR removal during retention preserved specific memories AND abolished contextual fear generalization — generalization is not a process running alongside forgetting, it is the RESIDUE of removing episode-specific detail. Richards & Frankland 2017 formalize it: transience is regularization against overfitting to particular episodes. The sleep literature gives the same answer from the constructive side: repeated interleaved replay into a slow learner plus global proportional downscaling composes into a compression operator whose measured output is gist — recognition of NOVEL sequences obeying the learned structure, proportional to slow-wave sleep obtained (Durrant 2011, PMID:21335017; Klinzing 2019, PMID:31451802; McClelland 1995, PMID:7624455). ETTZ has the fast store (events/), the slow store (claims/ with trial counts — a predicate with trials IS a slowly accumulated weight), and the scheduler (memory-nightly), but no re-presentation loop and no promotion path between them. Meanwhile entropy's dwell clock diverges from every biological forgetting channel on the decision variable: biology demotes by USE and by MISMATCH (complement tags on weakly-used synapses, Wang 2020, PMID:32029629; prediction-error-gated destabilization, Sevenster 2013, PMID:23413355); ETTZ disposes by elapsed time and owner verdict, and disposes FILES, not retrieval rank. So the estate runs disposal risk and disposal labor (2 batches, manifests, interlocks) while collecting none of forgetting's dividend: no claim, no procedure, no coarsened index ever falls out of what entropy removes. The candidate mapping 'active forgetting → entropy dwell clocks' is therefore superficial on the axis that matters — entropy is apoptosis (disposal discipline), not forgetting (regularization). The gap diagnosis is CONFIRMED as sharpest: three independent literature clusters (deletion→generalization, replay→gist, and neurogenesis — Akers 2014, PMID:24812394, where growth-driven forgetting PRESUMES prior consolidation-out to cortex) all point at the same missing loop.

## Strongest objection
Against 'partial', the strongest objection is that the verdict should be 'deliberately different': the owner's absolute law — compress and archive, never erase — forbids the very operation (removal of episodic detail) that Migues shows manufactures generalization, so the absence of forgetting-driven abstraction is doctrine, not a gap, and calling it 'partial with a missing piece' smuggles in a criticism of owner law. A secondary objection runs the other way: since NOTHING promotes episodes to claims/procedures, the honest verdict for the mechanism-as-stated is 'missing'.

## Verdict survives because
The doctrine objection fails on the biology itself: what produces generalization is removal of detail FROM THE ACTIVE INDEX, and Ryan & Frankland 2022 show natural forgetting is predominantly an accessibility-state switch on a surviving trace — physically closer to close-the-window-never-delete than to erasure. de Vivo 2017 (PMID:28154076) sharpens this: sleep's downscaling is a multiplicative rank contraction with a sparing predicate, ordering preserved, nothing individually judged for deletion — implementable in ETTZ as salience-rank decay on an index while every envelope stays on disk with its why-ledger intact. So doctrine does not forbid the computation; it forbids one implementation of it that biology itself mostly doesn't use. The divergence that IS doctrinal — microglia and Rac1 physically destroy, ETTZ quarantines under owner authorization — is real but sits on the executor, and Wang 2020's CD55 result shows the executor is not where policy lives: engram-specific complement inhibition alone blocks forgetting, so the TAG is the decision point and the destroyer is dumb. ETTZ can adopt the entire tagging/decision half and keep quarantine as executor, losing nothing computational. The 'missing' objection fails because the verdict is on the whole mechanism cluster, and ETTZ demonstrably carries the accessibility-switch, conflict-triggered-demotion, fail-on-use, and disposal-handoff logics under its own names; only the promotion/abstraction loop and the use-based decay variable are absent. Partial — with the missing piece named — is the accurate reading, and it survives both objections.

## Missing pieces
- The promotion loop (the confirmed sharpest gap): nothing mines recurring episodic envelope patterns into CANDIDATE claims or procedures — the hippocampus→neocortex re-presentation path (McClelland 1995; Klinzing 2019) has no ETTZ counterpart; claims/ is the right target format (predicate + trials = slow weight) but is only ever written by hand.
- Coupling of decay to abstraction: entropy disposes and the fold compresses, but promotion never demotes — no step closes out or down-ranks the constituent episodes once their regularity has been captured, so decay's cost is paid without its dividend (Migues 2016: the deletion IS the generalizer).
- A use/salience-based decay variable: entropy's only clock is dwell-time; biology demotes by usage tags (complement on weakly-used synapses, Wang 2020) and by prediction-error/mismatch (Sevenster 2013; Ryan & Frankland 2022). Bank balances and envelope-derived usage exist but drive nothing subtractive.
- An interleaving discipline for any future promotion: a candidate claim must accumulate trials across independent sources and passes (BINNA's parent=union-of-children is the natural interleaving set), never single-pass promotion of one high-frequency motif — McClelland 1995's catastrophic-interference warning.
- Retrieval-as-write-on-neighbours: choosing bank tool Y over X only increments Y's balance; Anderson 1994 (PMID:7931095) says the choice should also demote X's rank for that cue, proportional to X's strength — the CHOICE envelope already records the needed triple.
- A salience scorer upstream of any of this: frequency is not importance; biology gates durable storage on prediction error/novelty (Schultz 1997, PMID:9054347; Takeuchi 2016, PMID:27602521), and ETTZ has no write-priority signal at all.

## Adoptable proposal: GIST-PROPOSER: a nightly episodic-pattern miner that drafts candidate claims/procedures as question envelopes, and on owner confirmation demotes (never deletes) the folded episodes' index rank
- biological principle: Forgetting-as-regularization via consolidation: repeated interleaved replay of episodic traces into a slow store extracts shared structure, and removal of episode-specific detail from the active index is what yields generalization (Migues 2016 PMID:27013677; Richards & Frankland 2017 PMID:28641107; McClelland 1995 PMID:7624455; Klinzing 2019 PMID:31451802) — with forgetting realized as an accessibility-state switch on a surviving trace, not destruction (Ryan & Frankland 2022 PMID:35027710; de Vivo 2017 PMID:28154076 for rank-preserving multiplicative demotion with a sparing predicate).
- ettz change: Add one pass to memory-nightly. (1) MINE: over a bounded incremental batch of episodic envelopes (cursor-based, O(selected) not O(71k)), group by flow ref / subject / why-field motif and detect recurrence — threshold: same motif >= N times across >= K INDEPENDENT sources (the interleaving discipline; heartbeat/cron tonic streams excluded). (2) DRAFT: emit a QUESTION envelope (the existing splice grammar) containing a drafted candidate claim — a checkable predicate with trials=0 and the constituent envelope hashes as refs and a generated why — or a candidate procedure cued by the recurring situation. Never auto-commit belief; the candidate sits on the ignorance graph like any open question. (3) ON OWNER/BINNA DECISION ENVELOPE confirming: mark constituent episodes 'folded-into:<claim-id>' and demote their salience rank in the fold/GRAPHIFY index — a reversible index operation; files, hashes, and why-ledger untouched, --asof still reaches everything. Prioritize mining cues from the ignorance graph and desiderata (the targeted-memory-reactivation pattern: cortex queries the fast store, Rothschild 2017 PMID:27941790, rather than the fast store flushing).
- cost: Build: a motif-grouping pass over spine envelopes (they already carry content-hash id, src, subject, refs, mandatory why — the grouping keys exist), a candidate-claim drafting template, question-envelope emission through the existing schema, and a small rank field in the fold's state render. Roughly one organ-sized feature inside an existing organ; no new store, no schema break. Runtime: a bounded nightly batch riding the existing memory-nightly schedule; incremental cursoring keeps it off the known 60s cold-fold pain rather than adding to it. The real recurring cost is OWNER REVIEW BANDWIDTH: every candidate is a question envelope awaiting his verdict, so proposer precision directly prices his attention.
- risks: (1) Junk-candidate flood: high-frequency operational chatter (15s beats, n8n confirmations, collector housekeeping) is the most recurrent pattern in any 71k-envelope corpus; a naive miner drafts claims like 'binna emits heartbeats', flooding review and poisoning the owner's trust in the channel — the mitigation (tonic-stream exclusion + K-independent-sources threshold) is load-bearing, not optional. (2) Where the biological principle would be WRONG for ETTZ: Richards & Frankland's objective — decision quality over fidelity of transmission — is only HALF of ETTZ's objective; the estate is also an archive and provenance system where fidelity is terminal, not instrumental. Imported naively, transience-as-regularization argues for coarsening everything old, which for a provenance estate is self-harm. Hence demotion must be strictly index-rank, never archival — and even then a wrongly-confirmed gist claim plus demoted constituent episodes reproduces biology's overgeneralization failure (false gist, cf. blocked fear generalization in Migues): the detail needed to falsify the bad claim has been made hard to find by the very act of believing it. Reversibility (refs + asof) is the safety line and must be tested, not assumed. (3) Frequency-as-salience drift: with no prediction-error signal, the proposer ranks motifs by count, so estate beliefs drift toward what happens often, not what matters — biology gates on surprise (Schultz 1997, Takeuchi 2016). Acceptable for v1 only because the owner's verdict is the salience gate; it stops being acceptable if review load pushes toward rubber-stamping. (4) Single-pass promotion pressure: the temptation to let a very strong motif skip trial accumulation recreates catastrophic interference in claims/ (McClelland 1995); candidates must enter at trials=0 and earn confirms like any belief.
- owning session: memory-nightly (best fit: it is the consolidation organ, already scheduled, already reads events/ and writes durable state; kallimachos owns disposal not abstraction, and BINNA/owner sit correctly downstream as the review gate; GRAPHIFY consumes the rank field)
- priority argument: Ranks first or near-first among all proposals. It is the owner's own flagged sharpest gap, and this analysis CONFIRMS the diagnosis from three independent literature clusters (deletion manufactures generalization; replay+downscaling is the gist engine; neurogenesis-driven forgetting presumes prior consolidation-out — Akers 2014). It converts already-sunk infrastructure (envelope schema with mandatory why, claims trial ledger, memory-nightly schedule, ignorance graph, splice grammar) into the one dividend the estate currently forgoes: without it, ETTZ's semantic layer grows only at hand speed (4 claims in 7 weeks against 71,225 episodes — a fast store with effectively no consolidation path), and entropy remains pure cost. It is doctrine-compatible by construction (question envelopes, owner-gated commitment, rank demotion not erasure) and cheap relative to any new-organ proposal. Its only serious dependency — proposer precision vs. owner attention — is bounded by starting read-only (drafts only, no demotion) for the first cycle.
