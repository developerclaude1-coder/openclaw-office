# astrocytes-support
MECHANISM: Astrocyte as territory-owning homeostatic controller: glutamate clearance (fast-bind/slow-dispose, worst-case-sized), uptake-coupled metabolic supply gating durable writes, contested gliotransmission permissive gate, staged synaptogenic secretome (silent-then-unsilence, hevin:SPARC ratio set-point), MEGF10/MERTK activity-tagged lifelong pruning, exclusive territorial tiling with motile exploration.
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: No single organ; fragments distributed: (a) territorial tiling → the fractal law + ORGANON scope (one owner per element, parent = union of children, SPEC-SPINE-READ) — genuinely present; (b) fast-bind/slow-dispose clearance shape → QUARANTINE (cheap reversible hold with .why.md manifests, decoupled from owner-authorized disposal) + planned custodian A-002 snapshot interlock as the point-of-no-return check; (c) transmitter clearance → the Collector (pull-only draining of organ outboxes into the spine) — a batch mover, not a sized buffer with backpressure; (d) health metering → mesh beat analysis (jitter/missed/drift) — but tonic self-reports, not per-exchange third-party detection; (e) trophic support → CUSTODIAN backups — archival, not consolidation fuel; (f) pruning effector → kallimachos entropy A-003 + quarantine — but dwell-clock (time) driven, not activity-tagged; (g) proto-Hebbian usage signal → BANK balances and procedures' verified counts — the signal exists with no consumer.
VERDICT: partial

## Argument for
At the level of computational logic, two astrocyte mechanisms are present under ETTZ names. First, tiling: Bushong 2002 (PMID 11756501) shows adjacent protoplasmic astrocytes hold essentially exclusive, non-overlapping domains whose union covers the neuropil — every synapse has exactly one owning controller. That is ETTZ's fractal law and BINNA's SPEC-SPINE-READ ('a parent is the union of its children') stated as anatomy; the estate already enforces one-owner-per-element homing (every envelope has src, every store is homed). Second, the two-timescale clearance split: Diamond & Jahr 1997 (PMID 9169528) shows the fast operation is BINDING (hundreds of microseconds) decoupled from the slow transport cycle (tens of ms) that actually disposes. ETTZ's quarantine architecture has precisely this shape — a cheap, reversible hold (move to _Quarantine with a why-manifest) decoupled from an expensive, authorized, irreversible disposal (owner verdict, plus the planned A-002 'does a verified snapshot hold this?' interlock). Biology independently validates that as the correct architecture for removal. Third, the raw material for MEGF10-style logic exists: Chung 2013 (PMID 24270812) shows the elimination cue is derived from the same activity stream that scores retention — no second pipeline — and ETTZ's BANK balances, derived from spine envelopes, are exactly that single shared usage stream. The Collector, the mesh's beat analysis, and CUSTODIAN each carry a recognizable sliver of the support role (clearing outboxes, monitoring, trophic backup).

## Argument against
The analogy breaks in four places. (1) The astrocyte's defining property is being a NON-PARTICIPANT per-territory controller that sits beside every exchange; ETTZ has no such thing. BEATS are tonic self-reports by the organs themselves; the mesh analyses them but meters no per-exchange flow; the Collector moves envelopes on a schedule with no notion of buffer sizing or backpressure. Lehre & Danbolt 1998 (PMID 9786982) shows the clearance buffer is provisioned at 3-5x a simultaneous discharge of the ENTIRE population, and Tanaka 1997 (PMID 9180080) shows what happens without it: runaway (seizures, death), not slow service. Nothing in ETTZ is sized against worst-case simultaneous envelope discharge from all 12 sources, and stale-fabric alarms fire on SILENCE, not on flood — the estate detects the wrong failure direction for this mechanism. (2) The pruning selector is absent: entropy A-003 prunes by 30-day dwell clocks and owner verdict — time-dependent and judgment-dependent — while MEGF10/MERTK pruning is activity-tagged (Chung 2013), and Lee 2021 (PMID 33361813) shows blocking it leaves excess but functionally IMPAIRED synapses. (3) Coverage geometry as a tunable isolation dial (Oliet 2001, PMID 11340204) has no equivalent — organ coupling via contracts is binary, not continuous. (4) The consolidation fuel gate (Suzuki 2011, PMID 21376239: durable writes have a pre-staged local energy precondition transient ones lack) maps to nothing — the face's cold fold recomputes 71k entries in 60s+ with no per-organ pre-staged delta depot; the fingerprint cache is a central memo, not local fuel. One further candidate must be struck entirely: the D-serine permissive gate cannot be mapped as a dependency because it is contested in biology itself (Henneberger 2010's own caveat; Petravicz 2008 PMID 18463250 and Agulhon 2010 PMID 20203048 find intact plasticity without astrocytic Ca2+ signalling, per the verification critique).

## Strongest objection
The objection to 'partial' from both directions at once. From the 'missing' side: what ETTZ has are coincidental shape-resemblances, not the mechanism — the Collector clears on a minutes/batch timescale where transporters act in microseconds, quarantine holds files where transporters hold molecules, and the one thing that makes an astrocyte an astrocyte (a dedicated third-party controller owning a territory's entire signal hygiene) is nowhere; scattered fragments without the integrating role should score 'missing'. From the 'deliberately different' side: the sharpest astrocyte finding — Lee 2021's demonstration that failing to DESTROY degrades the surviving representation — collides head-on with owner law (compress and archive, never erase), so the pruning core of the mapping should be scored as doctrine-divergence, not as a gap to fill.

## Verdict survives because
The 'missing' half fails because the project's stated test is computational logic (signal, tag, threshold, timescale-STRUCTURE), not literal timescale: Diamond & Jahr's lesson is the DECOUPLING of a cheap reversible hold from an expensive irreversible dispose, and quarantine implements exactly that decoupling, including the biologically correct ordering (hold first, verify, then authorized disposal — the A-002 interlock is a grace-verification step biology performs implicitly); likewise Bushong's tiling logic (exclusive ownership, union-covers-field) is genuinely load-bearing in ETTZ, not decorative — BINNA's merged read and the homed stores depend on it. Two of seven logics genuinely present, with named absences, is the definition of 'partial'. The 'deliberately different' half fails as a WHOLE-mapping verdict because doctrine covers only the effector, not the selector: biology's destructive step is disconnection from the active substrate, and ETTZ's archival law is satisfiable by removal from the retrieval path (quarantine batch, closed window) — so the activity-tagged SELECTOR is importable without touching doctrine, which means its absence is a real gap, not a doctrinal choice. I record the effector divergence explicitly in doctrine_notes rather than letting it absorb the verdict.

## Missing pieces
- An envelope-stream intake controller sized against worst-case simultaneous discharge from all sources, with backpressure — and alarms for flood, not only silence (Lehre & Danbolt 1998 sizing rule; Tanaka 1997 runaway failure mode)
- A use-derived retention/retirement selector: nothing consumes BANK balances or flow-ref recurrence to nominate quarantine candidates; entropy A-003 is dwell-clock-only (Chung 2013, Lee 2021)
- Tunable inter-organ isolation: contract coupling is binary; no equivalent of coverage geometry as a continuous crosstalk dial (Oliet 2001)
- Two-phase silent-then-promoted link construction: no 'silent vs active' state on links, and no promotion rule driven by usage balances (Christopherson 2005 TSP silent synapses; Allen 2012 glypican unsilencing-by-relocation)
- Set-point control by a matched opposing pair: any future promotion/pruning threshold is a single level, where biology regulates a ratio from one source (hevin:SPARC, Kucukdereli 2011)
- Per-organ pre-staged consolidation state so the nightly fold merges deltas instead of a 60s+ cold recompute — the local fuel depot pattern (Suzuki 2011; Pellerin & Magistretti 1994: demand signal = payment signal, so envelopes should carry their own fold-cost accounting)
- Subject-scope boundary arbitration at join time: watch-contracts proposes joins but does not negotiate exclusive subject territory, the pathology tiling exists to prevent (Bushong 2002); corollary warning — GFAP shows only ~15% of true astrocyte volume, so treat contract-declared scope and the GRAPHIFY-rendered connectome as lower bounds on real coupling

## Adoptable proposal: Use-tagged quarantine nominator (MEGF10 selector for entropy A-003)
- biological principle: Astrocytic MEGF10/MERTK pruning derives its elimination cue from the same activity stream that scores retention — weakly-used connections are nominated by ordinary usage data, with no second scoring pipeline (Chung 2013, PMID 24270812), and it runs lifelong, not only developmentally (Lee 2021, PMID 33361813). Imported value: store-or-purge decisions become use-dependent, not just time-dependent — exactly the owner's named design question.
- ettz change: Add a read-only scoring pass to kallimachos A-003 that consumes existing spine-derived signals (BANK usage balances, flow-ref recurrence, last-referenced-by-envelope) and emits QUARANTINE-NOMINATION envelopes (with mandatory why, per schema) ranking files already in entropy's remit by a use-score alongside the existing 30-day dwell clock. Nothing downstream changes: grace verification, the custodian A-002 snapshot interlock, and owner authorization still gate disposal; the nominator only reorders and justifies candidates. Hard scope exclusion built in: claims and procedures are NEVER scored — claims die by window-close only, procedures by failure only (frozen vocabulary law); the selector applies solely to file/artifact material entropy already handles.
- cost: Build: one nightly scoring job inside kallimachos reading aggregates the bank fold already produces, plus one new envelope subject-type and a small extension to the .why.md manifest template ('nominated: dwell 34d, zero envelope references in 60d, bank balance 0'). No new organ, no new store, no wire changes. Runtime: one pass over usage aggregates per nightly run — negligible next to the existing 60s+ cold fold; adds envelope volume of order tens per night.
- risks: (1) Popularity bias nominating rare-but-critical material: a disaster-recovery script or the smartctl evidence file may have zero recent use precisely because things are healthy. Mitigation is structural (claims/procedures excluded by construction) but files referenced BY procedures need a reference-closure check or the nominator will nominate a verified procedure's dependency. (2) Self-confirming loop: quarantined items cannot accrue use, so nomination ratchets; grace verification must add a 'sought-and-missed' signal (spine queries or flow-ref lookups that failed against quarantined material) or the loop never exonerates anything. (3) Where the biological principle is WRONG for ETTZ: Lee 2021's urgency ('failing to prune actively impairs memory') holds for superposed neural representations where clutter causes interference at read time; ETTZ retrieval is explicit and indexed, so un-pruned material costs fold time, disk, and owner attention — not correctness. Importing the biological urgency at full strength would over-aggressively quarantine; the selector must rank, never auto-quarantine. (4) If the use-score ever leaks into procedure or claim handling it directly violates the NO-CLOCK / never-delete laws — the guard test that fails on any delete path should gain a sibling that fails if the nominator's input set includes claims/ or procedures/.
- owning session: kallimachos/entropy A-003 (data source: the bank's envelope-derived balances; disposal gate unchanged: custodian A-002 interlock + owner authorization)
- priority argument: Ranks high among astrocyte-derived proposals and mid-high overall. For it: it answers a design question the owner posed verbatim ('store-or-purge decisions — use-dependent, not just time-dependent'); the enabling stream (bank balances from envelopes) already exists with zero consumers, so it is the rare proposal that is pure wiring; its biological evidence (Chung 2013, Lee 2021) is uncontested, unlike the gliotransmission gate the critique flagged; and it strengthens rather than strains doctrine, since it only reorders candidates inside the existing quarantine-then-owner-verdict pipeline. Against ranking it first overall: the owner's suspected sharpest gap (nothing promotes repeated episodes into claims/procedures) is a bigger capability hole than better pruning, and ETTZ's failure mode from NOT having this selector is cost and clutter, not corruption — so it should queue behind an episodic-to-semantic promoter but ahead of speculative items like isolation dials or silent-link states, which need infrastructure this proposal does not.

## Doctrine notes
This mapping touches owner law at exactly one point and the divergence must be stated as doctrine, not failure: astrocytes DESTROY (MEGF10/MERTK engulfment is physical consumption of the synapse), while owner's absolute law is compress and archive, never erase — quarantine, not destruction; owner authorization gates disposal; claims close windows and never delete (a guard test enforces the absent delete path); procedures never age, they fail. ETTZ is therefore 'deliberately different' on the pruning EFFECTOR by explicit doctrine. The reconcilable reading, which the adoptable proposal relies on: biology's functionally load-bearing step is disconnection from the active substrate, and ETTZ's law is satisfied by removal from the retrieval path (quarantine batch with why-manifest, closed validity window) rather than removal from existence — the custodian A-002 snapshot interlock then serves as the estate's point-of-no-return check, a grace step biology does not have and doctrine requires. The proposal imports only the biological SELECTOR (use-tagged nomination); the effector chain — quarantine, verification, owner verdict — is untouched, and per the hard constraints nothing builds until the owner rules.
