# microglia-pruning
MECHANISM: Microglia run a tonic whole-territory scan and remove synapses by a two-stage MARK-then-EXECUTE architecture: the target self-writes an eat-me mark (local phosphatidylserine flip, Scott-Hewitt 2020 PMID:32657463), complement amplifies it into a covalent item-local tag (C1q→C3→iC3b, Stevens 2007 PMID:18083105) read by a single scoped reader (CR3, Schafer 2012 PMID:22632727), while use-dependence is carried not by the tag but by an activity-renewed don't-eat-me VETO evaluated at the moment of commitment (CD47/SIRPα, Lehrman 2018 PMID:30308165) — and the same contact that nibbles also induces growth (Weinhard 2018 PMID:29581545; Miyamoto 2016 PMID:27558646).
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: Executor + threshold: kallimachos/ENTROPY A-003 ("files earn their death": 30-day dwell clocks, grace verification, heal-before-harm; 2 batches disposed). Non-destructive staging: QUARANTINE batches with .why.md manifests (owner-delete-residue, music-review-rejects), owner authorization gating disposal. Commitment-time veto: the PLANNED CUSTODIAN A-002 interlock ("does a verified snapshot hold this?" — "no" blocks the delete). Tonic surveillance: BEATS (15s heartbeats, mesh jitter/missed/drift analysis). Reason-carrying tag material: the WHY-LEDGER + .why.md manifests. Latent use signal: the BANK's usage balances derived from spine envelopes, and CHOICE envelopes. Pro-connective organ (separate from the pruner): watch-contracts. No ETTZ equivalent of: the decoupled eat-me tag, the use-renewed veto, the bounded competence window, or filopodia induction from the pruning scan.
VERDICT: partial

## Argument for
The analogy holds at the level of architecture, not just vocabulary, on four of the seven computational elements.

(1) EXECUTOR SEPARATED FROM DECISION. Biology decouples mark from execute by hours-to-days: iC3b is deposited by the item's context, CR3 reads it later, and the reader set is scoped to one cell type in the P5 dLGN (Stevens 2007 PMID:18083105; Schafer 2012 PMID:22632727). ETTZ has exactly this shape: a candidate is identified, staged into a Quarantine batch with a .why.md manifest, and the actual disposal is a separate act by a separate authority (the owner). Same two-stage logic, different actors.

(2) A COMMITMENT-SITE VETO, NOT A DECISION-SITE ONE. This is the strongest genuine correspondence. CD47/SIRPα is an ITIM receptor that recruits SHP phosphatases and aborts assembly of the phagocytic cup — the brake fires at the act, after the target has already been selected and approached (Lehrman 2018 PMID:30308165). The planned CUSTODIAN A-002 interlock is structurally identical: kallimachos has already decided, has already approached, and a different organ answers "does a verified snapshot hold this?" at the point of irreversibility, with "no" aborting. ETTZ independently arrived at veto-at-the-act-site rather than veto-in-the-scoring-function. That is a non-trivial convergence.

(3) DWELL AS THE ESCALATION VARIABLE. Wake 2009 (PMID:19339593) is precise: contact per se is routine (~5 min, ~once/hour), and what distinguishes routine polling from commitment is contact DURATION — after ischemia contacts extend to ~1 h and the bouton frequently disappears. ETTZ's entropy uses a 30-day dwell clock: the state variable that promotes an item from "seen" to "actionable" is time-in-state, not the fact of being seen. Same variable, same role, timescale rescaled by ~10^4.

(4) HEAL-BEFORE-HARM AND GRACE VERIFICATION AS EAT-ME PRECONDITIONS. Biology does not let the deleter be the sole judge: the terminal flips PS itself first, and C1q-null mice show ELEVATED presynaptic PS with REDUCED engulfment, proving PS is upstream of complement rather than a consequence of it (Scott-Hewitt 2020 PMID:32657463). ETTZ's grace verification and heal-before-harm are the same ordering constraint — the item's own condition must be independently established before the disposer acts, so the disposer is an amplifier of a prior signal, not its origin.

(5) DOCTRINE-CONVERGENT SAFETY. Biology's pruning apparatus is dangerous precisely where ETTZ is conservative. CR3 or C3 deletion removes only ~50% of engulfment — redundant paths, no guard — and the resulting deficits are permanent (1.3x adult synapse density, 1.8x VGlut2 puncta, sustained segregation failure; Schafer 2012). Hong 2016 (PMID:27033548) shows the same C1q/C3/CR3 machinery reactivating in adult AD models produces pathological synapse loss. ETTZ's single disposal path with a guard test that fails if a delete path appears in the claims class is strictly safer than anything biology implements. The literature validates the doctrine rather than indicting it.

## Argument against
Three of the seven elements are absent, and one apparent match is superficial.

(1) THE USE SIGNAL IS NOT WIRED IN AT ALL — this is the real gap, and it is a wiring gap, not a data gap. Biology's selection is COMPARATIVE and ACTIVITY-KEYED: TTX into one eye at P4 makes that eye's inputs preferentially engulfed by P5, and forskolin inverts the preference (Schafer 2012 PMID:22632727) — the comparator is relative between rivals for shared postsynaptic territory, not an absolute threshold on any one input. ETTZ's entropy threshold is absolute and single-item (30 days elapsed). The signal biology uses exists in ETTZ — the bank derives usage BALANCES from 71,225 spine envelopes across 12 sources — and kallimachos does not read it. Two organs hold the two halves of the mechanism and there is no path between them.

(2) POLARITY IS INVERTED RELATIVE TO BIOLOGY, AND ETTZ HAS THE WRONG HALF. The decisive result is Lehrman 2018 (PMID:30308165): in CD47-null mice microglia do not merely over-eat, they lose activity preference ENTIRELY. Use-dependence is carried by the brake, not by the tag. Being used does not mark a synapse for keeping — it renews an expiring exemption; being silent merely lets an exemption lapse. ETTZ's dwell clock implements "silence accrues toward disposal," which reads as the same thing but is not: an accruing clock is positive evidence for disposal, whereas a lapsing veto is the absence of evidence against it. The difference is observable in the failure mode — a clock disposes of an item nobody has looked at because nobody looked; a lapsing veto disposes of it only when nothing objects at the moment of commitment.

(3) NO BOUNDED COMPETENCE WINDOW. Engulfment is high at P5 and sharply reduced by P9 while microglia retain full phagocytic CAPABILITY — competence expires independently of capability (Schafer 2012). Hong 2016 is the demonstration of what an unwindowed pruner does. ETTZ's entropy appears to be an ambient standing capability with no expiry on its licence to nominate. Nothing in the brief time-boxes a disposal campaign or forces a nomination to go stale.

(4) THE PRUNING APPARATUS HAS NO CONSTRUCTIVE OUTPUT — the sharpest asymmetry. Entropy's sweep is the estate's only whole-territory scan over files, the direct analogue of microglial tiling surveillance (Nimmerjahn 2005 PMID:15831717). In biology that same contact is a growth stimulus: 39% of contacted spines extruded a spine-head filopodium directed at the microglial process versus 7% of non-contacted spines, with the spine head frequently relocating to the filopodium tip and occasionally onto a NEW bouton — partner switching, not partner deletion (Weinhard 2018 PMID:29581545), independently confirmed in vivo where microglial ablation lowers spine density and L4→L2/3 connectivity (Miyamoto 2016 PMID:27558646). ETTZ's scan emits disposal candidates and nothing else. watch-contracts is pro-connective but reads DECLARED published contracts; it is a different organ, on different input, and nothing in the estate proposes an edge from OBSERVED co-occurrence. The estate listens for new limbs; it does not reach.

(5) A SUPERFICIAL MATCH TO FLAG. It is tempting to call .why.md manifests the iC3b tag. They are not. iC3b is deposited BEFORE and INDEPENDENTLY of the decision to act, is item-local and covalent, carries no instruction, and is read later by a different process. A .why.md manifest is written BY the disposer AT staging time. It is a receipt, not a tag. ETTZ currently has no annotation that says "flagged, at T, by S, because Z" living on an item ahead of any disposal decision.

CAVEAT (per critic): I do not rest anything on trogocytosis being a mechanistically separate pathway from complement-tagged engulfment — that rests on a non-significant difference in CR3-KO slices (2.3 ± 0.7 vs 1.5 ± 0.6 events/cell/3 h, p = 0.37), which is absence of evidence. What survives is the CLEM observation that sub-micron partial removal occurs while the synapse persists: an existence proof that partial edit is a real operation, not a claim about its machinery.

## Strongest objection
"Partial" is the wrong verdict; the honest verdict is "deliberately different." The owner's absolute law — compress and archive, never erase; quarantine, not destruction; owner authorization gates disposal — makes the microglial architecture inapplicable at its root. Microglia are a use-dependent DESTROYER; ETTZ is a use-independent STAGER. Calling ETTZ "partial" scores it as deficient against a template it has deliberately rejected, and then invents a missing piece (use-dependence) whose entire purpose in biology is to decide what to irreversibly destroy — a decision ETTZ does not make and will not make. Worse, the two named exclusions prove the point from inside the estate: procedures deliberately have NO CLOCK, and claims never die. A system that has explicitly legislated against both decay-by-disuse and decay-by-time in its two most valuable stores is not missing use-dependent pruning; it has ruled on it. And on a second front: the brief's "dwell clock" may already BE a recency-of-access measure, in which case ETTZ has use-dependence and I have mislabelled a shipped mechanism as absent.

## Verdict survives because
The objection conflates the EXECUTOR with the SELECTOR, and Lehrman 2018 is precisely the result that separates them. In biology the eat-me tag, the activity-renewed veto and the phagocytic act are three independently manipulable stages — CD47-null microglia still engulf, they just engulf indiscriminately. Nothing in "compress and archive, never erase" constrains the SELECTOR. The doctrine says what happens to a chosen item; it is silent on how the item is chosen. ETTZ has built the executor half — and built it better than biology, with one path plus a guard where biology has redundant paths and none — and has left the selector half at "30 days elapsed." That is the definition of partial: a specific, nameable stage of the computational logic is absent while adjacent stages are present.

The exclusions strengthen this rather than defeating it. Procedures-no-clock and claims-never-die are correct rulings, and biology agrees with them for a reason the owner has not yet stated: Schafer 2012's comparator is RELATIVE, between inputs competing for shared postsynaptic territory. Where there is no rival contending for the same slot, the comparator has no denominator and use-dependence is undefined, not merely unwise. A disaster-recovery procedure has no rival occupying its slot; a claim like "the NAS drive is physically healthy" is queried rarely precisely because nothing has gone wrong, so usage anti-correlates with value there. But bulk file residue on the NAS — owner-delete-residue, music-review-rejects — IS resource-contended: disk is the shared postsynaptic territory, and the comparator's precondition holds exactly there, which is exactly where entropy already operates. The exclusions are a boundary on where the mechanism applies, not a refusal of it.

On the second front: even granting that dwell is recency-of-access, the verdict holds with the missing piece narrowed rather than removed. Recency-of-access is a single-item absolute threshold. Biology's is (a) relative between rivals, (b) carried by a veto whose polarity means silence is a lapse rather than evidence, and (c) bounded by an expiring competence window. Under the recency reading, all three remain absent — and the multi-source, multi-touch signal that actually exists in the estate (the bank's usage balances over 71k envelopes) is still not read by kallimachos. The verdict is "partial" either way; only the size of the gap changes.

I therefore keep "partial" for the mapping as a whole, and record "deliberately different" as the correct local verdict for the executor stage alone — destroy versus quarantine — which is doctrine, and which the literature endorses.

## Missing pieces
- No use signal reaches the disposer. The bank derives usage balances from spine envelopes; kallimachos/entropy A-003 decides on a 30-day dwell clock and does not read them. Both halves of the mechanism exist in the estate with no path between them.
- No don't-eat-me veto keyed on use. The planned CUSTODIAN A-002 interlock is a DURABILITY veto ('is it backed up?'), which is the right structural position (commitment site, ITIM-like, abort-on-no) but the wrong variable. Biology's veto at that position is activity-renewed (Lehrman 2018 PMID:30308165). ETTZ has the socket and not the signal — and the interlock is planned, not shipped.
- No decoupled, item-local eat-me tag. .why.md manifests are written by the disposer at staging time; iC3b is deposited by the item's own context, before and independently of the decision, and read later by a scoped reader. ETTZ has no append-only 'flagged, at T, by S, because Z' annotation living on a content-hash id ahead of any disposal nomination.
- No bounded competence window on the pruner. Microglial engulfment competence collapses P5→P9 while capability persists; Hong 2016 (PMID:27033548) shows the unwindowed case is pathological. ETTZ's entropy is an ambient standing licence with no expiry on a nomination and no time-boxed campaign.
- No relative comparator and no gain control. Biology compares rivals for shared territory and bounds competition by divisive normalization (Carandini & Heeger 2012 PMID:22108672). ETTZ has an absolute per-item threshold and no normalizing organ, so any raw usage signal introduced now would run rich-get-richer without a denominator.
- No constructive output from the pruning scan. Entropy's sweep is the estate's only whole-territory file scan, and in biology that same contact induces directed filopodia (39% vs 7% contacted vs non-contacted; Weinhard 2018 PMID:29581545) and its ablation reduces connectivity (Miyamoto 2016 PMID:27558646). The scan touches everything and proposes nothing.
- No compress-in-place operator. 'Compress and archive' is stated as law, but the only described operations are whole-item quarantine and disposal. Weinhard 2018's CLEM shows sub-micron partial removal with the synapse surviving — partial edit as a first-class operation distinct from removal. ETTZ has the doctrine without the operator.
- No self-avoidance rule. Clustered protocadherin self-avoidance (Lefebvre 2012 PMID:22842903, via critic) is the canonical mechanism that stops a process treating its own siblings as partners. ETTZ has no rule excluding an organ's own envelopes from signals about the items that organ inspects — which is the direct cause of the first failure mode below.

## Adoptable proposal: The CD47 exemption: usage renews dwell, and can only ever extend it
- biological principle: Use-dependence is carried by an activity-renewed don't-eat-me VETO, not by the eat-me tag. In CD47-null mice microglia do not merely over-eat — they lose activity preference entirely, and the veto is evaluated at the commitment point where SIRPα/SHP aborts the phagocytic cup (Lehrman 2018 PMID:30308165). Being used renews an exemption; being silent lets an exemption lapse. Polarity is the whole content of the mechanism.
- ettz change: One predicate in kallimachos/entropy A-003's dwell evaluator, plus one new envelope type. At dwell evaluation, before nominating a file for Quarantine, entropy queries a subject-indexed touch view derived from spine envelopes (materialized nightly by memory-nightly, using the bank's existing balance derivation). If the item's content-hash id has been referenced by any source OTHER than kallimachos itself, CUSTODIAN/restic, or GRAPHIFY rendering within the dwell window, entropy emits an EXEMPTION envelope — why = the specific touches, with their srcs and timestamps — and extends the dwell clock by one bounded increment. It never shortens a dwell clock and never nominates anything the current 30-day rule would not already nominate: the change is monotone-conservative by construction, and a guard test asserts that (fails if any code path lets the veto reduce remaining dwell). Cumulative extension is capped so an item cannot become immortal by noise; on cap, the item proceeds to Quarantine on the normal path with the exemption history attached to its .why.md. EXPLICIT EXCLUSION LIST, enforced as a test: procedures/ (no clock, by doctrine — and by Schafer 2012's logic there is no rival contending for a procedure's slot, so the comparator is undefined, not merely unwise), claims/ (windows close, never delete; query rate anti-correlates with value — 'the NAS drive is physically healthy' is quiet precisely because nothing is wrong), the why-ledger, and events/. The veto applies ONLY to entropy's file domain, where disk is genuinely the contended shared territory. Read-only from the bank; append-only into the spine; no mutation of any existing record.
- cost: BUILD: small, roughly one focused session. A subject/content-hash-indexed touch-count view (the bank already derives usage balances from spine envelopes, so this is a re-projection, not new collection); one envelope schema addition (exemption, with refs to the touch envelopes cited as the why); one predicate plus one bounded counter in kalli's dwell evaluator; three guard tests (monotonicity, exclusion list, self-source exclusion). RUNTIME: the cost driver is the scan, not the predicate. Naive evaluation is O(candidates x 71k envelopes) per batch, which will behave like the known cold-fold pain (60s+ and growing). The view must therefore be materialized by memory-nightly and cached on the same exact-fingerprint discipline the face's fold already uses; per-candidate cost then falls to an index lookup. Ongoing: one extra envelope per exempted candidate per batch — bounded by candidate count, small against 71,225 existing entries, and each one is a genuine why-ledger entry rather than chatter.
- risks: 1. SELF-TOUCH CONTAMINATION — the concrete killer. Kallimachos' own scan emits envelopes referencing each file it examines. Those count as touches, every candidate becomes permanently exempt, and disposal stops silently while all the logs look healthy. This is the self-avoidance problem (Lefebvre 2012 PMID:22842903): a process must not count its own siblings as partners. Mitigated by the src exclusion list, but the exclusion list is now safety-critical and must be tested, not documented.
2. BACKUP-TOUCH CONTAMINATION — same class, separately fatal: CUSTODIAN/restic reads every file on every run, producing uniform touches and therefore uniform exemption. Same fix, different src, and it would be totally silent.
3. DISPOSAL-THROUGHPUT LEAK — usage anti-correlates with value in cold archive. A meme touched weekly gains permanent exemption; a 2019 tax PDF touched never does not. Because the veto is monotone this cannot cause data loss, so the failure presents as entropy appearing to 'stop working' — the estate bloats and the owner loses trust in the organ. The cumulative-extension cap is the only thing standing between this and unbounded retention.
4. RICH-GET-RICHER WITH NO DENOMINATOR — GRAPHIFY rendering a node touches it, which exempts it, which keeps it in the graph, which renders it. Biology bounds this by divisive normalization (Carandini & Heeger 2012 PMID:22108672); ETTZ has no gain-control organ. This is why the threshold must be relative to the batch's touch distribution (matching Schafer 2012's relative comparator) rather than an absolute count, and why GRAPHIFY sits on the excluded-src list in v1.
5. KEY DRIFT — if touches are matched on path or subject string, an organ renaming its subject namespace makes years of touches stop matching and a long-loved file looks cold the next morning. Representational drift is real and unmodelled here (Ziv 2013 PMID:23396101). Mitigation is hard: key the touch count on content-hash id only, and never let a rename reset a dwell clock.
6. INERT-BUT-REASSURING — if the view is built and the evaluator never actually consults it, the estate gains a comforting story and no behaviour change. Needs one M1 eval-harness assertion replaying a known batch and checking that a touched file's dwell actually extended.
7. WHERE THE BIOLOGY WOULD BE WRONG, restated as a live risk: if the exclusion list ever erodes and this reaches procedures/ or claims/, the mechanism inverts the estate's value ordering — it would preferentially age out the disaster-recovery procedure that is never used until the one day it is the only thing that matters, and the quiet claims that hold precisely because nothing has gone wrong. Biology only prunes by use where rivals contend for a shared slot; neither store has rivals. That boundary is the single most important thing for the owner to rule on.
- owning session: kallimachos/entropy A-003 owns the change (the dwell evaluator, the exclusion list, the guard tests). Two read-only dependencies, neither of which should own it: the bank supplies the usage-balance derivation, and memory-nightly materializes the subject/content-hash touch index on the existing fold-cache discipline. The M1 eval harness owns the replay assertion. No new session required.
- priority argument: Rank SECOND, immediately behind shipping the CUSTODIAN A-002 interlock. A-002 outranks it because that interlock is the ITIM checkpoint at the act site and it prevents actual irrecoverable loss, whereas this proposal only improves selection quality — safety before taste. But this ranks above everything else in the pruning cluster for three reasons. First, it is provably monotone: it can only slow disposal relative to today's pure clock, so it is the rare doctrine-adjacent change that cannot violate 'never erase' even if it is buggy — the worst failure is that nothing gets disposed of, which is loud and recoverable. Second, it consumes a signal that already exists and is currently stranded (71,225 envelopes, 12 sources, bank balances already derived), so it is wiring, not construction. Third, it is the prerequisite for the more valuable but riskier items behind it — the decoupled eat-me tag envelope, the expiring competence window, and the filopodia-analog constructive output from the entropy scan all assume a usage signal reaching the disposer, and none of them should be built before the owner has ruled on whether use may inform disposal at all. Rank it below any work on the episodic-to-semantic promotion gap, which is the estate's larger deficit and is not addressed by this literature at all.

## Doctrine notes
Four points where this mapping touches owner law, named explicitly.

1. DESTROY vs QUARANTINE IS DOCTRINE, NOT DEFICIENCY — and the literature endorses the doctrine. Microglia execute irreversibly; ETTZ stages into Quarantine with a .why.md manifest and requires owner authorization to dispose. Biology has no analogue of the claims class's guard test that fails if a delete path appears, and it pays for that: CR3 or C3 deletion removes only ~50% of engulfment (redundant paths, no guard), the deficits are permanent into adulthood, the window never reopens to correct the error (Schafer 2012 PMID:22632727), and the same machinery reactivating outside its window produces pathological loss (Hong 2016 PMID:27033548). ETTZ's single-path-with-guard is strictly safer than the biological design. Record the local verdict for the executor stage as "deliberately different," and record that the divergence is an improvement.

2. THE VETO'S POSITION IS ALREADY DOCTRINALLY CORRECT; ONLY ITS VARIABLE IS WRONG. Biology places the brake at the ACT site, not the decision site — SIRPα/SHP aborts the phagocytic cup at the point of commitment (Lehrman 2018 PMID:30308165). The planned CUSTODIAN A-002 interlock sits in exactly that position and answers exactly that shape of question. This is convergent design and should be recognized as such; the proposal above adds a second veto in the same socket rather than relocating anything.

3. PROCEDURES-NO-CLOCK AND CLAIMS-NEVER-DIE ARE THE HARD BOUNDARY, AND BIOLOGY AGREES. Schafer 2012's comparator is relative, between inputs competing for shared postsynaptic territory. Where nothing contends for the slot, use-dependence has no denominator and is undefined rather than merely inadvisable. Neither a procedure nor a claim has a rival occupying its slot; both have usage rates that anti-correlate with value (the rare critical procedure, the quiet claim that holds because nothing has gone wrong). Any use-dependent mechanism must carry these as tested exclusions, not documented intentions. This is the point the owner most needs to rule on.

4. APPEND-ONLY CONSTRAINS THE IMPLEMENTATION SHAPE. Repairs are splices, the estate is append-only at its core. Therefore the exemption must be an envelope with a why and refs, not a mutable field on a file's metadata, and the eventual eat-me tag (if the owner rules for it) must likewise be an append-only annotation keyed to a content-hash id — which is in fact closer to biology than a mutable flag would be, since iC3b is a covalent, item-local, instruction-free mark deposited by the item's context and read later by a scoped reader.

5. STATUS. Research and comparison only. Nothing above is built or should be built; the proposal is a candidate for owner review, and its exclusion list is the part that needs his ruling before any code exists.
