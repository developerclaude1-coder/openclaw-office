# apoptosis-vs-necrosis
MECHANISM: Apoptosis vs necrosis: two-phase programmed disposal (tag → contained packaging → verified phagocytic handoff → degradation inside the recipient), immunologically silent by construction; necrosis = the same plan with an unacknowledged handoff, spilling DAMPs and provoking inflammation (Kerr 1972 PMID:4561027; Nagata 2018 PMID:29400998).
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: The entropy pipeline (kallimachos A-003): dwell clock → grace verification → heal-before-harm → _Quarantine batch with .why.md manifest → owner-gated disposal; plus the PLANNED custodian A-002 interlock ('does a verified snapshot hold this? no → blocks the delete'). Necrosis in ETTZ = disk failure, corruption, and un-manifested deletion — with one observed instance already handled: the owner-delete-residue quarantine batch is the estate performing post-hoc phagocytosis of necrotic debris. Inflammation response = heartbeat-silence and stale-fabric alarms plus quarantine of the blast radius under a .why.md. Note the mapping is scoped to the FILE/artifact layer: memory records (claims) deliberately die by accessibility switching (window closure), the Ryan & Frankland 2022 (PMID:35027710) / Bouton 2004 (PMID:15466298) route, not by apoptosis.
VERDICT: partial

## Argument for
The structural correspondence is the tightest in the whole project, stage for stage. (1) Contained packaging: Kerr 1972 describes condensation into membrane-bound bodies whose contents remain intact and ultrastructurally preserved before any degradation — a _Quarantine batch with a .why.md manifest is exactly this: contents intact, boundary explicit, reason attached, nothing spilled. (2) Tagging: the caspase cascade simultaneously kills, emits find-me signals, and posts an eat-me tag (phosphatidylserine externalization read by TIM4/TAM; Nagata 2018) — the dwell-clock expiry is the find-me, the .why.md manifest is the eat-me tag, and policy lives in the tag, not the executor. (3) Silent by construction: apoptosis clears ~10^10-10^11 cells/day without inflammation because every death is packaged and handed off; ETTZ disposal emits envelopes with mandatory `why` into the spine, so orderly death leaves an auditable, non-alarming trace — the estate's equivalent of immunological silence. (4) Sub-lethal scoping: caspase-3 runs locally in dendrites for LTD, bounded by XIAP/Bcl-xL inhibitor thresholds, without killing the cell (Li 2010 PMID:20510932) — heal-before-harm and owner-authorization gates are exactly inhibitor thresholds bounding the blast radius of a destructive routine. (5) Single guardable executor: Bax is the sole death executor for adult-born neurons and knocking it out abolishes the death path entirely (Sun 2004 PMID:15590937) — mirroring ETTZ's pattern of one disposal verb whose absence/presence a guard test can check, as the claims class already does for delete. (6) Most decisively, the pathology maps: Nagata shows failed engulfment collapses to secondary necrosis — necrosis IS apoptosis minus the completed handoff — which is a literature-grade argument that the A-002 interlock is not hygiene but the load-bearing element. And the estate has already exhibited both the disease and the immune response: owner-delete-residue (deletion outside protocol, leaving debris) was quarantined with a manifest — DAMP cleanup, textbook.

## Argument against
Four real disanalogies. (1) Autonomy inversion: apoptosis is cell-autonomous — the dying cell runs its own death program, and the system's throughput (10^11/day) exists precisely BECAUSE no central adjudicator attends any death; ETTZ inverts this, funnelling every disposal through owner attention (2 batches in ~6 weeks). Biology's silence is an argument AGAINST central gating; ETTZ's gate is anti-apoptotic in architecture even while apoptotic in protocol. (2) Irreversibility: past executioner-caspase commitment there is no appeal; ETTZ death is reversible until the owner rules. The pipelines only look alike before the commit point. (3) Tag semantics: biological death candidacy is earned by losing a competition for a real resource or activity signal — limiting trophic factor (Oppenheim 1991 PMID:2031577), per-cell NMDA input (Tashiro 2006 PMID:16906136) — whereas ETTZ's dwell clock is a TTL: time-based, not use-based. The candidate-selection half of the analogy is the weak half. (4) Endpoint: apoptotic contents are lysosomally degraded and recycled as anonymous nutrients — identity is truly erased; ETTZ's A-002 endpoint preserves identity in a verified snapshot. At the deepest level ETTZ implements 'archive-then-release-the-live-copy,' not death. Calling the pipeline apoptosis flatters it; it is closer to accessioning into cold storage with a demolition permit.

## Strongest objection
Against 'partial', the strongest objection is: the verdict should be 'have it', because the handoff IS enforced — nothing is disposed without the owner's explicit authorization, so every disposal already passes a verification gate; A-002 merely automates what the owner-gate already guarantees, and the protocol (tag, package, manifest, gated executor) is live and has run successfully twice.

## Verdict survives because
The objection conflates two different checks. The owner-gate verifies AUTHORIZATION (permission to die); Nagata's discriminator between apoptosis and secondary necrosis is COMPLETED CONTENT TRANSFER (engulfment finished, contents degraded inside the recipient) — permission is not custody. An owner can authorize disposal of a batch whose only copy exists nowhere else; the current pipeline would execute it, and on 2026-09-01 the first Time Machine copy was still at 26%, so the 2 batches already disposed ran exactly the engulfment-unverified path — in biological terms, deaths whose bodies no phagocyte is confirmed to have eaten. Second, ETTZ's own jurisprudence distinguishes specced law from enforced law: the claims layer is protected by a guard TEST that fails if a delete path appears, not by intention. A-002 is a spec with no verb, no confirming envelope, and no guard. By the estate's own standard, the mechanism is not present until the disposal verb refuses without a custodian envelope. So 'partial' stands: packaging, tagging, grace, and gated execution are present; the single property that separates orderly disposal from catastrophe is designed but not built.

## Missing pieces
- Enforced completed-handoff: the disposal verb does not block on a custodian confirming envelope proving a verified snapshot holds every file in the batch BY CONTENT HASH — A-002 is specced, not live; 2 batches were disposed without it while the first backup copy was still incomplete (26%)
- No guard test on the disposal path: claims/ has a class-level test that fails if a delete path appears; entropy has no equivalent 'no-dispose-without-custodian-envelope' guard, so the interlock could regress silently even after being built
- No snapshot pinning: restic retention could later prune the very snapshot a disposal envelope cited, making the acknowledgment retroactively false — secondary necrosis AFTER engulfment, a failure mode biology does not even have; disposal-referenced snapshots must be tagged immortal or the disposal envelope must trigger re-verification before any prune
- No standing necrosis detector: un-manifested disappearance (owner-delete-residue, corruption, dead refs) was handled ad hoc; there is no rule 'content that vanishes without a disposal envelope fires an alarm and auto-quarantines its dangling flow-refs and index entries with a .why.md' — the inflammation response exists but is not reflexive
- Death candidacy is time-only: the 30-day dwell clock is a TTL, whereas biological candidacy is competitive and use-gated (trophic factor, per-unit input activity); bank balances and spine-envelope usage could inform candidacy without changing the executor (overlaps with the microglia analysis, noted, not double-claimed)

## Adoptable proposal: Completed-Handoff Enforcement (A-002 as a hard gate with confirming envelope, pin, and guard)
- biological principle: Nagata 2018 (PMID:29400998): necrosis is not a different plan from apoptosis — it is apoptosis with an unacknowledged handoff; the completed, verified transfer of contents to the ingesting cell is the ONLY property separating silent orderly disposal from inflammatory systemic damage. Kerr 1972 (PMID:4561027): degradation happens inside the recipient, never in the open.
- ettz change: Three small pieces, all inside existing organs: (1) custodian grows one query verb, 'snapshot-holds?', taking the quarantine batch's content-hash list and answering only after verifying each hash exists in a verified restic snapshot, emitting a confirming envelope (snapshot id in refs) on success; (2) kallimachos's disposal verb refuses to run unless that confirming envelope exists for the exact batch — the trigger-fabric pattern the estate already uses to close every other loop, now applied to its one irreversible operation; the confirming snapshot is tagged do-not-prune (pinned) in the same act; (3) a guard test, modeled on the claims no-delete guard, that fails CI if any disposal path exists that does not require the custodian envelope. Optional fourth (the reflexive inflammation rule — vanished content without a disposal envelope fires an alarm and auto-quarantines dangling refs) is noted for owner review but is separable.
- cost: Build: small — one verb on custodian, one precondition check on the kallimachos disposal verb, one guard test, one restic tag operation; all patterns (verb doors, confirming envelopes, guard tests) already exist in the estate. Runtime: per-batch hash verification against the restic index (minutes per batch, cheap at current disposal cadence of ~2 batches/6 weeks); disposal now has a hard dependency on custodian reachability and backup currency, which is the point.
- risks: (a) Starvation, not deadlock avoidance: with backups incomplete (Time Machine at 26% on 2026-09-01) or the CUSTODIAN drive unmounted, disposal blocks indefinitely and quarantine grows unbounded — the correct response is a stale-handoff alarm, and the temptation to add a bypass flag must be resisted, because a bypass reintroduces exactly the unverified path this closes. (b) False acknowledgment: if custodian verifies by path+mtime rather than content hash, the snapshot may hold an OLDER version and disposal destroys the only copy of the current bytes — verification must reuse the estate's content-hash ids, nothing weaker. (c) Pin erosion: restic retention pruning a confirming snapshot silently invalidates a past acknowledgment; without the pin, the interlock is only true at the moment of handoff. (d) Where the biological principle would be WRONG for ETTZ: do not import apoptosis's autonomy or its endpoint — biology runs 10^11 unreviewed deaths/day and recycles contents into anonymous nutrients (true identity erasure inside the phagocyte); an autonomous disposal path or a dedup/compress step that destroys identity would each violate owner law. The handoff discipline transfers; the autonomy and the anonymizing digestion must not.
- owning session: custodian A-002 (owns the interlock verb and pinning), with the disposal-side precondition and guard test landing in kallimachos/entropy A-003 — no new session required; this is the completion of an already-planned interlock.
- priority argument: Ranks at or near the top of all proposals in the project. It is the cheapest (every needed pattern exists), it is already owner-specced (A-002), and it guards the estate's ONE irreversible operation — the only place a bug can permanently violate the absolute law 'never erase.' The literature is unusually direct here: the acknowledgment is not hygiene but the sole discriminator between orderly and catastrophic disposal, and the estate is currently running the unverified path (2 batches disposed, backup incomplete). Richer proposals (episodic-to-claim promotion, Hebbian ranking) create new capability; this one closes an open wound. Build-nothing-until-he-rules applies, but this should be first on the docket.

## Doctrine notes
Three points where this mapping touches owner law, all to be stated as doctrine, not failure. (1) 'Compress and archive, NEVER erase' vs apoptosis's true destruction: apoptosis lysosomally degrades and anonymously recycles contents; ETTZ deliberately substitutes archive-then-release — the A-002 snapshot preserves identity where biology destroys it. This is a purposeful divergence and the proposal must not import the digestion step (no dedup/compression that destroys identity of disposed content). (2) 'Owner authorization gates disposal' vs cell-autonomy and the caspase point-of-no-return: biology commits irreversibly and autonomously at scale; ETTZ deliberately keeps every death reversible until the owner rules and centralizes adjudication. The throughput cost (2 batches/6 weeks vs 10^11/day) is the price of doctrine and is accepted; any future 'autonomous disposal' proposal would violate this law and is dead on arrival. (3) 'Claims close windows, never delete': memory-record death in ETTZ is deliberately routed through accessibility switching, matching Ryan & Frankland 2022 (natural forgetting as accessible→inaccessible state change, reversible) and Bouton 2004 (superseding record shadows, old record survives) rather than apoptosis — so the apoptosis mapping is doctrinally scoped to the file/artifact layer (entropy's jurisdiction) and must not be extended to claims or procedures. Finally, per project constraint: everything above is for owner review; the A-002 completion is proposed, not built.
