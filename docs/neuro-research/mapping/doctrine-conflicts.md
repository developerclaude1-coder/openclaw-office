# doctrine-conflicts
MECHANISM: Biology's disposal and abstraction primitives all terminate in an irreversible, unpermissioned, unlogged DESTRUCTION or IN-PLACE REWRITE verb: complement-tagged microglial engulfment of synapses (Stevens 2007 PMID:18083105; Schafer 2012 PMID:22632727; Wang 2020 PMID:32029629), tonic dopamine→DAMB→Rac1→cofilin intrinsic decay running as a default-on erase daemon (Shuai 2010 PMID:20178749; Berry 2012 PMID:22578504; Davis & Zhong 2017 PMID:28772119), Syt3-driven AMPAR endocytosis as the strength-decrement effector (Awasthi 2019 PMID:30545844), apoptotic packaging-plus-lysosomal-degradation-inside-the-phagocyte (Kerr 1972 PMID:4561027; Nagata 2018 PMID:29400998), diphtheria-toxin ablation of allocated units as the only demonstrated true erasure (Han 2009 PMID:19286560), and retrieval-induced reconsolidation in which a read returns the record to a labile state that must be re-written to persist (Nader 2000 PMID:10963596).
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: The whole ETTZ disposal/abstraction surface, defined by refusal of that verb: append-only envelope spine (71,225 records, content-hash ids); claims with bi-temporal validity windows and CLOSE-THE-WINDOW-NEVER-DELETE plus a guard test that FAILS if a delete path appears in the class; _Quarantine batches with .why.md manifests as the only removal-shaped operation; kallimachos/entropy A-003 dwell clocks + grace verification + heal-before-harm; the planned custodian A-002 snapshot interlock ('does a verified snapshot hold this?' — 'no' blocks the delete); owner authorization as the gate on disposal; the why-ledger carrying a reason on every judgment, never deleted; procedures with NO CLOCK (worked→verified, failed→contested, superseded = permanent known-bad pointer); flow-ref repairs as append-only splices (question envelope → decision envelope), never rewrites; frozen store vocabulary.
VERDICT: deliberately different

## Argument for
THE DOCTRINE REGISTER — every point where biology and ETTZ disagree ON PURPOSE. Format per conflict: BIOLOGY'S LAW / ETTZ'S LAW / WHY THE OWNER'S LAW IS THAT WAY / WHAT EACH SIDE BUYS. The adopter rule for each is filed as the correspondingly numbered entry in missing_pieces.

C1. ERASURE vs WINDOWS-NEVER-GRAVES.
BIOLOGY: traces are physically removed. Rac1/cofilin depolymerises the actin holding a spine and is orthogonal to the write path — retention is write-strength minus a continuously running deletion pressure, not the absence of refresh (Shuai 2010; Davis & Zhong 2017). Syt3 reads Ca2+ and internalises GluA2, and Syt3-nulls learn normally but do not forget and their LTP does not decay (Awasthi 2019) — decay is an executed removal call, not a TTL.
ETTZ: a contradicted belief has its validity window closed; the superseded claim IS the record of how understanding changed; no delete path exists in the class and a guard test fails if one appears. Repairs to a flow ref are splices, not rewrites.
WHY: auditability of BELIEF CHANGE. A closed window plus a why-ledger entry answers 'what did the estate think on 2026-08-03 and what changed its mind' — a question a physically erased trace can never answer, and the question the owner is actually building for (the estate's taste becoming his).
BUYS: biology buys a bounded substrate and the ability to be wrong cheaply. ETTZ buys time-travel (--asof), non-repudiation, and the ability to audit its own reasoning drift; it pays in monotonic growth and the 60s+ cold fold.

C2. MICROGLIAL DESTRUCTION vs QUARANTINE.
BIOLOGY: engulfment is unrecoverable and the ingesting cell digests the material internally (Kerr 1972; Nagata 2018). CR3/C3 nulls still retain ~50% of engulfment — the pathway is REDUNDANT with no guard anywhere, and its errors are permanent (1.3x adult synapse density, segregation deficits that never correct; Schafer 2012).
ETTZ: nothing is deleted directly; suspect material moves to a _Quarantine batch with a .why.md manifest naming the batch's reason (owner-delete-residue, music-review-rejects). Reversible by construction, documented by construction.
WHY: reversibility under uncertainty. Quarantine is a bet that the classifier is fallible; the manifest is the mechanism by which a wrong classification can be found and undone later by someone who was not present at the decision.
BUYS: biology buys reclaimed volume and metabolic budget immediately. ETTZ buys recourse; it pays with a second estate (quarantine) that itself accumulates and must be curated, and with the fact that a quarantined item is still discoverable, so quarantine is NOT a containment primitive for anything that must actually stop being readable.

C3. AUTONOMOUS PRUNING vs OWNER-GATED DISPOSAL.
BIOLOGY: no cell asks permission and there is no operator. ~10^10–10^11 cells are cleared daily (Nagata 2018). What checking exists is a local, expiring, act-site brake: neuronal CD47 engages microglial SIRPα, an ITIM receptor that aborts the phagocytic cup at the moment of commitment (Lehrman 2018 PMID:30308165). Competence is time-boxed rather than permissioned — engulfment is high at P5 and collapses by P9 while phagocytic CAPABILITY persists, and Hong 2016 (PMID:27033548) shows what the same machinery does when it runs unwindowed in adulthood: pathological synapse loss.
ETTZ: owner authorization gates disposal, by law. Two batches disposed in six weeks. The custodian A-002 interlock is planned as a second, act-site gate.
WHY: accountability. Disposal is the one operation with no undo, so the estate places it with the only party who can be accountable for it. Note the structural agreement hidden inside the disagreement: ETTZ's interlock and biology's CD47/SIRPα are BOTH act-site vetoes rather than decision-site ones — biology independently discovered that the last check must sit at the commitment point, not at the plan.
BUYS: biology buys throughput and latency. ETTZ buys accountability and pays with a human bottleneck whose failure mode is not data loss but an unreviewed backlog.

C4. FORGETTING-TO-GENERALISE vs TOTAL RECALL.
BIOLOGY: abstraction is the RESIDUE of deletion, causally. Blocking GluA2/AMPAR removal during the retention interval preserved specific memories AND abolished contextual fear generalization and spontaneous recovery of extinguished fear (Migues 2016 PMID:27013677); Richards & Frankland 2017 (PMID:28641107) formalise transience as regularization — memory's objective is decision quality under drift, not fidelity. Adding storage is itself destructive: raising neurogenesis after encoding induces forgetting in proportion to insertion rate (Akers 2014 PMID:24812394).
ETTZ: every envelope is kept forever; the fold is a recomputation, not a compaction, and nothing currently PROMOTES repeated episodic patterns into claims or procedures at all.
WHY: the why-ledger. Generalisation-by-deletion destroys the evidence for the generalisation; ETTZ's whole value proposition is that a belief can be walked back to the episodes that produced it.
BUYS: biology buys cheap abstraction and bounded storage; ETTZ buys provenance and pays by having to build an abstraction mechanism it does not yet have. THE LEGAL ROUTE EXISTS AND MUST BE STATED AS DOCTRINE, NOT DISCOVERED BY AN IMPLEMENTER: Ryan & Frankland 2022 (PMID:35027710) show natural forgetting is a reversible accessible→inaccessible switch; Ryan 2015 (PMID:26023136) separates connectivity (identity, survives amnesia) from weights (addressability, removed by amnesia); Teyler & Rudy 2007 (PMID:17696170) separate a compact index from bulk content and age them independently. So ETTZ may demote RETRIEVABILITY while keeping CONTENT. But this is the sharpest place where a biological principle can silently override owner law, because an index eviction that is not itself recorded is erasure with extra steps.

C5. DISTRIBUTED PATTERN vs ADDRESSABLE RECORD.
BIOLOGY: there is no record and no address. Content IS the pattern; recall is pattern completion from a partial cue via CA3 recurrence (Nakazawa 2002 PMID:12040087), and forcing the ensemble optogenetically in a neutral context reproduces the memory with no cue present (Liu 2012 PMID:22441246). The address is (cell set × input pathway), not a key (Abdou 2018 PMID:29903972). A single memory is written across ~117 of 247 regions (Roy 2022 PMID:35379803), so no unit holds it and destroying any unit degrades nothing. And the code drifts: the neurons representing a memory turn over across days while behaviour is stable (Ziv 2013 PMID:23396101).
ETTZ: content-hash ids, one JSON record per event, exact addressing, exact-fingerprint fold cache, flow refs as minted-opaque identity threads.
WHY: an auditor must be able to name the thing. 'The estate believed X' has to resolve to a citable record with an id, or the why-ledger is rhetoric.
BUYS: biology buys graceful degradation, cheap association, and generalisation for free from the representation itself. ETTZ buys exact citation and reproducibility, and pays by having no similarity retrieval at all and no read-time arbitration — nothing in ETTZ corresponds to divisive normalization (Carandini & Heeger 2012 PMID:22108672), the fast competition that decides which of several matching items wins.

C6. USE-IT-OR-LOSE-IT vs NO-CLOCK PROCEDURES.
BIOLOGY IS SPLIT HERE AND THE SPLIT IS THE POINT. The intrinsic-decay family says disuse kills: tonic dopaminergic drive runs an always-on erase daemon whose rate is set by Rac1 level, independent of whether the memory was ever used (Berry 2012; Liu 2016 PMID:27593377). The depression family says the opposite: LTD requires 900 pulses at 1–3 Hz — ACTIVITY, not silence (Dudek & Bear 1992 PMID:1350090) — and weakening is graded withdrawal of a recyclable resource with the structure intact (Malinow & Malenka 2002 PMID:12052905).
ETTZ: a procedure never decays from disuse, only from FAILING when tried; superseded procedures become permanent known-bad pointers.
WHY: a procedure's truth is a property of the world, not of attention. An unused backup-restore procedure is not less true for being unused, and demoting it by clock would punish exactly the rare, high-stakes knowledge the estate exists to hold.
BUYS: biology buys capacity. ETTZ buys reliability for cold-path knowledge and pays with UNEARNED CONFIDENCE: a procedure last verified in July and never tried since carries the same 'verified' status as one that worked yesterday, and the estate cannot tell them apart. That is a real cost of the doctrine and belongs in the ignorance graph, not in a decay clock.

C7. DEFAULT-PERMIT-WITH-EXPIRING-VETO vs DEFAULT-DENY-WITH-AFFIRMATIVE-AUTHORIZATION. (added)
BIOLOGY: deletion is the default and is blocked by an active, expiring exemption. Lehrman 2018 is decisive: CD47-null microglia do not merely over-eat, they lose activity preference ENTIRELY — so use-dependence is carried by the BRAKE, not by the eat-me tag. Being used renews an exemption; being silent lets an exemption lapse.
ETTZ: disposal is default-denied; the guard test enforces the denial in code; authorization is an affirmative act.
WHY: this is the polarity that makes the estate safe to leave unattended. Under biology's polarity, a broken usage-metrics pipeline queues the whole estate for disposal; under ETTZ's, a broken pipeline queues nothing.
BUYS: biology's polarity buys automatic capacity management with zero standing decisions. ETTZ's buys fail-safe behaviour under partial failure — the same instinct as the BEATS rule that 'silence is the one thing a heartbeat may never produce'. Silence is a fault signal in ETTZ; in biology it is evidence for disposal. These cannot both be true in one system.

C8. RECONSOLIDATION (READ MUTATES) vs APPEND-ONLY SPLICE. (added — the newest and least-guarded conflict)
BIOLOGY: a successful retrieval destabilises the stored item, which must be re-committed to persist; blocking protein synthesis inside that window erases the memory while the same block outside it does nothing (Nader 2000). Prediction error gates it: a read that confirms leaves the record alone, a read that contradicts opens it for edit (Sevenster 2013 PMID:23413355). Retrieval is also a write on NEIGHBOURS, suppressing strong competitors specifically (Anderson 1994 PMID:7931095).
ETTZ: reads are idempotent; the estate is append-only at its core; a repair is a splice — a suspected identity-join is a question envelope, a confirmed one a decision envelope.
WHY: content-hash ids are only meaningful if the content behind them never changes. In-place update destroys the id contract and with it every ref and flow ref that points at the record.
BUYS: biology buys update-on-access and a principled moment at which a record may be corrected. ETTZ buys immutability and referential integrity. The prediction-error GATE is the transferable half and is doctrinally clean; the LABILE REWRITE is not, and the two arrive in the literature welded together, which is exactly how a principle overrides a law by accident.

C9. SPARSE, COMPETITIVE, THRESHOLDED WRITE vs TOTAL CAPTURE. (added)
BIOLOGY: the substrate never writes everything. Allocation is winner-take-most on RELATIVE intrinsic excitability in the moments before encoding (Han 2007 PMID:17446403; Yiu 2014 PMID:25102562), sparsity is fixed by lateral inhibition (~2–5% of dentate granule cells) independent of how much is happening, and the IEG tag only latches inside an open 24–48 h window (Reijmers 2007 PMID:17761885).
ETTZ: every event, action, decision and observation is one envelope with a mandatory why. 71,225 and monotonically rising.
WHY: an audit trail with a sampling threshold is not an audit trail. The mandatory `why` is worth more than the storage it costs.
BUYS: biology buys capacity protection and, as a side effect, a built-in salience judgement. ETTZ buys completeness and pays the whole bill at fold time — the 60s+ cold fold is precisely the cost of refusing sparsity at capture. CONSEQUENCE FOR ADOPTERS: every biological threshold was tuned against a scarce substrate. None of those numbers transfer. Sparsity may be imported at the index/attention layer only.

C10. INSTRUCTION-FREE TAGS AND A DUMB EXECUTOR vs A WHY ON EVERY MARK. (added)
BIOLOGY: the opsonin iC3b is a covalent, item-local mark that says only 'flagged' and carries no reason; policy lives entirely in the labelling and the destroyer is dumb — engram-cell-specific CD55 alone blocks forgetting (Wang 2020). Tag families are context-specific and plural (CX3CL1/ADAM10/CX3CR1 for whisker-lesion cortical elimination, explicitly NOT CR3; Gunner 2019 PMID:31209379).
ETTZ: every judgment carries its reason, never deleted.
WHY: this is the one place ETTZ is strictly RICHER than biology, and the doctrine should be stated as a positive requirement so that importing the elegant mark/execute separation does not import the reasonlessness with it.
BUYS: biology buys a tiny, fast, composable mark. ETTZ buys a mark that can be argued with six months later.

C11. REPRESENTATIONAL DRIFT vs FROZEN VOCABULARY. (added)
BIOLOGY: the code moves under the function; stored keys go stale relative to the encoder (Ziv 2013).
ETTZ: the four memory sub-layers are a frozen vocabulary, all homed; ids are content hashes.
WHY: a graph you can still read in two years. Drift is what makes long-horizon audit impossible.
BUYS: biology buys continuous re-encoding without migration. ETTZ buys stable retrieval keys and pays by having no mechanism at all for the meaning of a frozen term shifting — if 'verified' comes to mean something else operationally, no id changes and nothing flags it.

WHERE BIOLOGY AND ETTZ ALREADY AGREE (recorded so the register is a test, not a defence). (a) Non-destruction is biology's DEFAULT too: natural forgetting is a reversible accessibility switch (Ryan & Frankland 2022), the silent engram is stored-but-unaddressable with connectivity intact (Ryan 2015), extinction is a competing context-keyed record with the old one surviving — proven by renewal, spontaneous recovery and reinstatement (Bouton 2004 PMID:15466298), and LTD recycles rather than destroys. Close-the-window-never-delete is not an eccentricity; it is the biological default with a manifest attached. (b) Mandatory acknowledged handoff: apoptosis without completed engulfment collapses into secondary necrosis and DAMP release — necrosis is not a different plan, it is the same plan with an unacknowledged handoff (Nagata 2018). That is an argument that the custodian A-002 interlock must be MANDATORY, not planned, and that disposal should be gated on a confirming envelope from the receiving store exactly as the trigger fabric closes every other loop. (c) Compartment-scoped demolition: caspase-3 runs sub-lethally in dendrites for LTD, bounded by XIAP/Bcl-xL and a short window, and the cell survives (Li 2010 PMID:20510932) — the precedent for owner-authorization gates as explicit inhibitor levels setting blast radius. (d) Act-site veto (C3 above).

AN INTERNAL INCONSISTENCY THE OWNER SHOULD RULE ON. Files can be disposed of under owner authorization (entropy has done two batches). Claims cannot be disposed of at all, ever, by construction — the guard test forbids the path. So the estate has two incompatible disposal doctrines depending on which store the material landed in, and the boundary is decided by ingestion routing rather than by policy. If a secret, a credential, or third-party personal data reaches a claim's text or a why-ledger entry, the doctrine currently has NO remedy — quarantine is not containment, since quarantined material remains readable, and there is no destruction verb in the class. This is not an argument to weaken the law; it is an argument that the law needs a named, owner-only, single-item exception path with its own manifest, so that the exception is exercised deliberately rather than by someone quietly adding a delete method and deleting the guard test with it.

## Argument against
The register overstates the amount of genuine disagreement, and a doctrine analyst who inflates conflicts is more dangerous than one who misses them.

First, on the headline conflict (C1), biology mostly agrees with the owner. Ryan & Frankland 2022 argue all natural forgetting is a reversible accessible→inaccessible state switch rather than trace deletion; Ryan 2015 shows engram connectivity — the identity — surviving retrograde amnesia intact while only weights/addressability are lost; Bouton 2004 shows extinction leaving the original record intact and merely out-competed by a newer context-keyed one; LTD withdraws a recyclable receptor pool without touching the structure. The only demonstrated true erasure in the whole engram literature is destroying the allocated units with diphtheria toxin (Han 2009), which is a substrate-level operation with no software analogue. On this reading, ETTZ has not diverged from biology at all — it has implemented biology's default and named it a law. The right verdict for C1 alone might be 'have it'.

Second, several 'conflicts' are category errors between a compute substrate and a storage substrate. C5 (distributed pattern vs addressable record) is not a doctrinal choice at all; biology has no addressing because it has no addresses, not because it decided against them. Calling that a deliberate divergence dresses up an inevitability as a decision.

Third, the doctrine is untested. Every biological law in this register was selected under a hard, non-negotiable volume and energy budget. ETTZ's law has never met that budget: 71,225 envelopes over six weeks is a trivial corpus, and 'never erase' is cheap at that scale. The one place the constraint has already bitten — the 60s+ cold fold — is exactly the place the doctrine is producing pain, and the estate's answer so far is a fingerprint cache, i.e. deferral. A law that has never been forced to choose is a default, not a doctrine, and labelling it 'deliberately different' grants it a standing it has not earned.

Fourth, C6 is not a clean divergence either. Biology's LTD family already implements the exact rule ETTZ chose — weakening requires activity, not silence — so 'no-clock procedures' has a biological warrant and is not a divergence from biology so much as a choice between two biological families.

Fifth, the register risks becoming an instrument of refusal. If every future proposal must pass eleven doctrinal tests, the practical effect is that nothing is adopted, the ignorance graph stops growing, and the estate's real deficits (no abstraction mechanism, no read-time arbitration, no similarity retrieval) go unaddressed while the doctrine is polished.

## Strongest objection
The verdict should be 'have it', not 'deliberately different'. Biology's default disposal path is already non-destructive and reversible — accessibility switching (Ryan & Frankland 2022), silent engrams with intact connectivity (Ryan 2015), extinction as a surviving competing record (Bouton 2004), LTD as recyclable withdrawal — and physical destruction is the exception requiring a specialised executor. ETTZ's close-the-window-never-delete, quarantine-with-manifest and append-only splice therefore reproduce biology's ordinary behaviour rather than diverging from it. Naming this a deliberate divergence manufactures a disagreement in order to erect a review gate around it, and it does so on the strength of a law that has never been stress-tested at a scale where keeping everything actually hurts.

## Verdict survives because
The objection is correct about OUTCOMES and wrong about VERBS, and the register is about verbs.

Where biology is non-destructive, it is non-destructive by physics, not by policy: nothing in a mouse decided that engram silencing should be reversible, and nothing in a mouse can be held to it. Where biology needs a thing gone, it goes — engulfment digests the material inside the phagocyte, apoptosis ends in lysosomal degradation, diphtheria ablation is permanent, and the errors are uncorrectable four days later (Schafer 2012's CR3/C3 nulls carry their deficit into adulthood; the window never reopens). Crucially there is no guard anywhere: the pruning pathway is REDUNDANT, so knocking out C3 or CR3 removes only ~50% of engulfment. ETTZ has the one artifact biology lacks entirely — a test that fails the build if a delete path appears in the claims class. That is an affirmative, code-level, enforced commitment, not the absence of a capability. 'Have it' cannot describe a property that biology does not possess and could not possess.

The verdict also survives on polarity, which the objection does not touch. C7 is a strict inversion, not a resemblance: biology deletes by default and is stopped by an expiring exemption (CD47-null microglia lose activity preference entirely — Lehrman 2018), while ETTZ denies by default and requires an affirmative act. An adopter importing the CD47 pattern would be importing correct biology and inverting owner law in the same commit, and the resulting system would read a broken metrics pipeline as evidence for disposal — the precise opposite of the estate's own BEATS rule that silence is never a valid output. Likewise C8: reconsolidation's prediction-error gate and its labile in-place rewrite arrive welded together in the literature, and the rewrite half breaks the content-hash id contract outright.

On the untestedness charge: that is a risk to log, not grounds to reclassify. The verdict states what the law IS and what an adopter must not do with it; whether the law should be revised under scale pressure is an owner ruling, and the doctrine guard below is precisely the mechanism that forces such a revision to be made explicitly rather than by an implementer who needed the fold to run faster. On the refusal charge: the proposal is deliberately scoped to proposals that ADD A VERB, so the abstraction, arbitration and retrieval deficits the objection rightly names are untouched by it — index demotion is the one that touches it, and that is the one case (C4) where an unrecorded demotion really would be erasure with extra steps. Finally, I concede C5 and C6 as weak: C5 is closer to an inevitability than a doctrine, and C6 is a choice between two biological families rather than a divergence from biology. They stay in the register as adopter hazards, downgraded in status; removing them does not disturb C1, C2, C3, C4, C7, C8, C9, C10 or C11.

## Missing pieces
- C1 (erasure vs windows): you may import the DECAY SIGNAL and the DECAY SCHEDULE — a rate, a threshold, an orthogonal always-on pressure — but never the removal verb. Every biological decay mechanism must land as a state transition on a retained record (window closed, status demoted, rank lowered) plus a why-ledger entry naming the pressure that caused it. If a proposal cannot state what the record looks like AFTER the decay fires, it has imported the verb.
- C2 (engulfment vs quarantine): you may import the TAGGING half in full — item-local marks, upstream self-declared weakness signals, plural context-specific tag families, a single scoped reader — but the executor is always quarantine-with-manifest, never digestion. Wang 2020 licenses this: the decision point is the tag, not the phagocyte, so nothing computational is lost by swapping the executor. Corollary: a quarantine batch must remain enumerable and reversible, and the .why.md must name the tag and its threshold, not just the batch.
- C3 (autonomous vs owner-gated): autonomy is granted per-verb by reversibility, never per-agent. An automated agent may PROPOSE, may TAG, and may execute reversible acts (quarantine, demotion, de-caching, rank change); it may never execute an irreversible one, and the owner's authorization must attach to the SELECTION SET, not merely to the run — authorizing a batch is not authorizing whatever a surveyor put in it. Import biology's act-site veto placement (CD47/SIRPa aborts at the commitment point) by making the custodian A-002 interlock mandatory and gating disposal on a confirming envelope from the receiving store, as the trigger fabric closes every other loop.
- C4 (generalise-by-forgetting vs total recall): abstraction may be paid for in RETRIEVABILITY, never in bytes, and the demotion must itself be a first-class record. A promotion step that folds N episodes into a claim must emit (a) the claim or procedure, (b) the explicit list of demoted episode ids, (c) the why, and (d) a reversal path — an index-level demotion that does not enumerate what it demoted is erasure with extra steps and must fail the doctrine check even though no delete verb appears in it. Use the index-vs-content split (Teyler & Rudy 2007) as the licensed shape: age the index, never the content.
- C5 (pattern vs record) [downgraded to hazard, not a doctrine]: similarity and pattern-completion retrieval may be added as an additional read path, but every result it returns must resolve to a content-hash-addressed record with a why. Never let a reconstructed or scored answer stand as the estate's belief without an id behind it, and expose the two-path read explicitly (cue-driven and id-addressed) so 'stored but unreachable' is distinguishable from 'absent'.
- C6 (use-it-or-lose-it vs no-clock) [downgraded: a choice between two biological families, not a divergence]: no clock may ever demote a procedure. The computational value of use-dependence enters as EVIDENCE AGE, not as decay: annotate each procedure with last-verified-at and surface stale-but-unfalsified procedures as ignorance-graph items so the owner can choose to re-verify. Confidence may be reported as unearned; status may only change on a trial outcome.
- C7 (default-permit vs default-deny): the polarity is not importable under any framing. You may use usage/recency as a veto-renewal signal that SUPPRESSES a disposal proposal, but absence of that signal must never by itself generate one — silence is a fault indication in this estate, not evidence. Any rule of the form 'if not used for N, then queue for disposal' is dead on arrival; the legal form is 'if proposed for disposal, then recent use blocks the proposal'.
- C8 (reconsolidation vs append-only): import the prediction-error GATE, never the labile rewrite. A read that confirms a claim leaves it untouched; a read that contradicts it emits a question envelope, and a resolved contradiction emits a decision envelope that closes the old window and opens a new one. No retrieval may ever mutate the record it returned, and no retrieval-induced suppression of competitors (Anderson 1994) may be implemented as a change to the competitor's stored content — only to a separate, recorded ranking layer.
- C9 (sparsity vs total capture): no biological threshold, sparsity fraction or window length transfers numerically — all of them were tuned against a scarce substrate that ETTZ does not have. Any imported threshold must be re-derived from ETTZ's own envelope statistics and recorded as a metaplastic parameter in the why-ledger (an auditable threshold-change judgment, per Abraham & Bear 1996), and sparsity may be applied only at the index, ranking or attention layer — never at capture. Capture stays total.
- C10 (instruction-free tags vs the why-ledger): every mark an imported mechanism writes must carry a why, a timestamp, an author organ and its threshold, even where biology's equivalent carries none. A bare boolean flag on an item is a doctrine violation regardless of how faithful it is to the opsonin it models.
- C11 (drift vs frozen vocabulary): imported mechanisms may not introduce a second, mutable naming layer. If a mechanism needs learned or embedded keys, those keys are a derived cache keyed by content hash, rebuildable and never authoritative; the frozen four-layer vocabulary and content-hash ids remain the only addresses of record. Any semantic drift in a frozen term must be raised as an ignorance-graph question, not absorbed silently.
- C0 (meta-rule, applies to all): the register is a checklist for PROPOSALS, not a veto on capability. A proposal that conflicts with a law is not rejected — it is escalated to the owner as a named law-change request with the conflict stated in these terms. The failure mode the register exists to prevent is silent override, not adoption.

## Adoptable proposal: The Doctrine Gate: a mandatory `doctrine` block on verb-introducing proposals, enforced by generalising the existing claims delete-path guard into a per-class VERB LEDGER
- biological principle: Two-stage separation of MARK from EXECUTE with a scoped reader and an act-site inhibitory checkpoint. Complement tagging deposits an item-local mark (C1q -> C3 -> iC3b) that carries no instruction, and a single scoped reader (CR3) executes hours-to-days later, with neuronal CD47 engaging microglial SIRPa to abort the phagocytic cup at the moment of commitment (Stevens 2007 PMID:18083105; Schafer 2012 PMID:22632727; Lehrman 2018 PMID:30308165). Wang 2020 (PMID:32029629) proves where policy lives: engram-cell-specific CD55 alone blocks forgetting, so the destroyer is dumb and the labelling is the decision. Nagata 2018 (PMID:29400998) supplies the enforcement principle — apoptosis without a completed engulfment handoff becomes secondary necrosis; necrosis is the same plan with an unacknowledged acknowledgment. The transferable value is that a system's destructive competence is defined by WHICH READER HOLDS THE VERB, and that the last check belongs at the commitment point, not at the plan.
- ettz change: Two small additions, both extensions of things that already exist. (1) PROPOSAL-TIME: add an optional-but-required-when-triggered `doctrine` block to the proposal envelope shape. It is triggered whenever a proposal introduces or modifies any verb that disposes, demotes, decays, re-indexes, or rewrites. The block is eleven short fields, one per register entry, each answering in literal terms: which verb does this add; is it reversible and by what path; where is its manifest; who authorizes it and does the authorization attach to the selection set or only to the run; does it touch the claims class; does it change retrievability, and if so does it enumerate what it demoted; is its polarity default-deny; does it mutate on read; are its thresholds re-derived from ETTZ statistics rather than copied; does every mark it writes carry a why; does it introduce a second naming layer. A proposal that answers 'no' to a law does not fail — it is routed to the owner as a named law-change request. (2) BUILD-TIME: generalise the single existing guard test (the one that fails if a delete path appears in the claims class) into a per-class verb allowlist checked in CI across the spine's classes. Adding a verb to the allowlist requires an owner-signed decision envelope whose refs point at the `doctrine` block that justified it. Nothing else changes; no new organ, no runtime component, no schema break to the envelope itself (the block is one optional object).
- cost: BUILD: small and mostly documentation. One optional object in the proposal envelope shape; one markdown register of the eleven laws (this analysis, edited down); generalising one existing test from a single hardcoded class check to a table-driven allowlist across the spine's classes — the test already exists and already knows how to fail on a delete path, so this is widening its input, not writing a new checker. Estimate: days, not weeks, and it can be split (register first, schema field second, CI generalisation third). RUNTIME: effectively zero — the block is inert data on an envelope, and the allowlist runs only in CI. THE REAL COST IS HUMAN AND ONGOING: one owner review step per verb-introducing proposal, plus the allowlist must be maintained as dependencies change. Secondary cost: proposal friction, which will be felt most by exactly the small good proposals (a rank tweak, a cache eviction) that the gate is not really aimed at — so the trigger condition needs to stay narrow or the gate will be routed around.
- risks: 1. CHECKBOX THEATRE — the highest-probability failure. memory-nightly promotes 40 episodes into a claim and drops them from the fold's render index. The doctrine block says reversible: yes, because the envelopes are still on disk and the why-ledger has the promotion. But nothing records WHICH 40 episodes were demoted, so the demotion cannot be enumerated, audited or undone. The guard passes because no delete verb appears anywhere, and the estate has performed an unrecorded erasure of retrievability with a compliance certificate attached. This is why the C4 rule demands an explicit demoted-id list, and it is the single thing the gate must catch to be worth building.
2. NAME-MATCHING GIVES FALSE ASSURANCE — the allowlist enumerates verbs by name (delete, unlink, rm, truncate, drop) and cannot see destruction arriving through a dependency: a `restic forget --prune` retention policy on the CUSTODIAN drive, an object-store lifecycle rule, a `git gc` on a spine repo, an ORM cascade, a log rotation on an organ's outbox before the Collector's pull. The restic case is the sharpest: the custodian A-002 interlock asks 'does a verified snapshot hold this?' and the custodian's own retention policy is capable of destroying the snapshot that answered yes. A verb ledger that does not cover retention policies certifies an interlock whose evidence base can expire underneath it.
3. THE GATE BECOMES A DAM — with owner review required per proposal and, separately, per disposal, the disposal queue grows without bound (two batches in six weeks is already the observed rate). The failure mode is not data loss; it is a permanently expanding _Quarantine second estate whose .why.md manifests accumulate faster than anyone reads them, so the auditability the doctrine exists to protect is destroyed by volume rather than by deletion. Mitigation to propose alongside: a disposal DOCKET that batches owner decisions with the selection set visible, so authorization scales without becoming per-item.
4. WHERE THE BIOLOGICAL PRINCIPLE IS WRONG FOR ETTZ. (a) Biology puts ALL policy in the tag and makes the executor dumb (Wang 2020). Ported literally, an automated surveyor writes the marks, the owner authorizes only the batch run, and the doctrine is formally satisfied while the actual disposal decision has been fully delegated to software — the gate would have blessed the inversion. Hence the C3 rule that authorization attaches to the selection set. (b) Biology's checkpoint is default-permit with an expiring veto (Lehrman 2018): if the gate's own trigger condition is implemented as an exemption that must be renewed, a lapsed or misconfigured registry entry silently unguards a class. The gate must be default-deny — an unknown verb in an unknown class fails the build. (c) Biology's competence windows expire and never reopen (Schafer 2012: high at P5, collapsed by P9; CR3/C3 nulls carry the deficit into adulthood), and Hong 2016 (PMID:27033548) shows the same machinery running unwindowed in adulthood produces pathological loss. A doctrine gate that quietly expires would be worse than none; conversely any DISPOSAL competence the owner grants should be time-boxed and expiring, never ambient.
5. THE GATE CANNOT SEE THE ONE HOLE IT MOST NEEDS TO — the claims class has no disposal path at all, so a secret or third-party personal datum that reaches a claim's text has no lawful remedy, and the pressure to 'just add a delete method and update the guard test' will arrive in a hurry, out of hours, from a legitimate need. The gate should ship WITH a named, owner-only, single-item exception path and its own manifest, or the first genuine incident will be resolved by deleting the guard test itself.
- owning session: ORGANON — it already owns conformance levels (participant -> organ -> system) and JOINING.md, so the doctrine block belongs as a conformance clause ('a component may not reach organ conformance while it declares a verb absent from its class allowlist'), and the register is a standards document, which is what ORGANON is for. The executable half — the generalised verb-ledger test — should live where the existing claims delete-path guard already lives, in memory-spine, because widening an existing test is cheaper and less forgettable than creating a new checker elsewhere. custodian A-002 owns the runtime half (the snapshot interlock as the act-site veto, plus the retention-policy coverage named in risk 2). No new session required.
- priority argument: Ranks FIRST among proposals that get adopted, and LAST among proposals that get evaluated — the distinction matters. It delivers no capability: it does not close the abstraction gap, does not add read-time arbitration, does not make the cold fold faster, and if the owner adopts nothing else it guards nothing. So it should not consume review attention ahead of the substantive proposals in this project. But every substantive proposal in this project adds a verb — promotion demotes an index, use-dependent pruning adds a disposal trigger, Hebbian ranking adds a demotion, an exploratory surveyor adds a mark — and each arrives with a biological principle whose destruction or in-place-rewrite verb is welded to the part worth having. The gate is the only proposal whose absence makes the others unsafe to adopt rather than merely unbuilt, and it is by a wide margin the cheapest (one schema field, one widened test, one document). Sequencing rule: the owner rules on the register and the substantive proposals together; the gate must land before the first verb-introducing proposal is merged, not before any of them is read. Against its own priority: it is also the proposal most likely to become ceremony, and if the owner intends to review every adoption personally at the estate's current scale, he IS the gate, and the honest version of this proposal shrinks to just the eleven-law register as a document he reads before ruling — which is still worth writing.

## Doctrine notes
Every entry in this analysis touches owner law by construction; these are the points where the law is at genuine RISK rather than merely engaged.

1. THE INDEX-DEMOTION LOOPHOLE (C4) is the sharpest. Biology's licensed route to abstraction — demote retrievability, keep the trace (Ryan & Frankland 2022; Ryan 2015; Teyler & Rudy 2007) — is doctrinally legal only if the demotion is itself a recorded, enumerable, reversible act. An implementer chasing the cold-fold problem can drop episodes from the fold's render index, keep every byte, truthfully report 'nothing was deleted', and have performed an unauditable erasure of access. The owner should rule explicitly on whether RETRIEVABILITY is covered by 'never erase'. My reading of his intent is that it is, because the why-ledger's value is the ability to walk a belief back to its episodes, and an unenumerated demotion breaks exactly that walk.

2. POLARITY IS LOAD-BEARING AND EASY TO INVERT ACCIDENTALLY (C7). The most attractive result in the pruning literature — usage renews a don't-eat-me exemption rather than marking for keeping — is correct biology and, imported with its polarity intact, would make silence evidence for disposal. That contradicts the estate's own BEATS doctrine ('silence is the one thing a heartbeat may never produce') in the memory layer while faithfully implementing a cited mechanism. Any use-dependence proposal must be read for polarity before it is read for merit.

3. THE ACT-SITE VETO SHOULD BE PROMOTED FROM PLANNED TO MANDATORY. Nagata 2018's secondary necrosis is a direct argument: disposal without a completed, acknowledged handoff is not a lesser version of orderly disposal, it is the catastrophic mode. The custodian A-002 interlock is structurally the CD47/SIRPa checkpoint and the estate's trigger fabric already has the right idiom (a confirming envelope closes each loop). Gating disposal on a confirming envelope from the receiving store is doctrine-consistent and uses existing machinery.

4. THE TWO-DOCTRINE INCONSISTENCY IS REAL AND UNADDRESSED. Files may be disposed of under owner authorization; claims may never be, by a guard test. The boundary is set by ingestion routing, not by policy. The remedy is not to weaken the guard but to add a named, owner-only, single-item exception with its own manifest — otherwise the first legitimate destruction obligation (a leaked credential in a claim's text, third-party personal data in a why-ledger entry) will be met by deleting the guard test under time pressure, which converts a bounded exception into an unbounded one.

5. AUTHORIZATION MUST ATTACH TO THE SELECTION SET, NOT THE RUN. Wang 2020 establishes that policy lives in the labelling. If ETTZ automates the labelling and the owner authorizes only execution, owner-gated disposal has been formally preserved and substantively abolished. This is the way owner law is most likely to be overridden without anyone noticing they did it.

6. THRESHOLDS ARE JUDGMENTS AND BELONG IN THE WHY-LEDGER. Every number in the biological literature was tuned against a scarce substrate. Importing any of them as a constant is an unrecorded judgment; metaplasticity (Abraham & Bear 1996 PMID:8658594) supplies the right framing — store and adapt the RULES' thresholds as auditable state, separate from the data they act on.

7. TWO REGISTER ENTRIES ARE DOWNGRADED AND SHOULD NOT BE CITED AS DOCTRINE. C5 (distributed pattern vs addressable record) is closer to an inevitability of substrate than a decision. C6 (no-clock procedures) is a choice between two biological families — the intrinsic-decay family disagrees with ETTZ, the LTD family agrees with it — so the owner's rule has a biological warrant and should be defended on that ground rather than as a divergence. Both remain useful as adopter hazards; neither should be used to reject a proposal.

8. EVIDENCE HYGIENE CARRIED FROM THE CRITIC. Nothing here rests on PKMzeta maintenance (contested by Volk 2013 PMID:23283174), on Drescher 1995's misattributed relative-threshold claim, or on the Ramirez 2013 / Zhou 2009 results named but uncited in the sweep. Weinhard 2018's two-pathway separation of trogocytosis from complement engulfment rests on a null result and is used here only for the filopodia-induction observation (39% vs 7%), corroborated in vivo by Miyamoto 2016 (PMID:27558646); the 'partial edit as a first-class operation distinct from removal' idea is therefore offered as suggestive, not as established architecture, and no doctrine entry depends on it.
