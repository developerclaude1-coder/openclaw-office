# reverse-gap-hunt
MECHANISM: Reverse-gap sweep across all twelve biology areas: brain mechanisms with no ETTZ analog (A-side) vs ETTZ mechanisms with no brain analog (B-side)
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: NONE as mechanisms. Scattered raw material exists for several A-side items — the ~anomalous trial outcome (a prediction-error datum that gates nothing), CHOICE envelopes (a counterfactual record that demotes nothing), stale-fabric alarms (an alert, not backpressure), entropy dwell clocks (ambient, never-expiring pruning authority), bank balances (monotone counters with no renormalization) — but in no case does an ETTZ organ carry the computation the biological mechanism performs. The B-side is the inverse: ETTZ mechanisms (why-ledger, ignorance graph, bi-temporal claims, content-hash identity, append-only archive with guard test, owner adjudication, CHOICE envelopes) for which biology has no equivalent.
VERDICT: missing

## Argument for
The verdict 'missing' is for the A-side aggregate; the B-side is the required companion finding — a list of ETTZ mechanisms with NO brain analog, each argued as an advantage to PROTECT from naive bio-mimicry, not a gap to fix. (1) THE WHY-LEDGER. No neuron records its reason: growth-cone valence is computed at the receiver from (ligand, receptor set, cAMP state) and vanishes when the state changes (Song 1998, PMID 9727979) — biology's judgments are physically embodied and uninspectable. ETTZ makes every judgment an auditable, append-only object. Protect: any bio-mimetic proposal that replaces recorded reasons with implicit state (e.g., 'just use the balance as the weight') destroys the estate's single most non-biological asset — that its taste is legible and correctable by the owner. (2) THE IGNORANCE GRAPH. Biology signals novelty (LC/dopamine bursts, Takeuchi 2016, PMID 27602521) but has no first-class object 'what I know I don't know' — its frontier is implicit in prediction errors as they occur. ETTZ's frontier is queryable in advance and can DIRECT search (the TMR analog: replay keyed on open questions). Protect: it is the natural cue-source for any consolidation or exploration mechanism other analysts propose; do not let a filopodia analog probe randomly when a curated frontier exists. (3) BI-TEMPORAL CLAIMS AND --ASOF. Biology cannot query its past belief state: reconsolidation literally overwrites the trace in place (Nader 2000, PMID 10963596), and a reconsolidated memory leaves no record of its prior version. A closed window IS the record of how understanding changed — strictly superior for audit, and the correct substrate on which a reconsolidation-like edit can be ADOPTED safely (edit = close window + open successor, provenance intact). Protect: never import biology's destructive re-write; ETTZ already has the safe splice form. (4) CONTENT-HASH IDENTITY. Biology suffers representational drift — the CA1 code for the same place turns over across days (Ziv 2013, PMID 23396101); its keys go stale by construction, and stability of function over a moving code costs continuous re-keying. ETTZ's ids are eternal and exact. Protect: any embedding- or similarity-based retrieval layer added later must sit BESIDE content-hash addressing, never replace it — ETTZ has solved a problem biology lives with. (5) THE APPEND-ONLY TOTAL ARCHIVE WITH A GUARD TEST. Biology's own default is closer to ETTZ than it looks — natural forgetting is a reversible accessible→inaccessible switch (Ryan & Frankland 2022, PMID 35027710) and true erasure requires destroying the units (Han 2009, PMID 19286560) — but biology has REDUNDANT delete paths and no guard: CR3/C3 knockouts retain ~50% of engulfment (Schafer 2012, PMID 22632727), and the pruning machinery reactivating in adulthood is Alzheimer-model synapse loss (Hong 2016, PMID 27033548). ETTZ's single-path-with-failing-guard-test is safer than biology. Protect absolutely. (6) OWNER-IN-THE-LOOP ADJUDICATION. Biology has no external authorizer; its closest analog is the CD47/SIRPα veto (Lehrman 2018, PMID 30308165), an automated brake that fails silently when its ligand lapses. An external adjudicator who must actively rule before disposal is a stronger interlock than any biological checkpoint. Protect: proposals for 'autonomous use-dependent pruning' must terminate in the owner gate, not replace it. (7) CHOICE ENVELOPES. Biology's roads-not-taken are invisible: inverse tagging demotes non-participating synapses (Okuno 2012, PMID 22579289) but records nothing about why they lost. 'Considered X, picked Y, because Z' is learnable counterfactual data no nervous system possesses — the substrate for a better-than-biological credit assignment. Protect and prioritize its implementation.

## Argument against
The case against 'missing': several A-side items are arguably present under other names, which would pull the verdict toward 'partial'. Prediction-error gating? — claims' trials already emit +confirm/−fail/~anomalous, which IS a prediction-error signal (a claim is a prediction; a trial is a test). Reconsolidation? — close-the-window-on-contradiction is a read-triggered re-commitment of sorts. Inhibition? — CHOICE envelopes spec why-NOT-used, a negative record. Backpressure? — stale-fabric alarms and beat jitter analysis watch for overload symptoms. Critical-period windows? — entropy's 30-day dwell plus grace verification is a bounded process per file. Renormalization? — the bank is new and balances could trivially be rescaled. On this reading, ETTZ holds most of the raw signals and merely hasn't wired them, so 'partial' would be more honest than 'missing'. Additionally, some A-items may be deliberate divergence rather than absence: an estate of record arguably SHOULD have static thresholds (predictability for audit) and exact-only retrieval (no hallucinated pattern completion in a system whose value is verifiability).

## Strongest objection
The strongest objection to 'missing' is the prediction-error case, because it is the load-bearing one: if claims' trial outcomes already constitute a prediction-error signal, then the centerpiece missing mechanism — salience/PE-gated consolidation — is not missing but merely unconsumed, and the correct verdict is 'partial' with the missing piece being 'a consumer'. By extension the whole A-list could be recast as 'signals exist, wiring absent', collapsing the sweep's finding into one generic observation.

## Verdict survives because
A datum is not a mechanism. Biology makes the same distinction sharply: glutamate is everywhere, but the NMDA coincidence GATE is what converts it into a write-enable line (Nowak 1984, PMID 6320006); dopamine is tonically present, but it is the D1/D5 GATE on PRP synthesis that makes salience a commit authorization (Frey & Morris 1997, PMID 9020359; Wang 2010, PMID 20962282), and Sevenster 2013 (PMID 23413355) shows retrieval alone does NOT open the update window — only mismatch does: the gate is the computation. ETTZ's ~anomalous outcome is stored and never gates anything; no threshold slides (metaplasticity), no balance is renormalized (Turrigiano 1998, PMID 9495341), no read makes anything labile, no negative signal subtracts at read time, no pruning competence expires. In every case the computational logic — signal ROUTED through a threshold to an effect on a defined timescale — is absent, and that logic is precisely what the verdict rubric measures. 'Partial' would be correct for any single item where the wiring is half-built; for the aggregate, where in twelve areas zero of the gates exist, 'missing' stands. The deliberate-divergence half of the objection survives only for exact retrieval (a genuine doctrine candidate, noted in doctrine_notes) and does not generalize: static thresholds are nowhere stated as owner law, and saturation of an un-renormalized bank is a bug by anyone's doctrine.

## Missing pieces
- NEUROMODULATORY SALIENCE BROADCAST (tagging-capture + critique: Schultz/Dayan/Montague 1997, PMID 9054347; Takeuchi 2016, PMID 27602521; D1/D5 gate in Frey & Morris 1997, PMID 9020359): nothing in ETTZ computes 'this period matters' as a signal separate from content; every consolidation/promotion proposal by the other analysts (tagging, sleep-replay, synaptogenesis) presupposes a commit-authorization input that does not exist — the gate with no upstream is the sweep's sharpest unclaimed gap.
- RECONSOLIDATION-ON-RETRIEVAL (critique addition: Nader 2000, PMID 10963596): ETTZ reads are idempotent; biology makes a retrieved record labile with a bounded re-commit window, giving update-on-access and a principled edit point. ETTZ claims are only re-examined when a trial is explicitly scheduled — a claim can be read a thousand times while stale. The safe ETTZ form exists (close window + open successor), but no mechanism triggers it on access.
- PREDICTION-ERROR GATE ON DESTABILIZATION (critique: Sevenster 2013, PMID 23413355): the companion that keeps reconsolidation sane — a read that confirms the record leaves it alone; a read that contradicts it opens the record for edit. ETTZ records ~anomalous outcomes but they gate nothing; matters because it is a directly implementable invalidation policy for claims/, and without it any reconsolidation import degenerates to 'every read rewrites'.
- PATTERN SEPARATION / COMPLETION (critique: Nakazawa 2002, PMID 12040087; Leutgeb 2007, PMID 17303747): ETTZ retrieval is exact-key only (content-hash, --asof, exact spine cue match). No mechanism assigns near-colliding keys to similar episodes (separation) or retrieves a whole record from a partial cue (completion). Matters for the promotion gap: detecting 'repeated episodic patterns' requires deciding when two episodes are the SAME pattern — which is exactly the separation/completion computation.
- CRITICAL PERIODS THAT CLOSE (microglia-pruning: Schafer 2012, PMID 22632727 — engulfment competence high at P5, gone by P9 while capability persists; Hong 2016, PMID 27033548 — the unwindowed pruner reactivating IS Alzheimer synapse loss; myelination: McGee 2005, PMID 16195464): entropy's dwell-clock authority is ambient and perpetual. Biology time-boxes destructive COMPETENCE independently of capability. Matters: Kalli-class processes should hold expiring, renewable mandates, not standing authority — the biological failure mode of ambient pruning is documented pathology.
- METAPLASTICITY / SLIDING THRESHOLDS (hebbian: Abraham & Bear 1996, PMID 8658594; BCM, Bienenstock 1982, PMID 7054394): every ETTZ threshold (30-day dwell, beat-jitter alarm levels, any future promotion bar) is a static constant; biology stores thresholds as state that slides with time-averaged activity, so a busy organ's bar for 'notable' rises. Matters: without it, adopted Hebbian ranking saturates and beat alerting misfires on legitimately busy organs.
- HOMEOSTATIC MULTIPLICATIVE RENORMALIZATION (hebbian: Turrigiano 1998, PMID 9495341; sleep: de Vivo 2017, PMID 28154076 — ~18% multiplicative shrink sparing the strong ~20%): bank balances accumulate monotonically forever; no periodic rescale-preserving-ratios exists. Matters: early-mover prompts/tools become permanently unbeatable (rich-get-richer lock-in), the exact runaway instability biology spends every night correcting.
- INHIBITION AS A FIRST-CLASS SIGNAL / DIVISIVE NORMALIZATION (critique: Carandini & Heeger 2012, PMID 22108672; also Anderson/Bjork 1994, PMID 7931095 — retrieval suppresses strong competitors): ETTZ has no negative signal class and no read-time arbitration when multiple procedures/claims match a cue. Most cortical computation is shaped by interneurons; sparsity, capacity ceilings and which-of-several-matches-wins are unenforced anywhere in the estate.
- BACKPRESSURE / EXCITOTOXICITY PROTECTION (astrocytes: Lehre & Danbolt 1998, PMID 9786982 — buffer sized 3-5x a simultaneous discharge of the WHOLE population; Tanaka 1997, PMID 9180080 — clearance failure is seizure and death, not slow service): no organ-external clearance/rate-limit controller exists for the envelope stream. Stale-fabric alarms detect silence, not flood. Matters: the failure mode of an unbounded intake queue is runaway, and biology sizes against worst-case peak, not mean.
- AWAKE / IDLE-TIME REPLAY (critique: Jadhav 2012, PMID 22555434; Foster & Wilson 2006, PMID 16474382): consolidation in biology also runs opportunistically in quiet-waking moments, in reverse order, and disrupting ONLY awake ripples impairs learning. memory-nightly is a cron batch; the scheduler architecture (idle-time background task vs nightly job) is different in kind, and the 60s+ cold fold is the symptom.
- SCHEMA-DEPENDENT FAST CONSOLIDATION (critique: Tse 2007, PMID 17412951): once a cortical schema exists, ONE consistent trial becomes hippocampus-independent in ~48h — writing into existing structure is orders of magnitude cheaper than a cold write. ETTZ has no fast path keyed on consistency-with-existing-claims; matters because the promotion mechanism others propose should run at two rates, and the cheap rate is where most real traffic lands.
- REPRESENTATIONAL-DRIFT TOLERANCE / CUE-STALENESS DETECTION (critique: Ziv 2013, PMID 23396101): biology keeps function stable over a drifting code by continuous re-keying; ETTZ's spine cues ('<situation>') and subject vocabularies assume semantics stay valid forever. Content-hash ids are immune, but the CUE layer is not — nothing detects that a procedure's situation-cue no longer matches how situations are actually described in current envelopes.

## Adoptable proposal: SALIENCE — a prediction-error-derived commit-priority signal for consolidation
- biological principle: Salience is a separate, broadcast commit-authorization signal, not a property of content: dopamine D1/D5 activation is a hard prerequisite for the plasticity-protein broadcast that makes any tagged event durable (Frey & Morris 1997, PMID 9020359; Wang 2010, PMID 20962282), the upstream computation is prediction error — fire on unexpected outcome, silence on expected (Schultz/Dayan/Montague 1997, PMID 9054347) — and mismatch, not retrieval per se, is what opens a record for update (Sevenster 2013, PMID 23413355). Novelty converts ordinary memories into persistent ones (Takeuchi 2016, PMID 27602521; behavioral tagging, Moncada & Viola 2007, PMID 17626208).
- ettz change: One new envelope kind and one consumer rule, both append-only. (a) When a claim trial lands −fail or ~anomalous, or an ingested event matches an open ignorance-graph question or desideratum, the spine mints a SALIENCE envelope (subject, scalar priority, mandatory why, refs to the triggering envelope) — prediction error and novelty are the only two sources, exactly biology's pair. (b) memory-nightly orders its fold/promotion batch by recent salience instead of pure chronology: high-salience windows are folded first and their co-windowed envelopes are consolidation-eligible (the behavioral-tagging rescue), low-salience traffic still folds, later. Salience prioritizes; it never admits or excludes anything from the archive.
- cost: Build: small — a rule at the spine's trial-recording path and ignorance-graph match path emitting one extra envelope (the schema already carries why/refs); a sort-and-window step in memory-nightly's batch selection. No new organ, no schema migration. Runtime: negligible envelope volume by construction (salience events are the rare exceptions, not the stream); one extra index on recency-of-salience for the fold; slight scheduling complexity in memory-nightly.
- risks: (1) Positive-feedback runaway: anomalies drive consolidation, freshly promoted low-trial claims fail their early trials, minting more salience — a salience storm that starves routine folding. Mitigation is itself biological (BCM sliding threshold: the bar for 'salient' must rise with recent salience rate, Bienenstock 1982) and must ship in v1, not later. (2) Where the biological principle is WRONG for ETTZ: in biology, no salience means NO consolidation at all — the broadcast simply never fires — and importing that admission-control semantics would silently starve routine-but-important state (a backup that always succeeds is low-salience forever and would never be promoted into claims). ETTZ must invert the polarity: salience sets ORDER and window-priority only; everything still lands in events/ and everything eventually folds. (3) Cold start: with 4 seeded claims the prediction-error source is nearly mute, so early salience is novelty-only (ignorance-graph matches), which over-fires on a young system where almost everything is novel — the sliding threshold must be active from day one. (4) Gaming-by-noise: a flaky organ emitting spurious anomaly envelopes buys itself fold priority; the salience rule must weight by the emitting claim's trial history, not raw anomaly count.
- owning session: memory-spine/spine sessions (the trial-recording and ignorance-graph paths where the signal originates), with memory-nightly as the sole consumer of the ordering rule.
- priority argument: Ranks first among unclaimed adoptions because it is UPSTREAM of every other analyst's proposal: the tagging analyst's salience-triggered consolidation pass, the sleep analyst's replay selection, the synaptogenesis analyst's promotion-on-recurrence, and the owner's own sharpest-gap fix (episodic→claims promotion) all presuppose a signal that says which windows matter — and no ETTZ mechanism computes it. Biology is unambiguous that this signal is architecturally separate from content (the D1/D5 gate is a different pathway from the tag), so it cannot be inferred later from volume statistics; it must be minted at event time. It is also the cheapest of the twelve missing pieces (two rules, one envelope kind, zero doctrine friction) with the highest fan-out: once salience envelopes exist, the sliding-threshold, renormalization, and reconsolidation imports each become a consumer rule rather than a new subsystem. It ranks above the inhibition and backpressure gaps only because those protect against loads the estate has not yet hit, while the salience gap blocks proposals the owner has already asked for.

## Doctrine notes
Three explicit touches on owner law. (1) COMPRESS AND ARCHIVE, NEVER ERASE: the salience proposal is doctrine-clean only under the stated polarity inversion — salience must be priority/ordering, never admission control on the archive; biology's 'no salience, no consolidation' semantics, imported literally, would be a de facto disposal decision made by an algorithm, which the law reserves to the owner. Likewise any adoption of the inhibition/divisive-normalization gap must be implemented as rank demotion and window-closing, never deletion — biology's own default supports this (forgetting is an accessibility switch, Ryan & Frankland 2022, PMID 35027710; only unit destruction truly erases, Han 2009, PMID 19286560), so the law has biological cover, not just moral force. (2) OWNER AUTHORIZATION GATES DISPOSAL: the critical-periods finding (missing piece 5) strengthens rather than threatens this doctrine — biology shows ambient pruning authority reactivating outside its window is pathology (Hong 2016), so the recommendation is to make Kalli's competence expiring AND still owner-gated: the window bounds the algorithm, the owner remains the adjudicator. The B-side finding that ETTZ's single-delete-path-with-guard-test is SAFER than biology's redundant-paths-no-guard is a doctrine vindication and should be quoted back to the owner as such. (3) RESEARCH-ONLY / NOTHING BUILDS UNTIL HE RULES: everything here, including the adoptable proposal, is for owner review. One candidate DELIBERATE divergence surfaced by the objection analysis deserves an explicit owner ruling rather than silent inheritance: exact-only retrieval (no pattern completion) may be intentional — a verifiability-first estate arguably should not pattern-complete — but it is nowhere written as law, and the promotion mechanism the owner wants cannot be built without SOME similarity computation; he should rule on whether approximate matching is permitted in the promotion path while retrieval stays exact.
