# engrams-distribution
MECHANISM: An engram is a memory carried as a sparse PATTERN across units — allocated competitively to the most-excitable units in the moments before encoding, tagged content-free by a threshold latch inside an open window, retrieved by pattern completion from a partial cue, distributed redundantly across ~117 of 247 regions, addressed as (unit set × input pathway), linked to temporally adjacent memories by shared membership, silenceable without being lost, and erasable only by destroying the units themselves (Josselyn & Tonegawa 2020 PMID:31896692; Yiu 2014 PMID:25102562; Reijmers 2007 PMID:17761885; Nakazawa 2002 PMID:12040087; Roy 2022 PMID:35379803; Abdou 2018 PMID:29903972; Cai 2016 PMID:27251287; Ryan 2015 PMID:26023136; Han 2009 PMID:19286560).
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: Split across four ETTZ mechanisms, none of which is an ensemble. (1) IDENTITY/ADDRESS: the content-hash envelope id and the one-file-one-address claim — a belief is a quotable object at a location. (2) CONNECTIVITY: envelope `refs` plus ORGANON O-8 flow refs — a minted opaque id that travels with an item across organs so a journey reads as one thread; this is the closest thing to an activity tag (opaque, content-free, marks participation, outlives the episode — functionally what TetTag does, Reijmers 2007 PMID:17761885). (3) WEIGHTS/ACCESSIBILITY: claims' trials (+confirm/−fail/~anomalous) with bi-temporal windows, the bank's usage balances, procedures' verified/contested state. (4) DISTRIBUTION: 12 sources across two machines, the fractal law (parent = union of children), BINNA's SPEC-SPINE-READ merged read, GRAPHIFY's ~150k-node render. Retrieval keys available today: content hash, subject, refs traversal, flow ref, procedures' `<situation>` cue, `--asof`. All exact-match.
VERDICT: partial

## Argument for
The analogy holds at computational-logic level in four places, and one of them is a structural match ETTZ should be given credit for.

STORAGE vs ACCESS, ALREADY SEPARATED. Ryan 2015 (PMID:26023136) is the sharpest engram result: identity lives in the connectivity pattern (survives protein-synthesis-blockade amnesia), while synaptic weights govern only whether a natural cue can pattern-complete into it. ETTZ has this split in its store layout already — refs/flow refs are the connectivity, claim trials and bank balances are the weights, and they live in different sub-layers with different lifecycles (connectivity is append-only; trials accumulate). Most software memory systems conflate these into a single relevance score. ETTZ did not.

CONSOLIDATION-AS-WINDOW-SHIFT, NOT COPY. Kitamura 2017 (PMID:28386011) shows every copy of a memory is written at encoding — the prefrontal copy is born SILENT — and what changes over two weeks is which copy answers to natural cues; the hippocampal copy goes quiet, nothing is moved. That is exactly ETTZ's bi-temporal, close-the-window-never-delete semantics: a superseded claim with a closed window is not a deleted claim, it is a copy that no longer answers. The logic is identical, and it is unusual.

INDEX/CONTENT SEPARATION. Teyler & Rudy 2007 (PMID:17696170, added by the critic) states the architecture ETTZ already has: a small fast index over large slow content, with the index able to survive while content is unreachable and each ageing independently. The spine is precisely an index — envelopes are records ABOUT things living in organ outboxes, pulled by the Collector; a new host joins the index with one registry drop.

DISTRIBUTION AND CO-ACTIVATION. Roy 2022 (PMID:35379803) — ~117 of 247 regions hold one contextual memory, and co-reactivating several recalls better than any one — is the biological statement of the fractal law and BINNA's union read. A journey genuinely does live across organs stitched by flow refs, and the flow ref behaves like an engram tag on the axis that matters: it is opaque, carries no content, and marks participation.

QUALIFIED ADDRESSING IS EXPRESSIBLE. Abdou 2018 (PMID:29903972) shows the address is (unit set × input pathway), never the unit. ETTZ's envelope already carries subject AND refs, so (subject × ref pathway) is available as a compound key without schema change.

Finally, on the owner's specific hypothesis: the auditability half is real and defensible. Liu 2012 (PMID:22441246) shows optogenetic reactivation of a tagged dentate ensemble produces the memory's behaviour in a neutral context with no natural cue present — the substrate cannot distinguish a genuine cue from an arbitrary driver, because there is no separate "read the record" step; the pattern IS the content. An engram has no provenance and no `why`. ETTZ's mandatory `why` field requires a record that can carry it, and a record that can carry a reason must have an address. The why-ledger and ensemble-only storage are genuinely incompatible, so localization on the ADDRESSING axis is a deliberate, doctrine-grounded divergence, not an oversight.

## Argument against
The mapping breaks in five places, and the first is the load-bearing one.

"DISTRIBUTED" MEANS TWO OPPOSITE THINGS. In engram work distribution is REDUNDANT ENCODING: the same memory is written into many units, so losing units degrades the memory gracefully rather than removing it — that is why Han 2009 (PMID:19286560) had to ablate the specifically allocated CREB-high neurons to erase a memory, and why ablating an equal random subset did nothing. ETTZ's distribution is PARTITIONED, not redundant: each envelope exists once, each claim in exactly one file at one address. Losing an organ loses its pieces outright; losing one claim file is a Han-2009 targeted ablation performed by accident. The same word describes opposite failure behaviour, and the candidate mapping's phrase "a JOURNEY lives across organs" quietly trades on the ambiguity. A journey being traceable across organs is provenance, not redundancy.

CONTENT-HASH ADDRESSING IS ANTI-COMPLETION BY CONSTRUCTION. It is tempting to read "content-hash id" as content-addressable memory. It is the reverse. A cryptographic hash is engineered so that near-identical inputs get maximally dissimilar addresses — it is a pure pattern-SEPARATION function with collision-resistance as its design goal. Leutgeb 2007 (PMID:17303747) shows DG separating and CA3 completing across the same graded input morph; ETTZ has built an extremely good DG and has no CA3 at all. Nakazawa 2002 (PMID:12040087) gives the exact phenotype: CA3-NR1 knockouts recall normally from a FULL cue and fail entirely from a PARTIAL one. That is ETTZ's condition, precisely and diagnostically.

GRAPHIFY IS A RENDERING, NOT A SUBSTRATE. ~150k nodes on :9930 with fractal zoom is a picture of the connectome. An engram's graph is the computation — reactivating part of it produces the memory. Nothing in ETTZ retrieves by traversing GRAPHIFY; it visualizes structure the estate does not think with. Counting its nodes as evidence of ensemble memory overstates the estate by one whole layer.

EVERY EDGE IS AUTHORED. Ensemble co-membership in biology is incidental — a byproduct of which units happened to be excitable, with a leaky hours-scale trace producing automatic linking of events close in time and ACTIVE disallocation of distant ones (Cai 2016 PMID:27251287; Rashid 2016 PMID:27463673). Every ETTZ ref was deliberately written by something. Deliberate edges give auditability; they cannot give serendipity, and they cannot form without someone already knowing the two things are related — which is the case where you least need the link.

NOTHING IS ALLOCATED, BECAUSE EVERYTHING IS WRITTEN. Allocation is a competition with a threshold and a window (Yiu 2014 PMID:25102562: rank, don't score; relative excitability immediately before encoding; lateral inhibition holding sparsity at 2-5% dentate). ETTZ writes an envelope for every event, action, decision and observation — 71,225 of them — with no threshold and no window. There is therefore no salience signal anywhere at write time, and the consequence has already surfaced as a symptom: a cold fold takes 60s+ because the read path has to consider everything, having been given no ranking at write. Carandini & Heeger 2012 (PMID:22108672, added by the critic) names the missing counterpart — divisive normalization, the fast always-on read-time arbitration deciding which of several matching items wins. ETTZ has no arbitration rule.

Evidence hygiene: the sweep's prose invokes Ramirez 2013 (false-memory installation), Zhou 2009 (excitability as the mediating variable) and Ryan & Frankland 2022 (forgetting as reversible state transition) with no citation records; per the critic these are unverifiable as written and I have not built on any of them. The confabulation point above rests on Liu 2012 alone, which is sufficient for it.

## Strongest objection
"You are calling this partial, but every gap you name is a retrieval convenience. ETTZ's memory is complete in the sense that matters: no fact is lost, every belief is quotable, every judgment carries its why, contradictions close windows instead of destroying evidence, and `--asof` reconstructs any past understanding. An engram is lossy, confabulating, unauditable and cannot tell you why it believes anything. ETTZ strictly dominates it on information content. Missing partial-cue retrieval is a search-UX shortfall that can be bolted on whenever it hurts. The honest verdict is 'deliberately different': the estate chose a different substrate and the engram criteria simply do not apply to it."

## Verdict survives because
The objection conflates a store with a memory, and the engram literature is precisely where that conflation is settled.

Josselyn & Tonegawa 2020 (PMID:31896692) defines the engram with three criteria, and the third is that reactivation must be SUFFICIENT to reproduce recall — a persistent physical change that cannot be reached is not yet memory. Nakazawa 2002 (PMID:12040087) then supplies the decisive case, and it is not a UX case: CA3-NR1 knockout animals HAVE the memory intact and retrieve it perfectly under a full cue, yet fail under a partial one. The store is complete; the animal is functionally amnesic, because natural cues are almost always partial. ETTZ's operating condition is identical. The cue an organ or agent actually holds at question time is a fragment — a subject, a rough time window, a phrase remembered from a `why`, a symptom — never a content hash. A retrieval layer that answers only exact keys answers only questions whose answers you already located.

Two further points defeat the "bolt it on later" half. First, the cost is already being paid: the cold fold at 60s+ over 71k envelopes is the read path failing to scale precisely because it has no ranking and no completion, and that cost grows with the corpus the doctrine requires to keep growing forever. Second, Ryan 2015 (PMID:26023136) shows that unreachability is invisible from inside the store — the only way to distinguish stored-but-unaddressable from gone is to run both read paths and compare. ETTZ currently cannot run that comparison, so the objection's confident claim that "no fact is lost" is untested. It is true that no BYTES are lost; whether any KNOWLEDGE is reachable is a different measurement that has never been taken.

Why not "deliberately different" wholesale: doctrine has to actually explain the absence for that verdict to hold. Owner law forbids deletion, mandates provenance and a `why` on every judgment, and makes repairs splices. None of that forbids a rebuildable derived index that ranks and completes — such an index deletes nothing, asserts nothing, and can be regenerated from the append-only spine at will. Localization is deliberate on the addressing axis and I have credited it as such; the absence of completion, of graded recall, of an ensemble noun, and of any read-time arbitration is a build-order gap wearing doctrine's coat. Hence partial, with the named missing piece being partial-cue retrieval.

One correction the objection earns: ETTZ's substitute for graceful degradation is not nothing. It is the CUSTODIAN — restic snapshots plus the planned A-002 interlock that blocks a Kalli disposal when no verified snapshot holds the file. Backup restores exactly where a degraded engram returns an approximation, so on the recovery axis ETTZ is arguably better. But backup restores the file, not availability during the outage, and the brief reports the first Time Machine copy at 26% on 2026-09-01 — so today, for claims, durability is one file plus a partial backup. That sharpens the verdict rather than softening it.

## Missing pieces
- Partial-cue retrieval (the CA3 completion function). Every ETTZ key is exact: content hash, subject, flow ref, `<situation>` string, `--asof`. Nothing reconstructs a whole record from an incomplete cue. Nakazawa 2002 (PMID:12040087) is the exact phenotype; Leutgeb 2007 (PMID:17303747) shows ETTZ has built the separator without the completer.
- No storage-vs-access diagnostic. ETTZ cannot distinguish silent-but-held from lost, because it never runs the id-addressed and cue-addressed read paths against the same item and compares (Ryan 2015 PMID:26023136). This is the instrument gap, and it blocks sizing every other gap here.
- No ENSEMBLE ID. Roy 2022 (PMID:35379803) shows one memory held by ~117 regions with recall scaling as more co-reactivate; ETTZ has no noun for 'the set of records that together constitute this memory', so a co-activated set cannot be named, referenced, or measured. Flow refs are the transpose (one item across many organs), not the ensemble (many records in one memory).
- No graded recall. BINNA's SPEC-SPINE-READ union is boolean: a parent is the union of its children. Corroboration by many sources does not raise retrieval confidence, and no organ-count-weighted recall strength exists, so Roy 2022's central quantitative finding has no ETTZ counterpart.
- No eligibility/co-allocation window. Temporal adjacency links nothing. Cai 2016 (PMID:27251287) / Rashid 2016 (PMID:27463673) compute proximity with a leaky analog variable rather than timestamp comparison; ETTZ has 15s beats and timestamps but no decaying score and no automatic co-tagging.
- No read-time arbitration when many records match (divisive normalization, Carandini & Heeger 2012 PMID:22108672). The 60s+ cold fold is the symptom of an unranked read path over an unsparsified write path.
- Refs are not a first-class component of the retrieval address. Abdou 2018 (PMID:29903972) makes the address (unit set × input pathway); ETTZ has subject and refs but does not key retrieval on the pair, so any future co-tagging risks collapsing distinct claims that share a subject.
- No redundant encoding for claims. One claim = one file = one point of ablation, which Han 2009 (PMID:19286560) identifies as the single irreversible operation. Durability rests entirely on CUSTODIAN, whose first Time Machine copy was 26% complete on 2026-09-01.
- No cheap reversible silencing primitive for episodic material. Biology's default disposal is a cheap accessibility flip; ETTZ's only accessibility switch is quarantine with a .why.md manifest and owner authorization — correct for disposal, far too heavy for routine 'stop surfacing this'.
- No modelled key staleness. ETTZ is immune today because content hashes never drift, but Ziv 2013 (PMID:23396101) means any embedding-based completion index reintroduces drift, so such an index must be declared rebuildable and never become a system of record.

## Adoptable proposal: THE SILENCE PROBE — a two-path read diagnostic and a silence ledger on the ignorance graph
- biological principle: The silent engram (Ryan 2015, PMID:26023136): a memory can be fully stored — engram-cell connectivity intact — yet unreachable by natural cues, because synaptic weights govern addressability while connectivity carries identity. The diagnostic that separates 'stored but unaddressable' from 'gone' is to bypass the cue and drive the ensemble directly: if direct activation retrieves and the cue does not, the item is silent, not lost, and the repair is to restore associations rather than to re-encode. Nakazawa 2002 (PMID:12040087) supplies the partial-cue manipulation that makes the failure visible: full-cue recall intact, partial-cue recall abolished.
- ettz change: Add a read-only probe job that, over a rotating nightly sample, attempts to reach the same item by two paths and records which succeeded. DIRECT path: content-hash id, flow ref, or explicit file address. CUE path: whatever cue-shaped retrieval the estate actually offers — subject match, refs traversal, claim-predicate lookup, the procedures' `<situation>` cue — driven by a DEGRADED cue derived from the item (fields dropped, per Nakazawa's partial-cue manipulation) or, preferably, by a real historical query recovered from the spine. Emit one probe-result envelope per item with the mandatory `why` (`probe: partial-cue reachability, cue class = <historical|synthetic>`), and publish the aggregate as first-class open questions on the IGNORANCE GRAPH: 'the estate holds N items it cannot reach from any cue it would plausibly be asked with', named individually while the semantic and procedural stores are small. Nothing else changes: no new store, no schema change to envelope.schema.json, no writes to claims, procedures or events, and no repair action. The probe reports; repair remains an O-8 splice under owner authorization.
- cost: BUILD: small. It is an eval — pick a sample, call two existing read paths, diff, emit envelopes. No new organ, no schema migration, no change to the append-only core. The only genuinely new work is the cue-degradation function and a decision about what counts as a realistic cue, which is where the design effort actually sits. RUNTIME: bounded only if sampled. A full-corpus pass is 71,225 items × two read paths, and the known 60s+ cold fold says the read side does not have that headroom, so cap at a few hundred items per night on rotation, run off memory-nightly's tail, and let the sample cover claims and procedures exhaustively (there are only 4 and 12) while sampling events. STORAGE: one small envelope per probed item per run, which is real append-only growth on a corpus that already strains the fold — so probe results should be summarised into a single run-level envelope plus per-item detail only for failures.
- risks: 1. THE MEASUREMENT IS THE EXPERIMENT, AND THE EASY VERSION IS WORTHLESS. If cues are synthesised by mechanically dropping fields from the record, the probe measures the index's tolerance to field-dropping — not reachability from the cues real agents hold. Expected failure: a flattering silence rate near zero while retrieval keeps failing in practice, and the owner rules against building a completion layer on the strength of a number that measured nothing. Mitigation: draw cues from historical query envelopes wherever they exist, and type synthetic-cue results as a strictly lower-grade evidence class that may not be aggregated with real-cue results.
2. SILENCE-DRIVEN RE-INGESTION. The obvious reflex on finding an unreachable item is to re-ingest it. In a content-hash store a re-ingest is a NEW envelope with a NEW id — the estate then holds two records of one fact with no way to know they are the same, which is exactly the identity-join problem O-8 exists for, and it inflates the corpus the fold already cannot read cold. Concrete and likely. Mitigation: probe output must be typed as an observation with no actionable verb door, and the only sanctioned repair is a ref-splice.
3. AUTO-MINTED REPAIR REFS AS SMUGGLED INFERENCE. Ryan 2015's repair is to restore weights; the tempting port is to auto-mint refs so silent items become reachable. That writes an inference into an append-only store as if it were structure. Under O-8 the honest form is a QUESTION envelope; only an owner decision makes it a splice. If this guard is skipped, the estate's connectivity graph silently fills with machine guesses that are indistinguishable from authored edges after the fact — and being append-only, they cannot be withdrawn, only questioned.
4. INVISIBLE SCHEDULE PRESSURE. A scan-heavy job on memory-nightly's tail leaves the heartbeat mesh seeing an organ that is busy, not silent — so no stale-fabric alarm fires and the fold quietly starts missing its window. Mitigation: probe emits its own duration into the run envelope with a hard budget and self-truncates.
5. STATISTICALLY MEANINGLESS RATES. Four claims and twelve procedures cannot yield a percentage. Reporting '25% of claims are silent' from one silent claim would be actively misleading to the owner's ruling. Report counts and names, not rates, until the semantic and procedural stores are an order of magnitude larger.
WHERE THE BIOLOGY WOULD BE WRONG FOR ETTZ: biology treats silence as ADAPTIVE — a reversible, environmentally modulated state that is useful, not a defect. ETTZ must import the diagnostic and reject the valuation: in the estate a silent item is a defect to be surfaced. Second, biology's repair is to strengthen existing associations automatically and continuously; ETTZ's must be owner-gated and discrete, because an automatic association-strengthener in an append-only store is an un-retractable opinion generator. Third, do NOT port reconsolidation alongside it (Nader 2000 PMID:10963596) — retrieval destabilising the stored record is flatly incompatible with append-only, and ETTZ already implements the prediction-error-gated version correctly (Sevenster 2013 PMID:23413355): a failing trial closes the window and opens a successor claim, which is update-by-splice rather than update-by-rewrite.
- owning session: M1 eval harness — best fit, because the probe IS an eval (it re-runs retrieval against known items and scores the outcome) and the harness already re-runs episodes, so the sampling, scheduling and pass/fail plumbing exist. memory-nightly hosts the schedule hook on its tail; the IGNORANCE GRAPH is the publication surface, since 'what the system knows it doesn't know' is exactly the right home for 'what the estate holds but cannot reach'. No new session required.
- priority argument: Rank it FIRST among the engram-derived proposals, and BELOW the generalisation fix in ultimate value. It is first because it is the instrument: every larger engram import in this mapping — a completion index, an ensemble id, graded recall, the co-allocation window — is a bet on the size of a gap nobody has measured, and Ryan 2015's whole contribution is that this particular gap is invisible without exactly this two-path comparison. Instrument before surgery. It is also the least doctrinally exposed thing this project will propose: read-only, additive, no deletion path, no assertion, nothing in the core touched, and if the owner rules against everything else the probe still leaves the estate with a health metric it lacked. It ranks below an episodes-to-claims promotion mechanism in final value, because the probe only measures while promotion adds capability — but the promotion should be built second, not first, because the probe is what will tell the owner whether promotion actually improved reachability or merely added records. One caveat against over-ranking it: a diagnostic nobody acts on is pure corpus growth on a store whose fold is already slow, so it should be built only if the owner intends to rule on the completion question afterwards.

## Doctrine notes
1. COMPRESS-AND-ARCHIVE-NEVER-ERASE IS EXTERNALLY VALIDATED, WITH ONE ASYMMETRY UNBUILT. Han 2009 (PMID:19286560) shows the only demonstrated true erasure is destroying the allocated units, and destroying a random equal subset does nothing; Ryan 2015 (PMID:26023136) shows everything short of that changes accessibility, not existence. So the owner's absolute law is the biological default, not a constraint fought against, and the CUSTODIAN A-002 interlock ('does a verified snapshot hold this?' blocks the delete) is the correct guard because it stands exactly where the one irreversible operation is. The asymmetry the owner should note: biology makes silencing CHEAP and reversible while making destruction expensive and rare. ETTZ has the expensive gate but no cheap silencing primitive — quarantine, with its .why.md manifest and owner authorisation, is the right weight for disposal and far too heavy for 'stop surfacing this'. That is a doctrine-adjacent gap, not a doctrine violation, and it is worth a separate ruling.

2. ANY AUTOMATIC LINKING IS AN INFERENCE AND MUST ENTER AS A QUESTION. This is the doctrinal hinge for every engram import. Biology's co-allocation is emergent and unlogged; ETTZ's edges are authored and carry a `why`. If a future co-allocation window or completion index mints refs or ensemble ids directly, it writes machine guesses into an append-only structure where they become permanently indistinguishable from authored edges. ORGANON O-8 already encodes the correct discipline — a suspected identity-join is a QUESTION envelope, a confirmed one a DECISION envelope (a splice) — and every proposal in this mapping should be held to it without exception.

3. DERIVED INDICES MUST BE DECLARED DISPOSABLE AND REBUILDABLE. A completion or similarity index is not part of the append-only estate and must never become a second system of record. The rule to write down before anything is built: the spine can regenerate the index; the index holds nothing the spine cannot regenerate. The moment that is violated, the estate has an un-append-only shadow store, and deleting a corrupted index becomes an erasure. Ziv 2013 (PMID:23396101) sharpens this — an embedding-based key drifts as its encoder changes, so a similarity layer's keys go stale in a way content hashes never do; rebuildability is what keeps that drift from being data loss.

4. RECONSOLIDATION MUST NOT BE PORTED LITERALLY, AND ETTZ ALREADY HAS THE RIGHT VERSION. Nader 2000 (PMID:10963596) has retrieval destabilise the record so it must be re-committed — read-modifies-write, incompatible with append-only at the root. But Sevenster 2013 (PMID:23413355) shows the gate is prediction error: a read that confirms leaves the record alone; a read that contradicts opens it. ETTZ's claims layer implements the doctrine-safe form of exactly this — a failing trial closes the window and a successor claim carries the new belief, so the update is a splice and the superseded claim IS the record of how understanding changed. Bouton 2004 (PMID:15466298) adds the corroboration: extinction is a competing context-keyed record, not deletion, with the old response returning outside the extinction context. ETTZ's bi-temporal windows are that pattern. This is a place where the estate is ahead of a naive biological port, and the owner should resist proposals that 'add reconsolidation'.

5. SPARSITY AT WRITE IS DELIBERATELY REJECTED AND SHOULD STAY REJECTED. Biology's severe sparsity (2-5% of dentate granule cells) exists because the substrate is capacity-bounded. The estate's substrate is not, and totality is doctrinally required — the why-ledger only works if every judgment was recorded. So refusing competitive write-time allocation is correct doctrine. What does NOT follow, and is the substantive finding of this analysis, is that the estate may also skip arbitration at READ time: Carandini & Heeger 2012 (PMID:22108672) is the fast competition among simultaneously matching items, and its absence is what the 60s+ cold fold is telling the owner.

6. NO CLAIM SHOULD BE SOLE-HELD. Han 2009 makes targeted destruction of the allocated units the one unrecoverable operation, and a one-file-one-address claim is by construction a set of one. Doctrine forbids erasure but does not currently forbid single-point custody, and with the CUSTODIAN's first Time Machine copy at 26% on 2026-09-01 the gap between the law and its enforcement is live. Whether claims should be redundantly held, or whether the custodian interlock is deemed sufficient, is an owner ruling this analysis cannot make.
