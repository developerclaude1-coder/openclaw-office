## Sharpest-gap verdict

CONFIRMED — the abstraction gap (nothing promotes repeated episodes into claims or procedures) is the sharpest capability gap, and it is the best-evidenced conclusion in the project. Three independent grounds: (1) the zero-fire fact — 71,225 episodic envelopes against 4 claims and 12 procedures, every one hand-seeded, so the episodic→semantic path has never executed once in the estate's life; (2) three literature clusters converge on it from different directions (deletion-manufactures-generalization per Migues 2016; replay-produces-gist per CLS/Durrant/Klinzing; neurogenesis-forgetting presuming prior consolidation per Akers 2014); (3) five analysts land on the same hole from five unrelated mechanisms (soma's absent coincidence detector on the afferent path, tagging's absent capture conjunction, sleep-replay's never-fired transfer loop, active-forgetting's absent promotion, signal-substrate's flight-recorder-that-defers-abstraction). Two riders sharpen rather than overturn the hypothesis. First, reverse-gap-hunt's candidate rival — the missing salience/neuromodulatory broadcast — is not a sharper separate gap but the load-bearing upstream COMPONENT of this one: a promotion gate with no commit-authorization input degenerates to frequency-mining, and v1 can bootstrap on signals the estate already emits (failing/anomalous trials, error streams, ignorance-graph matches, owner CHOICE envelopes). Stated fully, the gap is two-sided: no loop upward (episodes→claims) and no salience- or use-coupled demotion downward — the estate pays decay's labor while collecting none of abstraction's dividend. Second, one finding is sharper on a different axis — operational risk, not architecture: the unenforced A-002 handoff, where the estate's single irreversible operation has already run twice while the first backup stood at 26%; that is why it outranks even the abstraction fix in the adoption order. With that caveat recorded, the owner's hypothesis stands as the sharpest gap in the mapping itself.
