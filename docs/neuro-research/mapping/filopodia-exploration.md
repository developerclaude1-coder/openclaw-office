# filopodia-exploration
MECHANISM: Growth-cone exploration: cheap, time-boxed filopodial probes emitted stochastically beyond current contacts, steered by cue gradients read differentially with adaptive gain, with selective stabilization of the rare validated probe and default retraction of the rest (Ziv & Smith 1996 PMID:8755481; Bentley & Toroian-Raymond 1986 PMID:3773996; Rosoff 2004 PMID:15162167).
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: watch-contracts (BINNA keeper): diff of published organ contracts vs mesh membership, proposing joins/leaves. DESIDERATA + IGNORANCE GRAPH: an explicit want/frontier signal (a candidate gradient source with no actuator attached). Trigger-fabric confirming envelopes: the stabilization/clutch signal (progress only where acknowledgment validates traction). ORGANON participant→organ→system levels: post-contact maturation. Collector registry drop: contact instantiation. ("promote-candidates" is named only in the owner's question, not in the verified inventory — treated as not yet live.)
VERDICT: partial

## Argument for
The back half of the growth-cone loop is genuinely present in ETTZ at the level of computational logic. (1) Selective stabilization: biology's rule is that exploration is speculative but commitment is gated by a confirming handshake — a filopodium stabilizes only when contact evokes the right local signal (Lohmann & Bonhoeffer 2008 PMID:18667153: calcium-transient frequency decides stabilize-vs-retract within seconds, wrong partner class never stabilizes). ETTZ's trigger fabric has exactly this shape: verb doors act, and a confirming envelope closes the loop — the molecular clutch, traction granted only where acknowledgment validates it (Mitchison & Kirschner 1988 PMID:3078414, noting per the critique this is review-level sourcing for the clutch). (2) Proposal-then-gate: watch-contracts does not wire limbs, it PROPOSES joins for review — structurally the same separation of exploration from commitment that makes filopodia safe to run in parallel. (3) A want-signal exists: DESIDERATA and the IGNORANCE GRAPH are first-class encodings of 'what we lack,' which is precisely the role a chemoattractant source plays — a direction for growth that is not itself the grown structure. (4) Receiver-computed valence (Song 1998 PMID:9727979: identical ligand, cAMP state flips repulsion to attraction) is honored by the why-ledger/CHOICE-envelope design: the signal is neutral data, the sign is computed and recorded at the receiver. (5) Maturation staging: ORGANON's participant→organ→system levels mirror the probation-then-consolidation ladder new contacts climb (Holtmaat 2005 PMID:15664179). So the claim 'ETTZ has some of this logic under different names' is not vacuous.

## Argument against
The front half — the exploration itself — is absent, and the owner's own distinction cuts exactly here. Filopodia have four properties (Ziv & Smith 1996; synaptogenesis sweep §1): emitted BEFORE any target is known, cheap and disposable with retraction as the default outcome, spatially bounded to near-neighbourhood, and time-boxed (~9.5 min TTL). watch-contracts has none of them: it is a deterministic diff over contracts that organs have ALREADY published — reconciliation of self-announcements, not sampling beyond current contacts. Nothing in ETTZ extends toward a subject or organ that has not declared itself, and nothing retracts, because nothing was ever speculatively extended. Where is ETTZ's gradient? DESIDERATA is a static, owner-curated want-list — a list of destinations, not a concentration field: there is no differential readout ('am I getting closer?'), no adaptive re-zeroing of the kind Rosoff 2004 shows is mandatory (0.1% differential sensitivity exists only inside a narrow absolute band, requiring continuous gain control), and no actuator that consumes the wants and emits probes. Where is ETTZ's retraction? Nowhere — there is no probe object with a TTL that dies unanswered. And there is no repulsive channel at all: biology navigates largely by subtraction, with vetoes ~1000x more sensitive than go-signals (Luo 1993 PMID:8402908, 10 pM collapsin), while ETTZ has no 'do not connect here' cue class. Bentley & Toroian-Raymond 1986 gives the diagnosis its teeth: deprived of filopodia, growth cones still elongate but navigate blindly. ETTZ will not halt for lack of exploration — its pipelines run fine — it will simply never discover a connection that did not announce itself, and the deficit is invisible in the heartbeat/BEATS telemetry because silence-of-discovery is not a signal any organ emits.

## Strongest objection
The strongest objection to 'partial' is that it should be 'deliberately different': ETTZ is a curated estate under owner law — everything enters by explicit registry drop, every judgment carries its why, nothing builds until the owner rules. Stochastic majority-fail probing looks like the opposite of this temperament: manufactured noise in an append-only spine, speculative contact where the doctrine prizes deliberateness. On this reading, ETTZ listens rather than reaches ON PURPOSE, and calling the absence a gap mistakes doctrine for deficiency. A secondary objection pushes the other way, toward 'missing': that the stabilization machinery I credit ETTZ with (confirming envelopes, join proposals) is generic acknowledgment plumbing, not exploration logic, so no part of the growth-cone computation is really present.

## Verdict survives because
Against 'deliberately different': the owner's actual doctrine gates DISPOSAL and CONSTRUCTION (never erase; owner authorization gates disposal; nothing builds until he rules) — no stated law gates INQUIRY, and the estate already institutionalizes the opposite temperament for questions: the ignorance graph makes open questions first-class, desiderata explicitly 'drive acquisition,' and watch-contracts is glossed as the estate 'noticing a new limb and OFFERING to wire it.' The doctrine-compliant reading of filopodia is available precisely because biological retraction is recycling, not destruction — actin monomers are reused, identity is not erased — so a failed probe collapses to one appended choice-envelope ('probed X, no confirmation, retracted, because Z'), which is the why-ledger working as designed, not noise. And stabilization terminating in a proposal for owner review preserves 'nothing builds until he rules' exactly as watch-contracts already does. So the absence is unlegislated, not doctrinal: verdict stays 'partial,' not 'deliberately different.' Against 'missing': the objection undercounts what confirmation-gated proposal actually is. Biology itself dissociates exploration from stabilization (Bentley: filopodia carry navigation, not propulsion; Varoqueaux 2006 PMID:16982420: formation and validation are separable subsystems), and ETTZ demonstrably possesses the stabilization/validation subsystem (ack-closed loops, proposal gating, maturation levels) while lacking the probe-emission subsystem. Having one of two dissociable subsystems is the definition of 'partial' — with the missing piece nameable, which the verdict requires and the missing_pieces list supplies.

## Missing pieces
- Probe emission before self-announcement: no mechanism extends a speculative, time-boxed contact toward a subject/organ/link that has not published itself — watch-contracts can only reconcile declarations (the filopodium proper; Ziv & Smith 1996).
- A readable gradient: desiderata/ignorance-graph are static wants with no differential signal ('closer/farther'), no per-want probe budget, and no Rosoff-style adaptive re-zeroing against baseline — so nothing can steer, only enumerate.
- Retraction as a first-class outcome: no probe object with a TTL that expires by default into an appended retraction record; ETTZ has no vocabulary for 'tried and unanswered' distinct from 'never tried.'
- A repulsive cue class: no high-sensitivity 'do not connect' veto (Luo 1993 — vetoes at picomolar, far more sensitive than attractants); ETTZ navigation-by-subtraction is absent.
- Self-avoidance / duplicate-edge rejection: nothing prevents a probe from re-probing an existing contact or proposing a redundant or self-referential edge — biology solves this with combinatorial self-identity codes (Lefebvre 2012 PMID:22842903, protocadherin self-avoidance, added by the critique).
- Quorum on stabilization: no valency threshold requiring N distinct confirmations before a probe becomes a join proposal (Lee 2012 PMID:22457515, 1:2:4 stoichiometric quorum — noise rejection built into the connection primitive).

## Adoptable proposal: Filopodial probe envelopes (PROBE/RETRACT verb pair on the watch-contracts keeper)
- biological principle: Filopodial stochastic sampling with selective stabilization: emit cheap, time-boxed, majority-fail probes toward candidates not yet in contact, sourced by a want-gradient; stabilize only probes that receive a confirming handshake (Ziv & Smith 1996 PMID:8755481; Lohmann & Bonhoeffer 2008 PMID:18667153), with a stoichiometric quorum so a single incidental contact cannot trigger construction (Lee 2012 PMID:22457515), and retraction as recycling, not destruction.
- ettz change: Add one envelope subtype and one keeper behavior — no new organ. (1) A `probe` envelope: minted id, mandatory `why` citing the desideratum or ignorance-graph node that motivated it (the gradient source), a target subject/organ beyond current mesh membership or link topology, and a TTL (e.g. 7 days — ETTZ's analog of 9.5 minutes at estate timescale). Emitted by the BINNA watch-contracts keeper, budgeted per-want (a want whose probes keep expiring gets a decaying budget — the adaptive gain-control Rosoff 2004 shows is mandatory). (2) Stabilization rule: a probe answered by >=N distinct confirming envelopes (N>=2, distinct flow refs — the quorum) within TTL is promoted into the EXISTING watch-contracts join/link proposal path, for owner review; it never auto-wires. (3) Expiry rule: an unanswered probe collapses at the nightly fold into one appended choice-envelope ('probed X for want W, no confirmation, retracted, because Z') — retraction as append, feeding the bank so why-NOT-connected becomes learnable. (4) A dedup guard: a probe whose (src,target) pair matches an existing contact or an unexpired prior probe is refused at mint (the protocadherin self-avoidance analog, Lefebvre 2012).
- cost: Build: one schema addition to envelope.schema.json, keeper logic in the BINNA watch-contracts keeper (~emission, quorum check, dedup), one fold-time expiry sweep in memory-nightly, one guard test. No new host, no new organ, no wire changes. Runtime: probe traffic is majority-fail by design, so spine growth — bounded by the per-want budget (e.g. <=3 live probes per desideratum) — plus a few seconds added to the nightly fold for the expiry sweep; expired probes compress to single choice-envelopes so steady-state spine load is roughly one envelope per retired probe.
- risks: Concrete failure modes: (1) Probe storms — without the per-want budget, a large ignorance graph mints unbounded probes; 71k envelopes and a 60s+ cold fold is a known scaling pain, and probe traffic lands exactly there; the budget cap is load-bearing, not optional. (2) Alarm pollution — unanswered probes look like silence, and 'silence is the one thing a heartbeat may never produce'; if probes share telemetry channels with beats, stale-fabric alarms fire on expected failures, training the owner to ignore alarms. Probes must be exempt from silence alarms by type. (3) Gradient degeneracy — desiderata are curated statics, not measured concentrations; without the decaying budget the keeper re-probes the same dead want forever, which is exactly the failure Rosoff's narrow-band result predicts for a detector with no re-zeroing. (4) Where the biology would be WRONG for ETTZ: growth cones probe an anonymous extracellular space where contact is consequence-free; ETTZ probes hit real verb doors on real organs — a probe is a request with side-effect potential, so probes must be restricted to read-only/describe verbs, or a 'probe' that mutates state becomes an unauthorized action wearing an exploratory name. Also, biology's stochasticity earns its keep from massive parallelism (thousands of filopodia); at 12 sources the candidate space is small enough that random emission adds noise without coverage — emission should be want-ranked, not uniform-random, a deliberate departure from the biological mechanism.
- owning session: BINNA keepers (watch-contracts) — it already owns the contract-vs-mesh diff and the join-proposal path, so stabilization lands in existing machinery; memory-nightly owns the expiry sweep; kallimachos/entropy A-003 explicitly does NOT own retraction (probe expiry is an append, not a disposal, so no quarantine or custodian interlock is invoked).
- priority argument: Ranks second, deliberately below the episodic->claims promotion loop (the owner's suspected sharpest gap, where synaptotropic growth/Niell 2004 supplies the rule) — because promotion compounds the value of every envelope already collected, while probing only pays off at the frontier, and today the owner himself is the estate's growth cone, making the gap non-fatal at 12 sources. But it ranks above decay/signaling refinements for two reasons: Bentley's dissociation says the deficit is silent — the estate will never alarm on connections it failed to discover, so no existing telemetry will ever surface this gap — and the estate is explicitly in a growth phase (new hosts by registry drop, ORGANON JOINING.md), which is precisely the developmental window in which biology runs exploration hottest. Cheap to build, doctrine-clean, and it converts two existing passive assets (desiderata, ignorance graph) into actuated ones.

## Doctrine notes
Three explicit touches on owner law. (1) 'Compress and archive, never erase': biological retraction is compatible only because it recycles rather than destroys (actin monomers reused) — the ETTZ import must make retraction an APPENDED choice-envelope, never a removal; if any implementation deletes expired probes instead of folding them, it violates the absolute law and is dead on arrival. Probe expiry deliberately bypasses quarantine/entropy/custodian because nothing is disposed of — this must be stated, or Kalli's dwell-clock machinery gets misapplied to objects that are not files earning death. (2) 'Nothing builds until he rules': a stabilized probe terminates in a join PROPOSAL through the existing watch-contracts path — probes must never auto-wire an organ or link; the owner is the gate, exactly as he gates disposal. The biological analogy actually endorses this: judgment and execution are separated in biology too (Schafer 2012 tag-vs-executor), and ETTZ's doctrine puts the owner in the executor seat. (3) Microglia contrast, stated as doctrine not failure: biology's explorer and its pruner both end in irreversible physical acts; ETTZ diverges on purpose at both ends — probes cannot mutate (read-only verbs) and retraction cannot erase. Finally, one correction inherited from the critique is honored here: the 'relative addressing / self-rescaling gradient' logic must be sourced to Reber 2004 (PMID:15483613) and Cheng & Flanagan 1995 (PMID:7634327), not Drescher 1995 — relevant if the owner later wants probe targeting ranked by RATIO of want-strength rather than absolute counts, which the ephrin ratio result supports and Drescher does not.
