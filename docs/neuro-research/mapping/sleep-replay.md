# sleep-replay
MECHANISM: Sleep replay and systems consolidation: hippocampal SPW-R replay packets delivered into nested SO/spindle/ripple gating windows train the slow neocortical store offline (CLS), embedded in global proportional downscaling whose survivor-ratio yields gist-like generalization; extended by the critique's awake replay, schema-dependent fast consolidation, and prediction-error-gated reconsolidation.
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: memory-nightly (scheduled fold of events/ into durable state) + the face's fold (71k envelopes → claims/procedures state.json renders, fingerprint cache, 60s+ cold fold) + M1 eval harness re-running episodes. The CLS two-store substrate itself: events/ as the fast episodic writer; claims/ trials ledger and procedures/ verified counts as the slow store's incremental weights; flow refs as the ordered replay payload; ignorance graph + desiderata as unused cue sources.
VERDICT: partial

## Argument for
The load-bearing CLS architecture (McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly 1995, PMID:7624455) is genuinely present, not merely nameable. Its computational requirements are: (1) a fast, sparse, one-shot, high-specificity writer — events/ is exactly this: append-only, content-hash-addressed, one envelope per occurrence, pulled from every organ's outbox; (2) a slow store that changes a little per exposure and encodes structure across ensembles — claims/ predicates with trial counts (+confirm/−fail/~anomalous) ARE slowly-accumulated weights, and procedures' worked→verified counters are the procedural twin; (3) the rule that the fast store may never write directly into the slow store (catastrophic-interference avoidance) holds structurally in ETTZ — no single envelope rewrites a claim; claims move only by accumulated trials, and contradiction closes a window rather than overwriting (which is Bouton 2004's extinction-as-competing-record, PMID:15466298: the superseded record survives intact and bi-temporal context decides which is returned — ETTZ's --asof time-travel is precisely that context-gated retrieval). The timescale separation is also right: envelopes are second-scale, claim windows are day/week-scale, matching biology's encode(ms–s) → nightly cycles → cortical independence gradient. memory-nightly even runs at the correct phase — a scheduled offline pass, the low-acetylcholine analog (Diekelmann & Born 2010, PMID:20046194: consolidation requires the offline mode where the fast store's output can dominate). And the fold's 60s+ cold-fold scaling pain is the pathology CLS predicts for a monolithic re-derivation instead of incremental interleaved re-presentation — the analogy is deep enough to diagnose ETTZ's own performance bug.

## Argument against
On the specific test posed, memory-nightly fails: it is an AGGREGATION, not a REPLAY. Replay's defining property is that content is re-presented so the slow store LEARNS from it — replay is causally necessary for consolidation (Girardeau 2009, PMID:19749750) and its product is new abstracted structure (Klinzing 2019, PMID:31451802; Durrant 2011, PMID:21335017: after sleep, subjects recognize NOVEL sequences obeying learned statistics). The fold recomputes state.json renders from claims/procedures that already exist; it changes no claim, adds no trial, mints no predicate. Four claims exist and all were hand-seeded — the hippocampus→neocortex path has literally never fired. The M1 harness does re-run episodes, but for evaluation; its outcomes do not land as trials, so it is replay-shaped with the plasticity disabled. The shape objection is also fatal in detail: biology emits thousands of small, content-bearing, time-compressed packets (SPW-Rs, 50–200 ms, one trajectory each — Buzsáki 2015, PMID:26135716) into coincidence-gated windows (Staresina 2015, PMID:26389842; causally, Maingret 2016, PMID:27182818); ETTZ runs one O(all) batch with no gating, no selection policy, no interleaving discipline, and no decay against which replayed items outcompete unreplayed ones — and without the downscaling half, the survivor-RATIO mechanism that IS the memory (Tononi & Cirelli 2014, PMID:24411729; de Vivo 2017, PMID:28154076) has no ETTZ counterpart at all. The critique adds two more misses: consolidation in biology also runs opportunistically in awake idle moments (Jadhav 2012, PMID:22555434), whereas ETTZ is nightly-cron only; and schema-consistent items consolidate orders of magnitude faster (Tse 2007, PMID:17412951), a conditional fast path nothing in ETTZ exploits.

## Strongest objection
The strongest objection is that the verdict should be MISSING, not partial: the mapping was proposed for memory-nightly + M1 specifically, and neither carries the actual computational logic of replay — one renders cached state, the other evaluates without writing back. Having two stores is not having consolidation, just as having a hippocampus and a cortex in a jar is not having sleep; the essential mechanism (offline re-presentation that trains the slow store) has fired zero times in the estate's life, and a mechanism that has never executed is absent, not partial.

## Verdict survives because
"Missing" would be correct if ETTZ lacked the logic entirely, but CLS is an architectural claim about TWO cooperating requirements — the two-rate, interference-safe store pair AND the offline transfer loop — and ETTZ verifiably implements the first: the store separation, the append-only fast writer, the trial-increment slow updates, the no-direct-overwrite discipline, and the close-window-never-delete contradiction handling are all live and doctrine-enforced (a guard test fails if a delete path appears). Moreover the transfer loop is not entirely at zero: memory-nightly runs at the right phase with the right inputs (all outboxes merged), the M1 harness demonstrates episodes are mechanically re-runnable, and flow refs already package episodes as the ordered, thread-shaped payload a replay pass needs (Fernández-Ruiz 2019, PMID:31197012, shows payload-bearing sequence is what matters). That is substantially more than "no ETTZ mechanism carries this logic" — it is most of the machine with the connecting shaft missing. Partial, with the missing piece named exactly: no re-presentation loop that turns replayed episodes into slow-store updates (candidate claims / trial increments), and no decay for replay to select against.

## Missing pieces
- The hippocampus→neocortex path itself: nothing replays episodic envelopes into claims/ or procedures/ — no mechanism promotes repeated episodic patterns into candidate claims or trial increments (the owner's suspected sharpest gap, confirmed; Klinzing 2019, Durrant 2011 show generalization is replay's product, and ETTZ has never produced it)
- The downscaling half: no salience/rank decay against which replayed items' survival is measured — biology's memory is the RATIO of re-potentiated to uniformly-decayed weights (Tononi & Cirelli 2014, de Vivo 2017); ETTZ's bank balances only go up
- Packet shape: the fold is one O(all) monolithic re-derivation, not bounded, content-bearing, interleaved replay batches — the 60s+ cold fold is the symptom (Buzsáki 2015 compression; McClelland 1995 interleaving requirement)
- Cue-driven selection: the ignorance graph and desiderata do not steer what gets folded — no TMR/query-loop analog (Rasch 2007 PMID:17347444; Rothschild 2017 PMID:27941790: the slow store queries the fast store)
- Awake/idle replay: consolidation is nightly-cron only; biology also consolidates opportunistically in quiet-waking idle moments (Jadhav 2012, Foster & Wilson 2006) — an idle-time background task, not just a batch window
- Gating: no coincidence condition — the fold's timing has no phase relation to mesh quiescence or ingest load (Staresina 2015 nested clock; Diekelmann & Born 2010 offline-mode requirement)
- Schema-dependent fast path: a candidate consistent with an existing claim's predicate schema should commit far cheaper than a cold one (Tse 2007) — no such distinction exists
- M1 write-back: eval-harness re-runs produce no +confirm/−fail trials on the claims and procedures they exercise, so the one existing replay-shaped mechanism is plasticity-free

## Adoptable proposal: RIPPLE-PASS: candidate-claim replay inside memory-nightly
- biological principle: Active systems consolidation via replay: the fast store's episodes are re-presented offline, in bounded content-bearing packets, interleaved across contexts, so the slow store accumulates small updates whose product is new abstracted structure (McClelland 1995; Girardeau 2009; Fernández-Ruiz 2019; Durrant 2011; Klinzing 2019) — with TMR-style cue addressing (Rasch 2007; Rothschild 2017) selecting what is replayed.
- ettz change: Add one replay stage to memory-nightly, upstream of the render fold: each night sample a bounded batch of COMPLETE flow-ref threads from events/ (whole ordered journeys, never synthetic summaries — Fernández-Ruiz 2019's induced-ripple null: shape without payload buys nothing), preferring threads whose subject or mandatory `why` field matches an open ignorance-graph question or a desideratum (the TMR cue key). Mine the batch for recurring predicates that hold across ≥N independent replays spanning ≥2 sources (McClelland's interleaving discipline; BINNA's parent-union is the natural interleaving set), and emit each survivor as an append-only QUESTION envelope proposing a CANDIDATE CLAIM — a checkable predicate seeded with its supporting trial refs, landing on the ignorance graph for owner review. Nothing auto-confirms; promotion to a live claim remains an owner decision envelope (a splice). Secondary wire, nearly free: M1 harness re-runs post +confirm/−fail/~anomalous trial envelopes for the claims and procedures they exercise, giving replay real plasticity.
- cost: Build: a flow-ref batch sampler over events/, a motif/predicate miner with the two thresholds (repeat count, source spread), a question-envelope emitter, and the M1 trial write-back — all within existing envelope/claims vocabulary, no schema change; roughly the complexity of the existing Collector. Runtime: O(selected batch) per night instead of touching all 71k, plus a per-night cap on emitted candidates; strictly cheaper asymptotically than today's cold fold, small constant nightly cost.
- risks: (1) Frontier flooding: a naive miner promotes spurious co-occurrences into candidate claims and buries the ignorance graph's real open questions under machine-generated noise — the graph's signal-to-noise, currently the owner's frontier view, collapses; the source-spread threshold and hard nightly emission cap are load-bearing, not optional. (2) Self-replay feedback: candidate-claim and trial envelopes are themselves spine events; if the next night's sampler ingests replay-generated envelopes, motifs self-amplify into runaway duplicate candidates — the sampler must exclude envelopes whose src is the replay pass itself. (3) Induced-ripple failure mode: if the miner is pointed at pre-aggregated summaries or fold caches instead of raw ordered flow threads, it emits confident candidates with no evidentiary payload — biology's exact null result. (4) Cue-bias narrowing: replaying only ignorance-graph-matched threads means the estate never abstracts from domains it has no questions about yet; a fraction of each batch must be uncued random sampling (biology's decaying recency bias, Wilson & McNaughton 1994).
- owning session: memory-nightly (the consolidation organ already scheduled at the right phase, already reading all outboxes; candidate envelopes land in the spine's ignorance graph; M1 eval harness owns only the trial write-back wire)
- priority argument: Ranks first among this project's proposals: it targets the gap the owner himself flagged as sharpest (nothing promotes episodes to claims), the biological warrant is the strongest in the sweep (replay is causally NECESSARY — Girardeau 2009 — and generalization is its measured product — Durrant 2011), the substrate is unusually ready (flow refs are already the payload, claims trials are already the weight format, the ignorance graph is already the cue source and the review inbox), and it is doctrine-clean by construction: pure append-only question envelopes, owner-gated promotion, nothing erased. The companion downscaling/salience-decay proposal is deliberately left to the microglia/entropy analysis, where its doctrine questions belong.

## Doctrine notes
Three explicit touches on owner law. (1) Downscaling vs never-erase: biology's consolidation is embedded in global synaptic shrinkage and is lossy BY DESIGN — gist survives, episode-specific detail dies (Klinzing 2019). ETTZ must not import the lossy half literally: under "compress and archive, never erase," decay may only be RANK decay (a salience score falling), never record removal; the de Vivo 2017 sparing of the strong ~20% maps to quarantine-grace and the CUSTODIAN A-002 snapshot interlock as protection predicates. Abstraction in ETTZ is therefore additive (new claims appended beside intact episodes), not substitutive — a deliberate, doctrine-driven divergence from biology, correctly so. (2) Autonomy of disposal: biology's downscaling judges no item individually and asks no one; ETTZ's law is that owner authorization gates disposal — so any replay/decay mechanism may propose and rank but never dispose. The proposal honors this: it emits question envelopes only, and promotion is an owner decision envelope (a splice), consistent with append-only repair doctrine. (3) A convergence worth naming: close-the-window-never-delete on contradiction is not a compromise with biology but a match to it — extinction does not delete the old record; it writes a competing, context-gated new one, with the old record recoverable (Bouton 2004). ETTZ's bi-temporal closed windows with --asof retrieval already implement biology's actual mechanism more faithfully than a delete would; a superseded claim as "the record of how understanding changed" is renewal/spontaneous recovery made queryable. Nothing here builds until the owner rules; this mapping and proposal are for his review.
