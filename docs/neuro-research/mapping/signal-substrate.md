# signal-substrate
MECHANISM: Neural signaling substrate: spike trains (discrete addressed events), tonic/pacemaker firing where deviation-from-baseline is the signal, efferent command with retrograde acknowledgment, deafferentation/supply-silence detection, and neuromodulatory broadcast gain (dopamine/ACh) that globally reweights how strongly events are learned from.
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: BEATS + mesh jitter/missed/drift analysis (tonic signaling); n8n trigger fabric with verb doors and confirming envelopes (efferent command + acknowledgment); stale-fabric alarms (deafferentation detection); envelopes in the spine (the event substrate, but as flight recorder, not spike code); flow refs (axonal pathway tracing / retrograde receipt thread). For neuromodulation: NONE — no global state modulates how strongly any envelope is weighted; all 71,225 entries are gain-flat forever.
VERDICT: partial

## Argument for
Three of the four sub-mappings hold at the level of computational logic, not just metaphor. (1) BEATS = tonic firing is the strongest single analogy in the whole project: biology genuinely implements 'silence is the failure signal' as executable machinery — axon survival is a lease renewed by continuous somatic delivery of labile NMNAT2 (half-life of hours); cessation of delivery shifts NMN/NAD+, activates SARM1, and the distal segment self-destructs locally (Coleman & Hoke 2020, PMID:32152523). That is precisely a dead-man's switch with a metabolite-ratio threshold and an hours-scale TTL — the same logic as a 15s heartbeat with jitter/missed/drift analysis and stale-fabric alarms: absence is decoded against an expected tonic baseline, and the alarm executes locally at the detector, not centrally. (2) Trigger fabric = efferent-with-ack also holds: the confirming envelope that closes each loop is the computational twin of the retrograde channel — dynein-carried signaling endosomes returning trophic receipts to the nucleus (Maday et al. 2014, PMID:25374356) — and at the plasticity layer the back-propagating action potential is literally biology's acknowledgment signal delivered to just-active inputs (Markram et al. 1997, PMID:8985014; Bi & Poo 1998, PMID:9852584). Command, execution, receipt, alarm-on-missing-receipt: the loop structure is isomorphic. (3) Flow refs as pathway tracing holds modestly: an opaque id threading one journey across organs is a functional analog of tracing one axonal pathway, and its append-only splice repairs (question envelope → decision envelope) have no biological counterpart but no biological contradiction either. (4) Envelopes-as-spikes holds only at the thinnest level: discrete, timestamped, source-addressed, why-mandatory events do form the estate's only afferent traffic, so 'the spike train' is fair as a name for the substrate role.

## Argument against
Two disanalogies are structural, and one is a genuine absence. FIRST, envelopes are not spikes. A spike is all-or-none, near-zero-content, meaningful mainly in aggregate rate, timing, and population patterns; an envelope is an individually meaningful rich record with a mandatory why and a content-hash identity. The brain keeps no global append-only log of its spikes — spikes are consumed at write time into weight changes (weight = AMPA receptor count; Malinow & Malenka 2002, PMID:12052905) and the raw train is gone forever. The spine is therefore a flight recorder, not a nervous system, and the difference determines what each can compute: ETTZ gets perfect episodic recall, time-travel (--asof), and full provenance — capabilities no brain has — but gets NO free pattern-completion (partial cue → whole record requires CA3-style recurrent machinery, which is a circuit computation, not a log property; Nakazawa et al. 2002, PMID:12040087), no automatic generalization, and no compact index-versus-content separation (Teyler & Rudy 2007, PMID:17696170). Everything the brain computes at write time, ETTZ defers to fold time — which is exactly why the cold fold costs 60s+ and why the owner's sharpest gap (no automatic episodic→semantic promotion) exists: a flight recorder records; it does not abstract. SECOND, ETTZ's tonic and phasic channels are the same channel: beats are just envelopes on a schedule, whereas biology physically separates tonic pacemaker activity from phasic signaling. This mostly doesn't matter computationally, but it means ETTZ has no analog of tonic RATE as a carrier — the mesh reads presence/absence and jitter, and nothing anywhere in the estate reads envelope rate as a signal in its own right. THIRD, and this is the genuine absence: neuromodulation. Dopamine neurons broadcast a reward-prediction-error signal — firing to unexpected outcomes, going silent to omitted expected ones — that globally scales what gets durably written (Schultz, Dayan & Montague 1997, PMID:9054347), and locus-coeruleus input is what converts an ordinary event into a persistent memory (Takeuchi et al. 2016, PMID:27602521). ETTZ has no counterpart at all: no alert level, no attention state, no salience field, no gain term. Every envelope is weighted identically at fold time, forever. The critique's sharpest point applies verbatim: a consolidation gate with no upstream salience input is a gate with no input — and ETTZ's planned promotion machinery will inherit exactly that defect.

## Strongest objection
The objection to 'partial' from the flight-recorder side: since envelopes are categorically not spikes and the brain has no append-only log, the entire mapping is a naming exercise — the honest verdict should be 'deliberately different' (the log follows owner law, not neural design), with the beats/fabric matches dismissed as generic distributed-systems patterns (heartbeats and ack loops predate any neuroscience analogy and were surely built as engineering, not biology).

## Verdict survives because
The objection correctly kills 'have it' but overshoots 'deliberately different'. The project's rule is to test at the level of computational logic — signal, tag, threshold, timescale — and by that test the tonic-baseline-with-deviation-decoding logic (expected cadence, statistical thresholds on jitter/miss, locally executing alarm on supply silence) and the command→receipt→alarm-on-missing-receipt loop are genuinely present in ETTZ and genuinely present in biology (NMNAT2/SARM1, retrograde endosomes, bAP acknowledgment), with matching thresholds-on-absence and matching local execution. Convergent evolution is still a valid mapping: the verdict does not require that the owner copied biology, only that the logic is shared. So the mechanism is not 'missing' and not merely renamed. But 'deliberately different' as the overall verdict would also be wrong, because it explains only the envelope/log half (which IS doctrine: compress-and-archive-never-erase forces a flight recorder) and cannot explain the neuromodulation absence — no owner doctrine mandates that every event be gain-flat forever; that is an unexamined gap, not a ruling. A verdict must be assigned to the mapping as tested, and a mapping with two sub-parts present, one doctrine-divergent, and one absent with no doctrine covering it is the definition of 'partial' with named missing pieces. The verdict stands.

## Missing pieces
- Neuromodulatory gain / salience scoring: no global state (alert level, owner attention, novelty) modulates how strongly an envelope is weighted at fold or promotion time — the write-priority scorer biology implements as dopaminergic prediction-error broadcast (Schultz 1997, PMID:9054347; Takeuchi 2016, PMID:27602521) has no ETTZ counterpart, and any future episodic-to-semantic promotion gate will be a gate with no upstream input without it.
- Prediction-error gating wired to reads: claims have trials (+confirm/-fail/~anomalous) but nothing in the signal substrate flags 'this envelope contradicts an open claim' at ingestion — biology opens a record for update only on expectation mismatch, not on mere retrieval (Sevenster et al. 2013, PMID:23413355; Nader et al. 2000, PMID:10963596); ETTZ's contradiction detection happens only if someone runs the predicate.
- Rate-adaptive thresholds / divisive normalization: beat-alarm and any 'notable event' thresholds are fixed, not scaled by recent neighborhood activity — a busy organ and a quiet organ are judged by the same bar, where biology normalizes divisively on fast timescales (Carandini & Heeger 2012, PMID:22108672) and slides plasticity thresholds on slow ones (Bienenstock, Cooper & Munro 1982, PMID:7054394).
- Seconds-scale eligibility binding: nothing marks 'the envelopes of the last several seconds belong to one creditable episode' — flow refs are identity threads, not decaying eligibility traces; biology assigns credit across multi-second windows, order-symmetric, in one trial (Bittner et al. 2017, PMID:28883072).
- Aggregate-rate reading: outside beats, no ETTZ consumer treats envelope RATE from a source as a signal in its own right; the estate reads records individually, never as a population code.

## Adoptable proposal: Salience annotation layer (neuromodulatory gain for the fold)
- biological principle: Neuromodulatory write-priority: a broadcast prediction-error/novelty signal determines which events are learned from strongly, so consolidation is gain-weighted rather than uniform (Schultz, Dayan & Montague 1997, PMID:9054347; Takeuchi et al. 2016, PMID:27602521; gated by mismatch, not by mere occurrence — Sevenster et al. 2013, PMID:23413355).
- ettz change: A fold-time derived salience score per envelope — computed, never stored in the envelope (no schema change, no mutation): +salience when the event contradicts or anomalously matches an open claim's predicate (prediction error), +salience when it coincides with abnormal beat statistics from its source (context of disturbance), +salience when a later owner decision/chat envelope refs it (attention). memory-nightly consumes the score for ordering only: high-salience events fold first, seed the exact-fingerprint cache, and populate a ranked promote-candidates queue rendered on the ignorance graph for owner review. Retention, disposal, and claim windows remain salience-blind by construction.
- cost: Build: one scoring module inside memory-nightly's fold plus a claims-predicate cross-check pass and a beat-stats join; a config file of weights; guard tests asserting salience can never reach entropy/disposal code paths. Runtime: additive scan over new envelopes per nightly run (seconds against the existing 60s+ cold fold; the claim cross-check is bounded by 4 claims today and must be re-costed as claims grow).
- risks: (1) Forgetting-by-neglect: if low-salience events sink in every read surface, salience becomes a de facto erasure layer violating the spirit of never-erase — mitigation is structural: salience may order and cache, never filter or dispose, enforced by a guard test like the claims no-delete guard. (2) Self-excitation: salient events spawn discussion envelopes that ref them, raising their salience further — a positive-feedback loop biology caps with normalization (Turrigiano 1998, PMID:9495341; Carandini & Heeger 2012, PMID:22108672); the score must be normalized per-source per-window or the loudest incident dominates every fold. (3) Miscalibrated prediction error: with only 4 seeded claims, 'contradicts a claim' fires almost never and 'anomalous' heuristics fire constantly, flooding the promote queue with routine drift and training the owner to ignore it — the scorer should launch in shadow mode (log scores, actuate nothing) until its precision is measured against owner verdicts. (4) Where the biological principle is WRONG for ETTZ: dopamine gates what gets STORED AT ALL — biology discards the unweighted remainder; ETTZ must invert this to gating only what gets attended first, because the estate's whole value proposition is that the unweighted remainder is still there.
- owning session: memory-nightly (fold-time scoring and consumption), reading beat statistics from the BINNA mesh and predicates from the spine claims layer; promote-candidate rendering lands on the ignorance graph / memory face.
- priority argument: Ranks high — arguably first among signaling proposals — because it is the enabling dependency for the owner's own stated sharpest gap: any automatic episodic-to-semantic promotion mechanism (the Frey-Morris two-factor commit design from the Hebbian sweep) needs a salience input to decide which repeated patterns deserve a consolidation pulse, and today nothing computes one. It is also cheap (derived state only, no envelope schema change, no new organ) and doctrine-safe by construction (ordering, never retention). It ranks below nothing in this mapping; its only defensible deferral is waiting for more than 4 seeded claims, which shadow mode addresses.

## Doctrine notes
Two explicit touchpoints. (1) The envelope/spike disanalogy IS owner law, stated as doctrine and not failure: compress-and-archive-never-erase forces the spine to be a flight recorder rather than a nervous system. Biology consumes its spike train into weights and discards it; ETTZ keeps the train and defers abstraction to the fold. This trades away free pattern-completion and cheap generalization for perfect recall, provenance, and time-travel — a purchase the owner made on purpose, and every proposal here must respect it: abstraction layers (salience, promotion, indexes) may only be built AS DERIVED STATE OVER the immutable train, never as its replacement. (2) The salience proposal brushes the never-erase law at its most dangerous point: a gain signal that decides what gets read is one config change away from deciding what gets kept. Biology's neuromodulator gates storage itself — ETTZ must invert the principle so gain gates attention order only, and the inversion must be enforced by guard test (mirroring the claims-class no-delete guard), with salience provably unreachable from kallimachos/entropy A-003 disposal paths and from quarantine decisions, which remain gated solely by dwell clocks, custodian snapshot verification (A-002), and owner authorization. Research-only status honored: this is a proposal for owner review; nothing builds until he rules.
