# neurogenesis-joining
MECHANISM: Neurogenesis with staged integration: niche-restricted birth, migration along fixed streams, mandatory staged handshake (tonic listening -> input synapses -> output), use-it-or-lose-it survival checkpoints, transient high-plasticity critical period, over-provision-then-select, and neurogenesis-induced forgetting.
ETTZ EQUIVALENT: ORGANON JOINING.md + the Collector's one-registry-drop admission (the niche: units minted only at a registered factory); ORGANON conformance levels participant->organ->system (staged maturation); BEATS mesh membership (tonic-signal substrate a newcomer can passively read); trigger-fabric confirming envelopes (the round-trip-verification substrate a graduation gate could use, per Toni 2008's output-synapse check); watch-contracts diffing published contracts vs mesh membership and PROPOSING joins/leaves (a weak, structural selection analog); flow refs as the fixed-trunk routing analog of stream migration.
VERDICT: partial

## Argument for
The admission-control half of the analogy is genuinely computational, not decorative. Biology mints new units only at two registered niches where quiescence is the default and activation the exception (Doetsch 1999 PMID:10380923; Ming & Song 2011 PMID:21609825); ETTZ mints new sources only through ORGANON JOINING.md and a single registry drop to the pull-only Collector — same logic: no ad-hoc birth in the field, admission gated at a named point, identity specified at origin (as in SVZ neuroblasts, whose subtype is set at source, not destination — Lois & Alvarez-Buylla 1994 PMID:8178174). ORGANON's participant->organ->system levels are a real staging pipeline, structurally parallel to stem cell -> neuroblast -> immature -> mature neuron. The tonic-GABA phase (Ge 2006 PMID:16341203) — a synapse-less newcomer first reading aggregate network traffic — has a live ETTZ substrate: a joining organ can (and via the pull-only Collector effectively does) emit/read heartbeats and outbox envelopes before any verb door is exercised; listening precedes acting in practice. Toni 2008 (PMID:18622400) — a join is closed only when the new node both receives and delivers on live routes — is exactly the trigger fabric's own law that a confirming envelope closes each loop. And the Sorrells/Boldrini controversy (PMIDs 29513649/29625071) even normalizes ETTZ's demography: a mature system that throttles unit admission to near zero while keeping the niche machinery (watch-contracts still proposing joins) is biologically ordinary, not stagnant.

## Argument against
Every selective stage of the pipeline is absent, and selection is the point of the biological design. (1) No probation window: most newborn neurons die by default at days 1-4 (Sierra 2010 PMID:20887954; Bax executor, Sun 2004 PMID:15590937), and survivors must show cell-autonomous evidence of correlated input inside weeks 1-3 (Tashiro 2006 PMID:16906136). ETTZ has no window in which a newly joined source must demonstrate use; a registered organ that never carries a flow ref persists indefinitely. Watch-contracts proposes leaves on contract-vs-mesh DIFFS — a structural check — not on absence of traffic — a functional one. BEATS cannot substitute: an organ can heartbeat perfectly forever while carrying zero substantive traffic; beats measure aliveness, not use. (2) No critical period: Ge 2007 (PMID:17521569) gives survivors a transient NR2B-carried elevated learning rate and, implicitly, elevated attention; ETTZ gives a new joiner no extra telemetry, no ignorance-graph entry, no heightened scrutiny — it is treated as tenured at birth. (3) No enforced stage ORDER: Ge 2006 shows skipping tonic-listening-first wrecks wiring; nothing in ORGANON forces listen-only before verb doors open, and the brief states no usage-evidence criteria for level promotion. (4) No over-provisioning: biology's statistics (mint thousands cheap, keep the correlated few) simply do not apply to a handful of expensive, owner-hand-built organs — on this axis the analogy is superficial. (5) Neurogenesis-induced forgetting (Akers 2014 PMID:24812394; growth remodels and degrades stored memory) has, correctly, no ETTZ analog at all.

## Strongest objection
Against 'partial' one can argue the whole mapping should be 'deliberately different': ETTZ's admission is already gated by the strongest possible selector — the owner hand-builds and vets each organ BEFORE birth — so biology's post-hoc statistical selection (over-provision, then let uncorrelated units die) is solving a problem ETTZ does not have; and the destructive machinery (Bax apoptosis, microglial phagocytosis, growth-induced forgetting) is forbidden by the never-erase law, so the 'missing' checkpoint is missing on purpose.

## Verdict survives because
The objection conflates two separable things: the deletion EXECUTOR (genuinely forbidden — that part is doctrine, and I record it as such in doctrine_notes) and the use-evidence SIGNAL (not forbidden by anything, and genuinely absent). Tashiro 2006's computational content is not 'kill the unused' but 'retention requires evidence of correlated use inside a fixed window' — a detection-and-decision problem whose ETTZ-lawful terminal action already exists: quarantine-with-owner-authorization, precisely the interlock structure of kallimachos entropy (tagged candidate -> grace verification -> owner-gated disposal; the phosphatidylserine eat-me tag maps to the .why.md manifest). Owner pre-vetting does not close the gap either: vetting happens before traffic exists, and the estate today has NO mechanism that can ever notice 'this limb registered, beats, and has carried nothing for 60 days' — that is a real blind spot, not a doctrine choice; the owner's own design-question list asks exactly this ('does an organ that never carries traffic get proposed for removal?') and the honest answer from the brief is no. So the logic is partly present (niche, staging, round-trip substrate) and specific pieces are nameably missing: 'partial' stands, with the destructive stages carved out as deliberate.

## Missing pieces
- No probation window: no fixed post-join window in which a new source must show evidence of use (Tashiro 2006's weeks-1-3 input-gated checkpoint has no ETTZ counterpart at any timescale).
- No functional survival check: watch-contracts proposes leaves from contract-vs-mesh structural diffs, never from absence of traffic; heartbeats prove aliveness, not use — a silent-but-beating limb is undetectable as unused.
- No critical period: a new organ gets no transient elevated telemetry/scrutiny and no ignorance-graph entry ('is X carrying real traffic yet?') during its first weeks (Ge 2007 analog absent).
- No enforced stage order: nothing forces a joiner through listen-only (beats + outbox read by Collector) before its verb doors open to the trigger fabric (Ge 2006's mandatory tonic-first sequence is unpoliced).
- No evidence-based graduation criteria: ORGANON participant->organ->system promotion is not stated to require a verified live round-trip (confirming envelope on a real route) as its gate, though the trigger fabric already provides the substrate (Toni 2008 analog specced nowhere).
- No duty-cycle declaration in contracts, which any use-gated check would need to avoid falsely flagging legitimately rare-fire organs.

## Adoptable proposal: Probation window for new joiners (use-gated graduation, quarantine-not-death)
- biological principle: Input-gated survival checkpoint inside a fixed post-birth window (Tashiro 2006 PMID:16906136), with mandatory listen-first staging (Ge 2006 PMID:16341203), transient elevated scrutiny of the newest cohort (Ge 2007 PMID:17521569), and graduation only on verified bidirectional traffic (Toni 2008 PMID:18622400) — retention requires evidence of correlated use, but the executor is substituted per doctrine: quarantine and owner verdict replace Bax/microglial destruction (Sierra 2010 PMID:20887954).
- ettz change: When the Collector registers a new source (the registry drop), a birth envelope stamps it PROBATIONARY with a 30-day dwell clock reusing kallimachos entropy's existing clock pattern. Three effects, all envelope-level, no schema break: (1) an open question is minted on the ignorance graph — 'is <source> carrying real traffic?' — giving the critical-period elevated visibility; (2) ORGANON promotion participant->organ requires the graduation evidence: at least one trigger-fabric loop CLOSED by a confirming envelope on a live route, where the confirming reference comes from ANOTHER organ's flow ref (coincidence of own output and others' uptake — the NMDA-style correlated-use test, not raw self-emitted volume); verb doors stay closed until the listen-only phase has produced clean beats + collected outbox envelopes (enforced stage order); (3) at clock expiry with no graduation evidence, watch-contracts emits a proposed-leave QUESTION envelope with a .why.md manifest for owner review — the registration is quarantined pending his verdict, never deleted, and every envelope the source ever emitted stays in the spine forever. Contracts gain one optional field, expected duty cycle, so rare-fire organs declare their rhythm and get a matching clock.
- cost: Build: small — one dwell-clock table keyed by source-id (pattern copied from entropy A-003), one nightly check folded into watch-contracts' existing diff pass, three new envelope kinds (probation-start, graduation, proposed-leave), one optional contract field. No new session, no schema migration of existing stores. Runtime: negligible — a per-source counter over spine envelopes the Collector already gathers, evaluated once nightly.
- risks: (1) False flags on low-duty-cycle organs: a quarterly verifier legitimately carries no traffic in 30 days and gets proposed-for-leave repeatedly, training the owner to rubber-stamp dismissals of the alarm — mitigated but not eliminated by the duty-cycle contract field (organs will get it wrong at first). (2) Goodharting the gate: an organ (or its author) survives probation by emitting chatty self-referential no-op envelopes; the coincidence requirement (confirmation must arrive via another organ's flow ref) raises the bar but a colluding pair of new organs could still cross-confirm — the graduation check must therefore weight confirmations from TENURED organs only. (3) Spine pollution: probation machinery itself adds envelope volume to a fold that already takes 60s+ cold — bounded, since joins are rare. (4) Scope creep hazard: the same clock pattern applied beyond NEW joiners — to tenured organs or to procedures — would directly violate the no-clock doctrine ('a procedure never decays from disuse'); the probationary/tenured distinction must be hard-coded, biology's use-gate being explicitly WRONG for ETTZ's tenured items.
- owning session: BINNA keepers / watch-contracts (it already diffs contracts against mesh membership and owns propose-join/propose-leave), reusing the kallimachos entropy A-003 dwell-clock and .why.md manifest pattern; graduation evidence is read from the spine via the Collector's existing merged view.
- priority argument: Mid-high, but deliberately below the episodic->semantic promotion gap the owner already suspects is sharpest — Akers 2014 shows growth and abstraction-forgetting are one mechanism in biology, so the promotion machinery is the bigger prize and this proposal should not jump the queue. It ranks above signaling refinements because it closes a genuine blind spot (nothing today can notice a registered, beating, traffic-less limb — a functional-liveness check where only structural checks exist) at very low cost by reusing two proven patterns (entropy clocks, watch-contracts proposals). It is also naturally rate-limited: joins are rare in a mature estate (the Sorrells 2018 PMID:29513649 point), so even a flawed first version bothers the owner at most a few times a year.

## Doctrine notes
Three points where this mapping touches owner law, all handled as doctrine, not failure. (1) Compress-and-archive-never-erase vs microglial clearance: biology's survival checkpoint terminates in Bax-dependent apoptosis and apoptosis-coupled phagocytosis (Sun 2004; Sierra 2010) — a delete verb ETTZ forbids absolutely. The proposal imports only the detection-and-decision logic; the executor is substituted with quarantine-of-registration plus owner-gated disposal, mirroring the entropy/custodian interlock. A proposed-leave is a question envelope; only the owner closes it. (2) Neurogenesis-induced forgetting is DELIBERATELY DIFFERENT and must stay so: Akers 2014 shows adding units actively remodels and degrades stored memory; ETTZ's append-only core means a new source's arrival must NEVER trigger recompaction that alters existing claims, procedures, or episodic records. The only lawful ETTZ analog of growth-coupled forgetting is window-closing on superseded claims (the Bouton 2004 extinction-as-competing-record pattern from the critique — a context-scoped superseding write that shadows, never deletes, the old record), and that belongs to the separate promotion-mechanism proposal, not to joining. (3) The no-clock law for procedures ('never decays from disuse, only from failing when tried') is the inverse of the biological use-gate; the probation clock is lawful only because it applies exclusively to PROBATIONARY new joiners, never to tenured items — biology itself distinguishes the plastic newest cohort from annealed tenured units (Ge 2007), so the tenure boundary is biologically principled, not a compromise.
