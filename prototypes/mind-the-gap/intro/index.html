01 — The gap

Two sides of one world that cannot see each other.

Artists can't see who is collecting. Collectors can't see who is emerging. Galleries take on artists through introductions, not applications, and most sales are never disclosed.

Structural, not personal

04 — The collector layer

Collectors are not bound by borders.

A collector in Tel Aviv can buy from New York or Lagos. No title registry, opaque prices, authentication risk: the same problems in every market. So this layer is built once, for the whole world.

05 — The bridge

The index is the bridge.

Artists see who is collecting. Collectors see who is emerging. Institutions post calls where both sides can find them. A gap does not close by itself. Something has to span it.
