# GoFundMe campaign: draft to paste

A draft for the campaign the support page links to. Only the organiser can
create it on GoFundMe, because it needs their identity and bank details. The
numbers match `project/build/content.json`; if a goal changes there, change it
here too.

## Before you start

- **Who receives the money.** GoFundMe pays out only to a bank account in one
  of the countries it supports (about 20, including the US, Canada, the UK,
  Australia, Mexico and much of the EU). Israel was not on that list when this
  was written. If you are based in Israel, the campaign needs an organiser or
  beneficiary with a bank account in a supported country, for example a
  partner in New York.
- **What it is.** A donation, not an investment. Donors get no share and no
  repayment, and because Mind the Gap is not yet a company or a registered
  charity, a donation is not tax-deductible. The support page says so.
- **Fees.** No platform fee. A payment-processing fee comes off each donation
  (2.9% + $0.30 in the US). Donors are asked for an optional tip to GoFundMe,
  which does not come out of the campaign.
- **If the goal is missed.** You keep what is raised. The support page promises
  it is spent in the order shown there: the re-check first.

## Title

GoFundMe allows 60 characters.

**Mind the Gap: Help Check Every Open Call for Artists** (52)

Alternatives:
- Fund a Free, Fact-Checked Index for Artists (43)
- Help Build a Free Index of Open Calls for Artists (49)

## Goal

**$63,000.** Three months of an editor-in-chief and two researchers. The full
first phase, $202,000, is mentioned in the story as the stretch.

## Category

The arts or creative category.

## Cover image

A screenshot of the atlas or the intro's title scene, at 16:9.

## Story

> Open calls for artists are scattered across hundreds of institution
> websites, newsletters and inboxes. Many never say plainly whether you are
> allowed to apply. In our own index, about one commission in four takes no
> applications at all, which is exactly what an artist needs to know before
> spending a week on a proposal.
>
> Mind the Gap puts it in one place: public commissions, residencies, and the
> museums, galleries and biennials behind them, in six regional editions and
> two country editions, Israel in Hebrew and the United States. Every listing
> answers the question that matters first, can you apply, and links to the
> organiser's own page. Artists will never pay to find a call.
>
> It already works as a prototype, with 199 listings and 139 institutions in
> 46 countries. See it here: [link to the site]
>
> Before anyone relies on it, every listing has to be checked by a person at
> its source. Today only 10 of the 199 are rated high confidence. That is what
> this campaign pays for.
>
> **$63,000 pays for three months of an editor and two researchers, who will:**
>
> - open the source page of every listing and institution, and confirm or
>   correct it
> - add the deadline wherever the organiser publishes one, and retire calls
>   that have closed
> - write and publish the standards: what counts as a source, how confidence
>   is rated, and how fast a reported mistake is fixed
>
> If we raise more, it goes to the rest of the first phase, $202,000 in all:
> the database where every fact keeps its source and the date it was checked,
> the company and its policies, and clearing the name.
>
> We will post an update at each step: what was checked, what changed and what
> it cost.
>
> A donation is a gift, not an investment, and it is not tax-deductible.

## Once it is live

1. Copy the campaign's link.
2. Put it in `project/build/content.json` as `links.gofundme`, or send it over.
3. Rebuild (`node project/build/build.cjs`). The support page's button then
   reads "Donate on GoFundMe", a share box appears, and the top bar gets a
   Donate button.
