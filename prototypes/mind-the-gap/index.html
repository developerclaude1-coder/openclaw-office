RESOLUTION 01 / INSTITUTION → PEOPLE 10¹ m

Artists & collectors, on the same map.

The gap is usually structural: artists don't see who's collecting, and collectors don't see who's emerging until a gallery decides they should. Mind the Gap indexes both sides and shows the shortest honest path between them.

For artists

Be discoverable on your own terms.

  • A living portfolio that syncs to shows, prices, and provenance.
  • See who saved, followed, or acquired — with consent, not scraping.
  • Route directly to open commissions and residencies that fit your practice.
  • Keep your own contacts; galleries amplify, they don't gatekeep.
MIND THE GAP

For collectors

Collect with context, not FOMO.

  • Follow practices, not just names — track an artist from studio to survey.
  • Transparent provenance and edition data on every work.
  • Discover through residencies and commissions before the waitlist forms.
  • Build a collection thesis, then let the index surface what fits it.
