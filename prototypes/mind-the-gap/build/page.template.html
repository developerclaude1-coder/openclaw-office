      About this data. Every organisation, residency and commission listed here is real and was researched and fact-checked against primary sources, most recently in __ASOF__. Map coordinates were verified independently by point-in-polygon testing against national boundary data. Even so: programmes change, funding changes, and application windows move every year. Nothing here is an application portal — always confirm current terms and dates with the organisation directly using the source link on each card. Entries marked ◍ medium or ◍ low carry details we could not fully confirm. Listings added or re-checked in the latest pass were read from the organisers' own pages as quoted by a search engine, without opening the pages themselves, so none of them is rated above medium.
